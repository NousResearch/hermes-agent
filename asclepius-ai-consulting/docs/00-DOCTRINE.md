# 00 — Doctrine

## Mission

Help owner-operated small businesses safely adopt AI where it saves real time, reduces expensive manual work, and improves customer responsiveness, without asking the owner to become technical. ASCLEPIUS is a consulting practice, not a software platform: Richie diagnoses, prescribes, implements, and trains.

## Positioning statement

**The AI Prescription:** a doctor-style diagnostic-then-treatment model for small business owners who feel behind on AI and need a trusted operator to identify practical, low-risk workflows.

## Ideal client profile

Owner-operated businesses with 3–50 employees, especially service-based or light-product operations where the owner has budget authority and feels the pain of messy admin. Examples:

- 8–25 person HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping, or home-services companies.
- Clinics, dental offices, med spas, physical therapy practices, and wellness offices.
- Small law firms, accounting firms, agencies, and professional services shops.
- Local retailers or distributors with repetitive customer questions, ordering, and follow-up.

## The pricing ladder and rationale

### 1. The Diagnostic — $999

The Diagnostic is priced high enough to attract serious owners and low enough to be a fast yes when the pain is visible. It includes a structured call and a written AI Prescription. The margin is secondary: this tier creates trust, exposes implementation work, and gives Richie permission to make a specific Build offer.

### 2. The Build — $2,500–$10,000+

Build pricing is fixed-fee by prescription line item. The range allows small setup projects such as AI-assisted intake triage at the low end and multi-workflow integrations, staff training, and SOP rollout at the high end. Fixed fees protect the client from hourly uncertainty and reward Richie for speed.

### 3. The Retainer — $500–$2,000/month

The Retainer turns one-time implementation into compounding support. It covers quarterly re-diagnosis, tool evaluation, workflow tuning, new-hire onboarding, and light support. The goal is to convert 30–50% of Build clients within 90 days.

### 4. Office Hours / Overflow — $150–$300/hour

Office Hours catch clients who need expert help but do not want a retainer. This is not the main revenue engine; it is a paid safety valve that prevents unpaid favors and keeps relationships alive.

## What we will never do

- We will not turn a Diagnostic call into free implementation or live tool shopping.
- We will not work for free “to build the portfolio”; if a case study is useful, discount intentionally and document the exchange.
- We will not automate human judgment calls that require client context, ethics, legal review, or owner approval.
- We will not prescribe tools without verifying current pricing, privacy posture, and client fit.
- We will not accept open-ended “make us AI-powered” scopes; every Build has named deliverables and boundaries.
