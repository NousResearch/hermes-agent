# Worked example — 12-person HVAC company

## Client

- Business: North Ridge HVAC
- Owner: Fictional example
- Date: August 2026
- Diagnostic focus: lead response, dispatch admin, customer communication, review generation

## What we found

| Bottleneck | Plain-language symptom | Business cost |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Slow lead follow-up | Web inquiries and voicemail callbacks wait until the office manager has a break. | Lost booked jobs and owner anxiety. |
| 2. Repetitive customer questions | Staff retype answers about service windows, maintenance plans, and financing. | 5–8 staff hours/week. |
| 3. Dispatch notes are messy | Tech notes are inconsistent, making follow-up and invoicing slower. | Delayed invoices and callbacks. |
| 4. Reviews are requested inconsistently | Happy customers are not asked at the right time. | Missed local SEO and trust signals. |

## What we prescribe

| Bottleneck | Prescription | Why this fit |
|---|---|---|
| Slow lead follow-up | AI-assisted lead intake form plus scripted first-response email/SMS templates in the existing CRM. | Faster response without replacing human scheduling. |
| Repetitive questions | FAQ knowledge base and approved response snippets for office staff. | Reduces typing while keeping staff in control. |
| Dispatch notes | Mobile note template with AI cleanup into invoice-ready summaries. | Improves clarity without changing field workflow. |
| Reviews | Post-job review request sequence triggered after invoice/payment status. | Captures goodwill at the right moment. |

## Estimated impact

| Prescription | Estimated savings | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Lead response workflow | 2–4 additional booked jobs/month if current missed-lead pattern holds. | Medium |
| FAQ response library | 5 staff hours/week. | High |
| Dispatch note cleanup | 3 admin hours/week and faster invoicing. | High |
| Review sequence | 6–12 new reviews/quarter. | Medium |

## Suggested order

1. Build the FAQ response library first; low risk and fast adoption.
2. Add lead intake/first-response templates.
3. Pilot dispatch note cleanup with two technicians for two weeks.
4. Add review sequence after operations accepts the first three changes.

## Recommended Build

Start with a $4,500 fixed-fee Build covering FAQ library, lead response templates, CRM setup, staff training, and a 14-day tune-up window.
