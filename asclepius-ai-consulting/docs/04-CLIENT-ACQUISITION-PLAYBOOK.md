# 04 — Client Acquisition Playbook

## 90-day priority

Month 1 priority: warm referrals, partner referrals, and local networking. These are fastest to first client because trust already exists. LinkedIn content and cold outreach compound more slowly and should run in parallel.

Assumptions, not guarantees: 100 warm/referral asks may produce 10–20 calls; 4 networking events may produce 4–8 serious conversations; 200 cold emails may produce 2–6 calls; one workshop may produce 2–5 diagnostics if the room is targeted.

## Weekly cadence

| Channel | Weekly actions | 90-day target |
|---|---|---:|
| LinkedIn organic | 3 posts, 10 thoughtful comments, 5 DMs to engaged owners. | 36 posts |
| Local networking | Attend 1 event/week; book 2 follow-up coffees. | 12 events |
| Warm referrals | Ask 10 people/week for one intro. | 120 asks |
| Cold outreach | Send 50 targeted emails/week plus LinkedIn view/DM. | 600 emails |
| Partner referrals | Contact 5 CPAs/bookkeepers/agencies/coaches/week. | 60 partners |
| Workshops | Pitch 2 hosts/week; deliver 1/month. | 3 workshops |
| Local groups | Post value 2x/week; DM helpful follow-up only. | 24 posts |

## Month-by-month plan

### Month 1

Build the first trust loop: warm asks, local meetings, partner coffee chats, and one workshop pitch. Goal: 3 paid Diagnostics.

### Month 2

Turn early calls into proof: publish anonymized bottleneck stories, ask every Diagnostic client for two introductions, and close 1–2 Builds. Goal: 5 additional Diagnostics and 1 Retainer conversation.

### Month 3

Systematize what worked: double the best channel, run a second workshop, and formalize 3–5 referral partners. Goal: 6–8 Diagnostics, 2–3 Builds, 1–2 Retainers.
