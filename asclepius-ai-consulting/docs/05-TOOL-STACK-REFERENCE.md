# 05 — Tool Stack Reference

**Dated reference: August 2026. Verify current pricing, features, privacy terms, and integrations before prescribing. This list goes stale fast. Review/update monthly.**

## Customer service

Categories: website chat, shared inboxes, helpdesk macros, AI-assisted reply drafting. Examples to verify: Intercom, Zendesk, Help Scout, Gorgias, Tidio, Crisp, ChatGPT Team/Enterprise with custom GPTs.

## Scheduling

Categories: booking pages, routing forms, automated reminders, calendar coordination. Examples: Calendly, SavvyCal, Acuity, Google Calendar appointment schedules, Motion, Reclaim.

## Content and marketing

Categories: drafting assistants, repurposing, creative generation, social scheduling. Examples: ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Canva, Descript, OpusClip, Buffer, Hootsuite.

## Bookkeeping and admin

Categories: receipt capture, invoice workflows, document extraction, spreadsheet cleanup. Examples: QuickBooks, Xero, Dext, Hubdoc, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Zapier, Make.

## Sales follow-up

Categories: lightweight CRM, email templates, meeting notes, lead routing, proposal follow-up. Examples: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Close, Apollo, Gmail/Outlook templates, Fireflies, Fathom.

## Internal knowledge and search

Categories: SOP libraries, searchable docs, AI assistants over approved files. Examples: Notion, Coda, Google Drive, SharePoint, Slack AI, Glean, Guru.

## Prescription principle

Start with the client’s existing stack. A boring workflow inside tools they already pay for beats a shiny AI tool that staff will not adopt.
