## Post 1 — owner time drain
Most small business owners do not need “an AI strategy.” They need one repeated task removed from their week.

Examples I look for in an AI Diagnostic:
- leads waiting too long for follow-up
- staff retyping the same customer answers
- messy job notes that delay invoices
- owners rebuilding the same reports every Friday

The win is not looking futuristic. The win is getting five hours back.

If you own a 3–50 person business and suspect AI could help but do not know where to start, I’m booking AI Diagnostics.

## Post 2 — anti-hype
AI is loud right now. Small business adoption should be quiet.

No massive transformation deck. No random tool subscription. No replacing your team.

Just diagnose the bottleneck, prescribe the workflow, train the people who use it, and measure whether time came back.

## Post 3 — story format
I reviewed a service business where the owner thought “marketing AI” was the opportunity. The real leak was lead response. Inquiries sat for hours because the office manager was buried.

The first prescription was not glamorous: intake cleanup, approved response templates, and a follow-up sequence.

That is the point. Practical beats impressive.
