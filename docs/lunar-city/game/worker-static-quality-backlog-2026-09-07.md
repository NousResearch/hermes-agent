Re-render the exact edited candidate before closing a row.

P1 = fix visible structural/projection defects before static acceptance. P2 = retain recognizable silhouette but repair projection, part definition and surface quality. No worker is marked complete. Global washout in these renders may include lighting; do not prescribe brighter/darker material values without neutral-light and base-color inspection.

A recurring visible defect is repeated artwork on the roof: a face, tool, beacon or logo appears twice. The inspected three-view color projector has no PLAN sampler and falls back to front/back colors on upward-facing normals; a separate four-view projector also exists. Saved receipts do not distinguish these producers, so PLAN projection is not established as the cause for a particular export. Some cards themselves put chest symbols on roofs, or disagree about antenna position, support count and pose. The observations below distinguish visible duplication from design ambiguity. A painted duplicate is not proof of duplicate mesh. Solid, wireframe and material-isolation views must decide which layer to repair.

## Repair order and closure evidence

1. Remove or reconstruct annotation artifacts, detached fragments, broken tool ends and fused major branches: data, CI, arts, memory, knowledge, core first. Retain immutable originals.
2. Reconcile a single 3D part inventory against all four card views. Clean roof projections without copying a second face/tool into geometry. Rebuild shell surfaces and mechanical gaps with deliberate silhouettes; do not use smoothing alone to hide broken branches.
3. Check front, both sides, rear, top and underside in neutral solid shading, then compare materials at equal lighting. Inspect feet/wheels/support contacts at the real design height (default 1.20 m; compact archive 0.95 m). These images do not measure dimensions or prove grounded pivots.
4. Save candidate-specific static turnarounds and close individual defects only after visual inspection. Rig stress results may identify suspicious fused topology, but neither stress-pose success nor a game import accepts static beauty. Resume rigging only after static shape and parts are approved.
