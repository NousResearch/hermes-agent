# Sensitive family-conflict email drafting (NDR's style)

For emails to family members in the business (e.g. Bhavik Ranka, first cousin) where NDR
must rebut a written accusation but keep the relationship. Long multi-round workflow —
NDR dictates facts in voice chunks over many turns; the draft is ITERATED in chat until
he says "go", then built as HTML and left as a Gmail draft. Never send.

## Workflow
1. Before drafting, pull the facts from source systems and VERIFY them:
   - Kelsa petty-cash records: amounts, raised date, approved date, by whom, attached vouchers, current stage.
   - Drive: the project folder only (users say "only limit your search to <project> folder" — do exactly that, check the parent chain before downloading so you don't pull a same-named folder from another project).
   - Present the verified record (IDs, dates, Kelsa links) to NDR in the first reply — he builds the email around it.
2. Draft in sections: facts → what happened (timeline, dates) → the accusation dismantled politely → what was contributed → where the hurt lies → exit/next steps. Numbered sections, bold markers (**...**) standing in for the bold/larger text NDR wants in the finished HTML.
3. Each round, NDR corrects facts, dates, tone, and scope. Apply EVERY correction; re-present the full draft. He reviews in chat before any HTML is built.
4. End each round with a short "confirm these N points" list (spellings, dates, whether a risky line stays). He answers terse — fix and redraft.

## Tone rules (NDR's explicit corrections — violation = draft rejected)
- Polite, non-hurtful, family-sensitive. Show hurt and pain on NDR's side, NOT aggression.
- Open with something like "I am sorry if you feel that way. It was never my intention..." — then pivot that the same disrespect is happening to him.
- Do NOT go out of the way to defend/justify the third party (e.g. the employee whose payment was withheld). Short, factual. Long defense reads as an ego tussle; NDR explicitly asked to cut it.
- Do NOT name approving authorities when accusing ("decisions approved by you" → "decisions taken because it was the need of the hour").
- Do NOT put potentially incriminating admissions in writing — grey-area / pre-OC / illegal-adjacent work must be phrased with suggestion, never explicit statement ("everything that could be advanced before OC was advanced"; "matters of a sensitive nature" instead of naming illegalities).
- Avoid taunts ("were you expecting me to make good 8000 from my pocket" → rephrase as a direct question of fairness).
- Repeat key themes NDR wants emphasized: "free of cost, not on salary", "not the first time", the disrespect of withholding an approved payment without telling him.
- Ask hard questions as genuine requests for clarification ("what safeguard was violated? what were you going to check? please tell me which one it was") — this is his favourite rhetorical device.
- Close with ACTIONABLE SOLUTIONS, not blame: name options (jointly hire a professional to finish / fix a defined window to finish together / someone else steps in), propose board meeting, state his own condition (if he spends more time he expects compensation).
- Show the timeline as bolded bullets — the sequence is the case.

## Fact-checking pitfalls
- Kelsa stage "Expense Approved" does NOT mean paid — accountant often doesn't record the actual payment. Say "not released" only when NDR confirms; otherwise "I am told Kelsa stage doesn't always reflect payment".
- Spellings matter: Eshwari is E-S-H-W-A-R-I (accounts), Dharmesh (not Thamesh), Anbu = Anbarasan, Gopal Sanitations / Shankar Electricals.
- Confirm dates NDR gives from memory ("around 18 Sep") before putting them in writing; he often revises 2-3 times.
- Kelsa records may be reused for a different project name in narration — verify company and project fields before citing.
- **Verify a recalled "smoking gun" record actually proves what NDR thinks before citing it as one.** In the Ranka Iris session a chipping-machine entry was remembered as a ₹7,200 benchmark and as "approved by Bhavik" — the record showed a per-day breaker rental (not comparable) and Bhavik only wrote "Please release" on it. Pull the record, read its content and note trail, and if it contradicts his memory, say so and write it accurately — he checks dates and figures hard.

## Delivery
- Final: build HTML with bold + larger text on marked lines, threaded reply (or reply-all) to the original mail in NDR's Gmail Drafts. He sends it himself. Never send.
