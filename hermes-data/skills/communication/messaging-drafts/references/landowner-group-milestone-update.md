# Landowner-Group Milestone Update (WhatsApp group broadcast)

The recurring NDR task: *"give all the landowners an update"* — a long WhatsApp
message to a project landowner group announcing a milestone and convening an
all-hands meeting. Verified 2026-09-12 on the **Ranka Northstar Landowners**
group (Ranka Northstar / Allalasandra).

Related: `messaging-drafts` → `references/personal-messaging-workflow.md`, `messaging-drafts` → `references/messaging-links-workflow.md` SKILL.md §2 (group link
= empty phone), §3 (no raw `&`), P5 (long-message splitting).

## Step 1 — Ingest the group's own history FIRST (non-negotiable)

NDR usually uploads the WhatsApp export with the request: web-chat attachments
land at `/mnt/uploads/<id>_WhatsApp Chat with <Group>.txt`. **Read the whole
file** (these run 400+ lines) before drafting anything.

He states the priority himself: *"keeping the entire context the long duration
the sequence dates are critical"*. Extract from the transcript:

- **Group identity + rename history** (created as "Allalasandra- LO & Dev Team"
  → renamed "Ranka Northstar" → "Ranka Northstar Landowners") and the member
  list — who is a landowner, who is DRA staff.
- **NDR's own last update** — the new message must read as a continuation of it
  (what was promised, on what date, and what has since changed).
- **The follow-up personalities** — who chases (Vaidyanathan writes formal
  "kindly respond with definite time-bound action plans" messages every few
  weeks; Swaminathan is the senior landowner whose sign-off matters).
- **The emotional state** — long delays already apologised for; the message
  must open with the milestone, not with another apology.

**Pitfall — do NOT claim you can see the group chat live.** You cannot; Hermes
has no WhatsApp connection. What you have is (a) every message you previously
drafted for the group and (b) whatever export the user uploads. Say so plainly
if asked, then ask for the export ("WhatsApp → group → Export chat") — that is
how the context becomes available.

## Step 2 — Ground the money/negotiation context from Drive

A landowner update is read against the private commercial history. Before
drafting, pull the landowner folder (Entity → Project → Land Owners → Name):
`Exit Proposal/`, `Correspondence/`, `Banking and Financial Information/`.
For Ranka Northstar the Sunder Padmanabhan folder id is
`1Rc0dBjlVwp0lUATZ9Sr59DMWpICYWDji` (exit proposal notes, agreed commercial
terms PDF, WhatsApp chat transcript).

Keep straight which facts are **public to the group** and which are private to
one landowner. Private terms (e.g. a ₹20L advance, the collapsed exit offer,
₹2 Cr consideration) must NEVER appear in a group broadcast — they inform
*your understanding* of why a person behaves as they do, not the text.

## Step 3 — Message structure (approved shape)

Six blocks, WhatsApp markdown, blank line between blocks:

1. **Headline** — `*🌟 <PROJECT> — <MILESTONE> 🌟*` then a short warm line.
2. **What we have achieved** — 3 bullets max, the facts (approval secured,
   plans vetted, in-principle approval, sanction within 30 days).
3. **The meeting request** — name who must travel ("Sundar sir, you are the
   only one who will need to fly in"), ask everyone to come **"with your legal
   heirs"** and to bring **heirs/children** for transparent succession
   discussion.

   **CORRECTED 2026-09-21 — it is "legal heirs", NOT "legal ears".** NDR's
   dictation says "legal ears" (STT); the first draft reproduced it verbatim and
   he corrected: *"update draft 2 to correct 'ears' = 'heirs'."* Always write
   **legal heirs**. Same dictation also produced "Kanshika" (→ **Kankshita
   Swaminathan**), "Shriraman / Sriram" (→ **Sreeraman Vaidyanathan**),
   "Alal Sandra" (→ **Allalasandra**) and "Karvin" (→ **Arvind**). Correct
   landowner names against the addendum/JDA party list (see
   `property-title-due-diligence` → `references/jda-addendum-party-signatory-verification.md`)
   before the message goes out, and tell NDR which names you corrected.
4. **Two objectives** — numbered `1)` `2)`: (a) register the JDA addendum
   (state the *why*: no bank pre-approval or project finance without it, and
   commit to same-day registration); (b) clear outstanding **refundable**
   matters **between landowners** ("these are matters you resolve amongst
   yourselves") with the businessman's framing: *my further investment should
   not get stuck in any misunderstanding between landowners*.

   **CORRECTED 2026-09-21 — it is "refundable" ONLY. Never write
   "non-refundable".** The first draft said "refundable / non-refundable" and
   NDR corrected: *"There is only refundable. There is no non-refundable at all.
   So we are only talking about refundable."* The signed addendum captures the
   developer↔landowner position fully; the residual issue is purely how the
   **refundable** is shared between the landowners (who bears what), so
   objective (b) asks the landowner group to sit together and settle it as a
   group — the developer has no outstanding matter of its own.
5. **One critical decision** — the in-person-only item (e.g. khata area vs
   actual land in possession after Kaveri 2.0). State the documentary facts,
   then: *"certain aspects cannot be discussed in writing — better in person"*.
   Always signal a solution exists and needs the group's buy-in; never leave
   the decision looking unsolved.
6. **What happens next + one commitment** — dated bullets (same-day
   registration → upload → sanction in 30 days → RERA in the next 30 →
   groundbreaking/Bhoomi Puja → construction). Close with the single commitment
   (fastest pace the contractors can deliver) and gratitude for decades of
   patience. Sign `*Nishant Ranka*`.

## Tone / psychology rules (NDR dictation, keep them)

- Acknowledge the decades of patience and investment *with the deepest
  respect*; never lecture the group.
- Land the metaphor he uses: *"the light at the end of the tunnel is finally
  here"*.
- Frame the ask as collective ("let us get on the table"), not as a complaint
  about disputes.
- Acknowledge the relationship survives past disagreements: *"whatever ups and
  downs we have had, I am sure we share a respectful relationship"*.
- Everything must feel forward-moving: milestone → meeting → registration →
  construction. No analysis of past blame.

## Formatting pitfalls (WhatsApp, not Markdown)

- **No underline exists in WhatsApp.** NDR asks for "bold and underlined"
  headings — deliver **bold caps headings** (`*WHAT WE HAVE ACHIEVED*`) and say
  in the chat reply that underline isn't supported so bold-caps was used.
- **Never use `~`** — it renders as strikethrough. Write "approximately 43,000
  sq ft", not "~43,000".
- Bullets `•`, numbered `1)` `2)`, one idea per line, blank line between blocks.
- **No raw `&`** (tool converts it to a fullwidth ampersand — breaks the link).
  Write "and" / "plus".
- Round/approximate sensitive figures and say they are approximate; the exact
  area must be confirmed from the survey sketch before anyone relies on it.

## Delivery

- **Show the FULL text in chat and get approval BEFORE generating links
  (NDR workflow, 2026-09-21).** For landowner-group broadcasts NDR reviews the
  wording separately from the delivery: *"can you give me the draft here only in
  HTML? … so I can decide which draft to use"*, and for the heavily-edited ones
  *"once I approve it, give me the WhatsApp links for it."* So: present the
  complete message text (all parts, labelled `PART n OF m`) in the chat reply,
  wait for the edit rounds, and only then call `whatsapp_link`. He will iterate
  several times on specific sections ("re-do just the two objectives") — apply
  the edit to that section only, show the revised section, and carry everything
  else forward unchanged. When he asks for HTML, write a plain WhatsApp-style
  HTML file (styled bubbles, pre-wrap) to `/opt/data/drafts/` and tell him the
  path; do not replace the in-chat full text with the file.
- Group link = **no phone** (`args = {"phone": "", "text": msg}`) — the user
  picks the group at compose time.
- Long broadcasts split (this one: 3 parts for the full draft, 2 for the
  condensed one). Deliver **one link per part**, each labelled
  `(Part N of M)`; never merge parts into one bubble.
- Offer two drafts when the user says "one or two drafts if required":
  a **full** version (all six blocks, more psychology) and a **concise**
  version (same facts, tighter). Label them clearly so a tap is unambiguous.
- If a terminal fallback is needed (tool not registered in the session), call
  `tools.whatsapp_link_tool` through the Hermes venv — see SKILL.md's
  "Terminal fallback when tool isn't registered".

## The broadcast is a SEQUENCE, not one message (extended 2026-09-21)

One milestone update is never the whole job. Over the following days NDR sends a
chain of follow-ups to the same group, each building on the last reply. Track the
running state and reuse the approved blocks verbatim (headline style, block names,
sign-off) so the group reads one continuous thread.

The observed sequence on Ranka Northstar Landowners:

1. **Milestone + all-hands meeting request** (the six-block message above).
2. **Date confirmation** — one landowner (Sundar) confirms a date; the next
   message thanks him by name, names the date, asks everyone else to confirm,
   and lists the day's logistics (10 AM at the office, transport to the
   sub-registrar, lunch, discussion). Tone goes noticeably less formal here —
   NDR's correction: *"I want this message to be made more informal and
   things."* Short sentences, "my treat", "just let me know".
3. **Answers to queries raised in the group** — restate each question, give the
   specific documentary answer, and state plainly what stays between the
   landowners vs what is the developer's business.
4. **Blocker notification** — when a procedural obstacle appears (e.g. an
   unregistered GPA not accepted by the sub-registrar), state it factually,
   name the process required, ask for the contact needed to expedite, give the
   revised timeline honestly ("a week to 10 days"), say what continues in
   parallel, and thank them for patience.
5. **Practical re-plan** — when one stakeholder's availability slips, propose
   the split: hold the decision meeting on the available dates, and do the
   registration later once the blocker clears.

### Same-day follow-up messages (individual, not broadcast)

The group thread usually spawns 1:1 messages to the same people — bringing one
landowner round on a procedural point, asking a colleague to book the
sub-registrar, requesting a scan from the office vault. For those, use the
individual link (with phone) and the colleague-caption rule in SKILL.md; keep the
tone identical to the group message that preceded it.
