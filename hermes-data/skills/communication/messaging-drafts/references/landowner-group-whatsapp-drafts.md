# Landowner Group WhatsApp Updates — Drafting Workflow (Ranka North Star pattern)

Reusable pattern for NDR's landowner-group messages (real-estate JDA projects where a group
of co-owners holds land under a JDA and the developer communicates project milestones).

## Pre-draft grounding (do all three BEFORE drafting)

1. **Read the exported group chat first.** NDR often attaches "WhatsApp Chat with ... .txt"
   via /mnt/uploads. Ingest it — dates and sequence are critical to him ("the long duration,
   the sequence dates are critical"). The group history tells you what was already promised
   (e.g. "will share plans once in-principle approval comes") — your update must land on that
   thread of promises.
2. **Study the Drive transaction files** for the specific landowner (e.g. Sunder Padmanabhan
   exit file: ₹2 Cr consideration, ₹20L advance, balance at registration) so numbers cited are
   grounded, not recalled.
3. **Verify names/roles**: who is Bangalore-based vs flying in; who has decision authority
   (e.g. Vaidyanathan takes decisions for his family; Swaminathan defers to group).

## Message structure that worked (NDR-approved psychology)

- Bold ALL-CAPS heading line with 🎉/🌟 emoji + project name + milestone.
- Warm greeting (festival tie-ins land well — Ganesha Chaturthi opening was approved).
- "Critical milestone reached" section with bullets of concrete progress (FAR confirmed,
  vetting done, sanction timeline 30 days).
- Meeting request: name the one person who must travel, ask heirs/legal heirs to attend,
  "legal ears" → correct to "legal heirs" (NDR corrected this — heirs, not ears).
- TWO OBJECTIVES section, numbered: (1) register JDA addendum, same-day; (2) resolve
  refundable matters between landowners — developer side has no outstanding matter, addendum
  provides proportionate recovery; landowners are collectively responsible as a group.
- ONE CRITICAL DECISION (khata vs actual land area): say "we have a solution that needs
  everyone's buy-in; some aspects cannot be put in writing" — this opaque phrasing is
  deliberate to force in-person attendance.
- WHAT FOLLOWS: addendum registered same day → plan upload, no NOCs → sanction 30 days →
  RERA next 30 → groundbreaking/puja → rapid construction commitment.
- Close with gratitude + "Nishant Ranka / DRA Ranka Holdings".

## Formatting & delivery rules

- WhatsApp markdown: *bold* headings, • bullets, numbered 1) items, blank lines between
  paragraphs, emoji sparingly. User wants it "cozy and readable".
- Long messages auto-split into parts via the whatsapp_link tool — send EACH part as its own
  message in the group, in order. Never combine.
- Group link = no phone parameter (user picks the group on WhatsApp).
- When giving NDR the drafts, ALWAYS show the full text in the chat too (he picks between
  drafts by reading; "give me the draft here only in HTML / as text").
- Offer 2 versions: Draft 1 full/emotional, Draft 2 concise — then iterate on the chosen one.
- When a landowner replies privately (e.g. Vaidyanathan "what is final take"), draft the reply
  for THAT person, not the group, and resolve their number via contact_resolver fresh each time
  (see contact-phone-lookup references/never-quote-numbers-from-memory.md).

## Content cautions

- Don't let the 43k vs 53k khata issue be discussed in writing; push to in-person.
- Refundable: developer captured everything in the addendum; NO outstanding matter from
  developer side; landowners resolve among themselves; any private arrangement contrary to
  proportionate recovery is between landowners only.
- Registration date slips (GPA/consulate delays): be transparent, give new timeline
  ("shift by a week to 10 days"), keep everything else "ready and parallel".
- Sudar/Sundar/Sunder — contact sheet spelling: Sunder Padmanabhan (Sunder sir).
