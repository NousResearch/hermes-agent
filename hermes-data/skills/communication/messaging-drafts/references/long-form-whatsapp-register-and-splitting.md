# Long-form WhatsApp: pro register + part splitting

Worked case: **DR Villa (Ranka 99) position paper for Roshni, 2026-09-25.**
Two corrections from NDR in one turn — one about register, one about splitting.
Both are now rules in SKILL.md; this file is the worked example.

## What went wrong (turn 1 — the failed attempt)

NDR dictated ~1,300 words of reasoning: development is dead, two valuation paths,
rental maths, buyer obstacles, survey mismatch, his recommendation.

Agent's first draft compressed it into **terse bullets** and split it into **2 parts**.

His two complaints, verbatim:

> *"This bullet point style does not capture a lot of the details that I had provided.
> So please redo it explaining every point... point by point by point so each point
> has to be elaborated not just capturing only the key points like you have done.
> So bullet point to show each point separately but elaborated each point elaborated
> with relevant details."*

> *"The part 2 has not come out with a WhatsApp link... it has ended at point 1,
> point 2, point 3 and only the title of point 4, not the body."*

So: **both** the register and the split were wrong. The split problem is not
cosmetic — a part that ends on a bare heading is unreadable in isolation.

## What good looks like (turn 2 — the accepted version)

**8 numbered points, delivered as 6 parts**, each part self-contained:

| Part | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 of 6 | 1. Development of the Plot — Effectively Closed |
| 2 of 6 | 2. How Do We Arrive at a Value? Two Paths · 3. Path One — Rental |
| 3 of 6 | 4. Path Two — Finding a Buyer · 5. The Bigger Risk — Problems Get Harder with Time |
| 4 of 6 | 6. The Biggest Red Flag — Survey Number Mismatch |
| 5 of 6 | 7. What This Means in Front of a Buyer |
| 6 of 6 | 8. My Recommendation |

Note the pairing in parts 2 and 3: two short sections were merged into one part so
the part was a *sensible unit*, not a fragment. Splitting is not "one section per
part" — it is "each part is a complete readable thought".

Every point is a full paragraph. Example of the required density:

> **3. Path One — Rental**
> Rental is the far more straightforward and defensible route. Last time we had an
> offer, or at least a discussion, from what appeared to be an F&B brand — possibly
> Bastion, though the agent did not disclose the brand name. The proposition was a
> rental of ₹5 lakh per month, after we renovate the structure. The renovation itself
> was estimated at about ₹2 crore. The arithmetic is then simple and clean. ₹5 lakh a
> month is ₹60 lakh a year. Even at a 5% yield — which is typical for residential —
> that translates to a value of ₹12 crore. That is how I arrived at ₹12 crore as the
> bottom line.

Contrast with what was rejected: "*~₹5L/month post renovation · ₹12 Cr bottom line*"
— true, but it destroys the reasoning NDR wanted to *show* the recipient.

## Method: rent-capitalisation when the sale route is blocked

Recurring DRA pattern — derive asset value from rental when a clean sale is
impossible. Worth reusing for the other liquidation-book assets.

1. **Anchor rent from an actual offer**, and name it even if the brand is undisclosed
   ("possibly Bastion; agent did not disclose the brand").
2. **Annualise**: monthly rent × 12.
3. **Divide by the residential yield** (~5% is NDR's working assumption).
   `₹5L/month → ₹60L/yr ÷ 5% = ₹12 Cr`.
4. **Add renovation capex** as a separate line (₹2 Cr here) — it is capex, not a
   discount to value.
5. **Cross-check against market comparables** to produce a band, then state the
   number actually offered to a counterparty (₹12–15 Cr; offered Mamta ₹15 Cr).
6. **Keep commercial deals out of asset value.** Rental-plus-revenue-share is a
   *business call*, not an asset value — NDR insists on that separation explicitly.

**Sale-blocker taxonomy to check before ever promising a buyer** (each is a real
blocker seen on Ranka 99, not hypothetical):
- Title papers not in hand — sitting in access, awaiting a succession process
- Khata only part-way (to the ancestor's name); next transfer needs a family
  settlement that is **unregistered**
- **Survey mismatch** — title in Sy 92/93 but the villa sits in Sy 96; requires
  survey-boundary rectification, a *phodi* revenue process, which again needs
  resident cooperation
- **Resident consent**, now reinforced by a recent **BDA notification making
  resident approval mandatory** for any modification, *even where extra FAR exists*
- None of this touches **possession** — possession is unchallenged, and NDR holds
  that possession alone is the value base

Blocker classes worsen with time as records digitise — say so; NDR framed it that way.
