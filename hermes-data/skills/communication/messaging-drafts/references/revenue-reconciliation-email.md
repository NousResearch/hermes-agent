# Revenue reconciliation email — worked example (Ranka Oasis × Jiraaf/Giraffe, Oct 2026)

## Situation
Colleague (Sarthak Sharma, admin3.blr@draas.com — "Sartak") emailed updated Section 9 financials on 8 Oct 2026: Jiraaf revenue **Rs 95.12 Cr (95,12,22,967)** against **Jiraaf saleable BUA 97,279 sqft**. Agreed deal inputs: DRA delivers a *sellable super built-up area* (not FSI/FAR), average realisation **Rs 9,000/sqft** (endeavour, non-guaranteed), **5% sales & marketing fee** netted off revenue.

## Expected working (the "target")
- 97,279 × 9,000 = **Rs 87.55 Cr** (87,55,11,000)
- less 5% S&M = **Rs 83.17 Cr** (83,17,35,450)

## Implied-rate test — spot the mismatch in one line
95,12,22,967 ÷ 97,279 = **Rs 9,778/sqft**, and no 5% deduction visible. Quote this in the draft: "your figure implies ~₹9,778/sqft with no 5% marketing fee" — it forces the model owner to answer which area/rate/deductions they used.

## Draft pattern (to the model owner)
1. State his figure and the model's own area.
2. State your inputs: area, rate, marketing %.
3. Show the arithmetic visibly (area × rate = X; less 5% = Y).
4. Ask: which area, which rate, which deductions — share the exact calc immediately.

## Correction to the counterparty
Same numbers, shorter, no accusation. Thread it as a reply to the **previously SENT pack email** (reply to the sent message so To/Cc resolve from what actually went out). WhatsApp equivalent is a one-liner: "the revenue you need to project for Giraffe: Rs 87.55 Cr less 5% S&M = Rs 83.17 Cr."

## Dictation vs source-rule
NDR dictated "92,729 sqft"; the model and Sartak's email both say **97,279 sqft**. NDR's ruling: *"Don't go by my dictation — go by Sartak's 8th October email and the model."* Source document wins over voice dictation; always flag the conflict before finalising.
