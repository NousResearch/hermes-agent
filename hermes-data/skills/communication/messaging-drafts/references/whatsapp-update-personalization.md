# WhatsApp update messages — external counsel / CA personalization (NDR, 2026-09-30)

Applies when NDR says "personalize the messages" for updates to external parties (lawyers,
CAs, advisors) about a live matter — e.g. court-hearing status updates. Dictated verbatim
30-Sep-2026 while re-drafting the P-141 (Ashok Kumar v UOI) update for Trust Law + CA.

## NDR's rules
1. **Greet by first name**: "Hi Darshan, good morning." — never a nameless "Good morning."
2. **Skip the numbers when the recipient knows the matter** — no case/WP numbers, no IA
   numbers, no filing dates, no judge name: *"We don't need to get into too much detail
   because he knows the IA, he knows the matter... we don't need to refer to the numbers
   and all."* Keep only: hearing took place → matter posted/adjourned to next date → no
   order published → both IAs still pending.
3. **Role-differentiated ask**:
   - Appearing counsel (the firm that appeared in court) → *"since your team appeared in the
     matter, just wanted to know what you're doing on it and how you'd suggest we take it
     forward."* Do NOT ask "what exactly happened in court" — NDR already knows from them in
     person (*"I know from you exactly what happened in court, that part is fine"*).
   - Non-lawyer stakeholder (e.g. CA Saurabh) → inform + advice ask: *"no order was passed,
     it's posted to the 13th; it looks like it's going from date to date. Just wanted to
     know your advice — how do you think we should handle this?"*
4. Deliver as WhatsApp deep links only (whatsapp_link tool, platform='telegram'; show the
   display_link inline) — nothing is sent, NDR taps to send.

## Generalizable shape
- One factual update shared by all recipients; per-recipient tailoring is just the greeting
  + one role-appropriate ask (check-in on work vs request for advice).
- When re-personalizing an existing draft: keep the facts byte-identical across recipients,
  change greeting and ask per role.
