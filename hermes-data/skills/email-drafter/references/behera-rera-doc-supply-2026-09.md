## Operative JDA / Addendum clauses (for the Appeal's non-joinder + direction-against-KPDL grounds)
Verified from the registered instruments (OCR, 24.09.2026). Reproduce these in the Appeal / impleadment application:
- **Cl. 4.1 & 4.5** — Developer builds "at its own cost and expenses"; Owner "shall have no liability of any nature whatsoever" for construction; Developer "shall at all times keep the Owners harmless and protected and shall indemnify... against all actions, costs, claims, losses and damages".
- **Cl. 6.2 & 10.3** — Owner executed a registered, irrevocable, consideration-backed GPA in favour of the Director/s of the Developer (Naresh A. Patil / Vandana Patil) to "facilitate for sale and transfer" and agreed "not to cancel the said GPA" — the machinery through which KPDL marketed/sold owner-share units (incl. B1-203).
- **Cl. 9.3** — in delay of delivering the Owner's area, "the developer shall be liable to pay the owner compensation" at ₹25/sq.ft/month after a 6-month buffer — JDA itself puts delay liability on the Developer, closely analogous to the RERA delay-interest formula.
- **Cl. 10.2** — unqualified indemnity from Developer to Owner for any failure to discharge its liabilities/obligations.
- **Cl. 14.6** — Developer had the mandate to market even the Owner's allocation at an Owner-approved price (3% commission); Owner free to sell directly — evidence that landowner-share allottees (like Behera) were marketed/negotiated/compensated by KPDL.
Takeaway for the appeal: KPDL assumed full turnkey responsibility (construction, approvals, marketing, sale, RERA obligations); landowner's role was title + share of proceeds → excluding KPDL while weighing delay liability entirely on the landowner does not carry the ends of justice.

## Pitfalls / techniques learned
1. **"We found all five" can overstate reality.** Empty pack sections ≠ documents don't exist; registered instruments frequently exist ONLY as Gmail attachments. Search Gmail by topic keyword + project (`q='"SPA" mirabilis'`, `q='"power of attorney" mirabilis'`, `q='from:roshan@ashlarlaw.com mirabilis'`, `q='from:admin2.blr@draas.com'`) before concluding anything is missing.
2. **docx "GPA"/"SPA" template files with blanks (`[ ]` / `[]`) are NOT executed instruments** — verify by extracting `word/document.xml` text via zipfile before attaching; prefer the registered PDF (Doc No. 1352/2022-23 etc.).
3. Gmail attachment download needs the base64 padding guard (`len(data)%4` → append `=`).
4. **When one requested doc is genuinely missing, keep the draft honest** — write "arranging a certified copy, will share" and flag the gap to NDR in chat; never silently omit or fabricate.
5. **Reply anchoring:** user says "reply to that email" (the original 5-doc request), but the latest message on the thread re-listed the same pending docs — anchor the reply to the latest message (In-Reply-To/References to its Message-ID), same threadId. Keep the appeal-draft reply (authorised signatory etc.) for NDR's subsequent instruction — do not bundle it.
6. Reply-all recipient set: To = counsel; Cc = union of recent thread Cc (Prakash psingh@draas.com, Manish mdr@draas.com, Mamta maadi32@gmail.com, Suhail, Vinay, Pradyumna, info@smritilegal.com).
