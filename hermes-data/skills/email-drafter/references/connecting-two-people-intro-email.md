# The "connecting you two" email (internal + external on one thread)

Verified 2026-10-21. NDR: *"I want an introductory email to <internal> and <external> only…
connecting the two of you."*

This is a coordination email, not a note — use HTML (never plain text with forced line
breaks; it renders as one running block). Default shape below.

## 1. Address list

- **To: both people, no Cc**, when he says "to X and Y only". Adding the cc'd person he
  mentioned in passing is a mis-send.
- Keep the external party's own address exactly as it appears in the thread; the internal
  person's is their real sending address (`gsingh@draas.com` for Gowri Singh), not a guess.

## 2. Subject

ASCII only, `-` not `—`, no ₹. e.g. `Ranka Amber: connecting you two - content, brochure and
creative approvals`.

## 3. Body order that worked

1. **One line on why** — "I am connecting the two of you directly, so that <thing> has one
   channel instead of several."
2. **Introduce both people in one paragraph each, with their remit** — the external party's
   company and why they are in the deal; the internal person's exact ownership ("everything
   from the brochure and walkthrough through to the website sits with her").
3. **"What I have asked <external> to do"** — tinted box, bullets: coordinate direct instead
   of through the earlier middleman; **every creative through the internal owner for approval
   before it runs**; only company-issued creative on material carrying the brand name.
4. **"What I have asked <internal> to do"** — tinted box, bullets: release each collateral to
   the external party as it lands (not only to the previous middleman); send **a content
   inventory with status and expected dates** for every item (name the specific ones —
   brochure, walkthrough, explainer video, website); be the single point of contact for
   review, pricing, availability and any change in the offering.
5. **"What they already have"** — framed link card to the current document, plus one line on
   what it actually is. Then the positioning rule: *"this is too detailed for a first touch;
   it goes only to a prospect already engaged, not to a cold lead, and not as a substitute
   for the brochure."*
6. **Optional red box of concrete fixes** owed before that document goes to any prospect —
   only include if you have actually reviewed it, and keep it to 3 numbered items.
7. Close: one line to each person by name; sign off as MD, DRA Realty.

## 4. Judgement calls

- **Put the short punch list in the email** when you have reviewed the asset — it is
  actionable for the person who has to fix it, and it signals to the external party that
  what they hold is a working document. Offer the user the option to delete that box rather
  than asking permission first.
- **Do not send.** Draft only, always; tell the user it is in Drafts.
- Verify after creating: `labelIds == ['DRAFT']`, both addresses in `To`, subject exact.
