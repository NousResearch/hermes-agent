# Cross-check dictated numbers against the thread before drafting

When NDR dictates an email by voice he is summarising from memory, and he compresses numbers.
The thread already contains the authoritative figures — usually ones **he himself sent hours
earlier**. Reconcile before you draft, and flag any mismatch to him as a question.

## Worked example — Prestige Hermitage khata, 16 Oct 2026

- His **13:52** mail on the thread stated **8 additional car parks** (2 Lower Basement + 6 Upper
  Basement, per Prestige's 16 July 2026 letter), and his own *option 2* left **2 unallotted**.
- His later dictation asked to "conclude on the decision of where the additional **two** car parks
  go — whether to attach it to 2401 or not".

Both readings are plausible, so the draft was written exactly as dictated, and the discrepancy was
flagged **before he sent it**:

> Your earlier mail today put the additional pool at **8** car parks (2 Lower Basement + 6 Upper
> Basement, per Prestige's 16 July letter), and option 2 left **2 unallotted**. The draft says
> "the additional two car parks" — if you mean those 2 unallotted ones, it reads fine; if you mean
> the 2 extra above the base 4 on 2401, say so and I'll reword.

## Rules

- **Write the draft faithfully to the dictation.** Do not silently "correct" his numbers — he may
  have changed position deliberately.
- Pull the last 2–3 messages of the thread and reconcile **every** figure: counts, amounts, dates,
  unit numbers, car park slots.
- **Flag every mismatch** to NDR alongside the draft, phrased as a question. Never bury it in a
  footnote, never paper over it, never guess which reading he meant.
- Same treatment for names, roles and spellings you are not certain of (voice dictation mangles
  these constantly).
- Also flag the arithmetic of the *decision* itself when his own options imply different totals —
  he is asking two people to vote, so the numbers in the ask have to be unambiguous.
