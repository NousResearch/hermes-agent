# Vendor email: asking for something outside the agreed scope — don't be rude

**Trigger:** NDR asks you to draft or redraft an email to a vendor (designer, consultant, contractor)
that asks them for work, where the honest framing is that the work is *not* in their contracted scope —
or where you/DRA have already done part of that work yourselves.

## The correction (NDR, 17-Oct-2026 — Gowri Singh, 13 hoardings)

The first draft contained:

> "preparing these posters was **not our scope**, but we have prepared them anyway… Please treat the
> attached posters as a reference only."

NDR's feedback, verbatim:

> "whatever you have prepared … it was not under our scope but you cannot keep the same thing in the
> draft, it looks rude. … so we are requesting her, can't be so rude."

**Rule: never put the scope-boundary sentence in the email.** The scope argument is an *internal*
justification. The vendor does not need to read it, it reads as a complaint, and it undercuts the ask.
Delete it and let the request stand on its own.

## How to reframe

| Instead of (rude) | Write (request) |
|---|---|
| "this was not our scope" | — omit entirely — |
| "we prepared them anyway" | "we have prepared a working reference ourselves, purely so that X is settled before design begins" |
| "treat the attached as a reference only" | "treat these as a working reference – **the design itself is entirely in your hands**" |
| (nothing) | add a collaborative close: "Happy to walk you through any of these on a call before you begin." |

Three moves make the difference:

1. **Drop the scope sentence** — say what you did, never why it wasn't your job.
2. **Hand authority back** — "the design itself is entirely in your hands" / "we are requesting" /
   "we'd be grateful if you could". The vendor must not feel dictated to or complained at.
3. **Add a collaborative close** — offer a call, offer to walk them through it. One line, big effect.

## Don't over-correct either

Keep the substance exactly as NDR had it — subject, attachments, tables, the specific confirmation
points he asked for. Only the framing of the ask changes. His complaint was about **tone**, not
content: *"the content and all you are well aware of right?"*

## Reporting shape

After the rewrite, state plainly in the chat reply:

- what the rude line was (**quote it**),
- what it now reads,
- what was left unchanged.

NDR wants the diff, not a summary. And confirm it is a **draft only** — nothing sent.
