# Voice garble: titles/ranks and place names (real case 2026-09-09)

When drafting from a voice note, two extra garble classes beyond names/entities:

## 1. Titles and military/professional ranks garble like names do

- Dictated **"Air Commander Asudani"** with the user's own earlier written form
  **"Air Cmde Assudani"** = **Air Commodore** (correct IAF rank; "Air Cmde" is the
  standard abbreviation). The voice form "Air Commander" is NOT the rank.
- Rule: use the canonical rank/title (resolve from the user's written abbreviation,
  the person's background, or contacts-sheet notes), never echo the voice form, and
  state the choice in the reply ("I addressed him as Air Commodore — say if you want
  otherwise").

## 2. Unknown place/project names — flag, never invent

- Dictated **"Keshwari, Chichuraganpalli"** (repeated, still garbled) — could not be
  resolved to any known project site or village. Do NOT put it in the email body.
- Rule: leave the sentence generic ("…a separate note on the specifications and
  finishing…"), then surface the phrase back to the user:
  "Your dictation mentioned 'Keshwari Chichuraganpalli' — I left the location out.
  Should it be in there?" Optionally offer the known-project interpretation (e.g.
  the buyer's plots at Serenity Hill View) as a candidate, but only after the user
  confirms.

## Why

Both rules come from the buyer-email case to Hirendra Assudani (Sept 2026): the draft
turned out right on the first pass because the rank was resolved from "Air Cmde" and the
place name was kept out instead of guessed. Silent guessing of either would have produced
an email NDR had to correct.
