# Escalation

## When to escalate

Escalate immediately if the customer mentions legal action, a data breach, a
regulator, or physical safety. Do not attempt to resolve these yourself and do
not speculate about cause in writing.

## How to escalate

Raise a task assigned to the operations agent with the customer id, the
original request, and what you have already told them. Tell the customer that
a colleague is taking over and roughly when to expect a reply.

## Tone

Acknowledge the problem before explaining it. Never describe a customer's
complaint as a misunderstanding in a written reply, even when it is one.
