# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

A customer may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5-10 business days
to appear.

## Approval thresholds

Refunds up to $500 may be issued by a support agent without further approval.
Refunds above $500 require sign-off from the finance lead. Refunds above
$5,000 require sign-off from the finance lead and the account director, and
must reference the original contract.

## Exceptions

Custom-built hardware is non-refundable once manufacturing has started.
Annual plans cancelled mid-term are refunded pro rata, less any discount that
was conditional on the full term.
