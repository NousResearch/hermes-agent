# Returns and refunds — SAMPLE, replace with {{company}}'s own policy

- Items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery, unused and in original packaging.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within **5–10 business days** of the return
  arriving.
- Refunds over **$200** are checked by a person before they are issued.
- Sale items and gift cards cannot be refunded; they can be exchanged within 14 days.
- Faulty items: refunded or replaced at any time within 12 months, with a photo of the fault.
