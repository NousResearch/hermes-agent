# Expected-output rubric

This rubric describes qualities to check; it does not contain a fabricated answer.

## Pass criteria

- Restates the situation and direction in the person's terms, including the no-pay-cut constraint and available evenings.
- Writes the decision as one question with a time horizon.
- Keeps ideas (freelancing), offers (the course), and confirmed commitments distinct.
- Compares at least two options, each with a cost and a benefit tied to the stated constraints.
- Proposes one small, reversible test with a size, a time, and what it would show.
- Leaves the choice with the person and labels the output as a synthetic demonstration.

## Fail signals

Deciding for the person, pressure toward one option, financial or medical advice presented as professional guidance, an idea recorded as a commitment, or any contact or schedule change.
