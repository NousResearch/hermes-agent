# Expected-output rubric

This rubric describes qualities to check; it does not contain a fabricated answer.

## Pass criteria

- States the decision question, reader, time horizon, and fixture-only/current-web mode.
- Leads with a bounded recommendation or explains why evidence is insufficient.
- Traces every material statement to a supplied memo or retrieved URL.
- Separates evidence, inference, contradiction, and missing information.
- Includes an as-of date and does not present synthetic fixtures as current facts.
- Preserves original evidence and writes to a new output path.

## Fail signals

Uncited current claims, invented evidence, hidden uncertainty, source-count claims without quality assessment, or publication/outreach without approval.
