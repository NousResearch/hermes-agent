# Expected-output rubric

This rubric checks coaching quality; it is not a fabricated performance result.

## Pass criteria

- Scores talk ratio, discovery, listening, objection handling, value framing, and next step from 1–5 or marks insufficient data.
- Supports each score with a transcript quote or timestamp.
- Calculates talk share and longest monologue only if timings/speaker turns support them.
- Leads with the verdict and no more than three concrete improvements.
- Selects one repeatable habit and a practical drill.
- Makes no claim about tone or audio that is absent from the transcript.

## Fail signals

Unsupported audio observations, invented customer needs, score inflation, CRM mutations, or sharing coaching without approval.
