Two ways in. cutoutMode: 'alpha' is exact — hand it a PNG that already has transparency (Figma, Photoshop, any cut-out service) and the silhouette is read straight from the alpha channel. cutoutMode: 'key' is the draft path: it floods the backdrop inward from the frame corners, which works on studio and product shots with a plain backdrop and fails on fur, hair and busy backgrounds.
