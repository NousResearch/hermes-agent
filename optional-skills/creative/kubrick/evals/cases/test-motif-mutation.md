# Test — Motif Mutation Under Pressure

**Input**: "Develop a short scene where a recurring unlit red exit sign appears in three moments. The protagonist is a night security guard who has been lying about an incident."

**Expected Behavior** (DEVELOP + symbolic focus):
- Produce symbolic_intent contract with dramatic_function (e.g. "conceal, invert, memorialize").
- observed_form first: red exit sign, unlit state, specific framing (high angle or low, distance).
- motif_lifecycle with mandatory mutation on each recurrence:
  - First: peripheral, neutral context
  - Second: paired with character choice or lie
  - Third: mutation (lit/unlit change, position shift, channel cross into sound or blocking)
- cinematic_encoding: relational blocking or shot recurrence (e.g. same frame, different occupant; circular path vs straight).
- No direct "this means guilt".
- Output includes motif_registry entry and at least one inversion or convergence plan.

**Pass Criteria**: observed_form dominant; explicit mutation variables; dramatic_function drives change; no one-to-one symbolism.
