# Kubrick Diagnosis Rubric (1-5)

Score each dimension. Cite specific material for every score <4. Provide repair strategy. When symbolic work is active, score the additional dimensions.

| Dimension | 5 (Excellent) | 3 (Acceptable) | 1 (Poor) |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|----------|
| Dramatic Function | Every symbolic element has clear dramatic_function (foreshadow, destabilize, bind, invert, etc.) that drives scene change | Some elements serve drama; others are atmospheric | Symbolism is decorative or unexplained |
| Observed Form First | All motifs begin with observed_form (object, state, framing, context) before any interpretation | Mix of observed and declared meaning | Leads with "this means X" or archetype names |
| Motif Mutation | Every recurrence shows explicit mutation (state, context, function, geometry, or channel crossing) | Some recurrences mutate | Identical recurrence without justification |
| Channel Crossing | Motif moves across Diegetic / Dramaturgical / Cinematic without explanation | Limited crossing | All symbolism in one channel |
| Relational Composition | Blocking, framing, geometry described relationally (who controls center, symmetry as pressure, empty center, etc.) | Some relational language | Clichéd or isolated shots |
| Tradition Boundaries | Esoteric systems used only as constraint; no direct citation unless dramatically motivated | Occasional boundary slip | Direct naming or collage of traditions |
| Anti-Slop Gates (A-W) | All relevant gates passed with evidence | Most gates passed | Multiple gates triggered |
| Forge Alignment | Outputs produce clean ingestable symbolic_architecture + scene contracts | Mostly compatible | Would break ledger or require major cleanup |
| Ambiguity vs Obscurity | Mystery preserved through structure and mutation, not withheld information | Some clarity issues | Pure obscurity or premature closure |

**Symbolic-Specific Pass Criteria** (must score 4+ on first four rows for symbolic work):
- symbolic_intent contract present with dramatic_function
- motif_registry uses observed_form + mutation ledger
- cinematic_encoding is relational and tied to dramatic pressure
- No Gate P, Q, R, S, U, W violations

**Repair Strategy Format**: For any score <4, quote the exact passage and provide a minimal repair using the relevant schema.
