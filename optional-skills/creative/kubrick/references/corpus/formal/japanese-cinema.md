# Formal Tradition: Japanese Cinema (Ozu, Kurosawa, Mizoguchi influences)

## Key Mechanisms

- Negative space and interval (ma): what is not shown carries meaning.
- Low camera height (tatami shots in Ozu) for grounded, observational stance.
- Long takes with precise blocking in Kurosawa.
- Symbolic through environment and gesture rather than overt imagery.
- Transferable: use of empty frames, careful composition, character movement within stable frame.
