# Formal Tradition: Soviet Montage

## Key Mechanisms

- Collision over continuity: juxtapose images to create new meaning (Eisenstein).
- Metric, rhythmic, tonal, overtonal montage.
- Symbolic use: objects or actions gain power through collision rather than development.
- Transferable structure: rapid associative cutting to build idea or emotional state without dialogue.
- Good for: propaganda, political drama, high-tension sequences.
- Mutation: change the colliding elements while keeping the collision rhythm.
