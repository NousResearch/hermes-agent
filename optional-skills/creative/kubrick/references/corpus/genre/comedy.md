# Genre Pack: Comedy

## Core Patterns

### Ritual Form That Fails Comically (Repetition Without Mutation)
- Transformation: attempted coagulation that fragments.
- Affordances: REPEAT (deliberate lack of mutation), INVERT.
- The more the character tries to enforce the pattern, the more it breaks.
- Cinematic: precise, symmetrical compositions that gradually become chaotic.
- Mutation rule: every attempt at the ritual introduces a new small error.

### Geometric Blocking for Physical or Social Comedy
- Triangles of pursuit, circles of avoidance.
- Affordances: BIND, DIVIDE, INVERT.
- Camera height and lens chosen to emphasize physical relationships over faces.
