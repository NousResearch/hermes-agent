# Genre Pack: Horror

## Core Patterns for Horror

### Domestic Space as Trap (Repetition without Mutation → Mutation under Pressure)
**SymbolicNarrativePattern**:
  pattern_id: "horror_domestic_trap"
  name: "Familiar Space Becomes Contaminated Trap"
  source_family: "Horror Cinema"
  source_records:
    - author: "Stanley Kubrick (and broader horror tradition)"
      title: "The Shining (and influences from The Haunting, etc.)"
      date: "20th century horror cinema"
      source_tier: "SCHOLARLY / PRACTITIONER"
  observed_structure:
    agents: ["family / inhabitant", "house / domestic space"]
    objects: ["repeating rooms, carpets, doors, objects"]
    spatial_relation: "once protective now enclosing and hostile"
    temporal_relation: "repetition that tightens rather than comforts"
    sequence: ["arrival / normalcy", "first anomalies", "repetition without release", "full contamination"]

  cinematic_affordances:
    composition: "repeating architectural lines that begin to feel wrong"
    blocking: "characters circling the same paths"
    camera: "steadicam or slow tracking that feels predatory"
    editing: "lengthening takes on familiar spaces"
    sound: "house sounds (creaks, wind) that gain acousmatic power"

  transformation_grammars: ["putrefaction", "contamination", "inversion"]
  affordances: ["CONTAMINATE", "ENCLOSE", "HAUNT", "REPEAT"]

**Usage Notes for Horror**:
- Mutation is critical: the space must change (color bleed, objects migrate, geometry distorts).
- Prefer low-cost production: use existing location + blocking + sound + editing.
- Counterpoint: bright cheerful domestic visuals while sound or behavior reveals horror.

### The Acousmatic Threat That Precedes the Body
- Source: Many horror films (e.g., Paranormal Activity style, but grounded in Chion).
- Affordances: CONCEAL, HAUNT, REVEAL.
- Transformation: gradual de-acousmatization that does not resolve the terror.
- Rule: The reveal should usually make things worse or shift the threat rather than end it.
