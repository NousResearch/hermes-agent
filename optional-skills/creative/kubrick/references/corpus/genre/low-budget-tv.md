# Genre Pack: Low-Budget / Independent / TV / Short-Form

## Core Principles for Constrained Production

- Prefer: performance symbolism, blocking, recurring props, sound design, editorial cadence, controlled composition.
- Avoid when possible: new locations, heavy lighting changes, VFX, large set builds.
- Vertical/short-form: motif must mutate visibly within 15-30 seconds. Use extreme close-up + sound + graphic match.
- TV: motif that can carry across episodes with seasonal mutation and payoff.
- One-location: turn the single space into the main symbolic character (enclosure, contamination, inversion of its original function).
- Documentary: lean on indexical traces (real objects, real sounds) rather than constructed symbols. The "symbol" is the way the real is framed and repeated.
