# Genre Pack: Noir / Thriller

## Core Patterns

### Shadow and High-Contrast as Moral / Psychological Ambiguity
- Transformation grammars: INVERT, CONCEAL.
- Cinematic: Hard key lights, deep shadows, practical sources that leave faces half-lit.
- Affordances: CONCEAL, INVERT, MIRROR.
- Mutation: Light sources that move or fail over the course of the story.
- Production note: Excellent for low-budget (practical lighting + blocking).

### Offscreen Space as Constant Pressure
- Affordances: CONCEAL, HAUNT.
- Use sound bridges, reaction shots, and negative space.
- Never show the full threat until the final inversion or payoff.

### Recurring Match Cuts That Hide or Reveal Identity
- From classic noir and modern thrillers.
- Graphic match that substitutes one face or object for another.
- Affordance: TRANSFER + INVERT.
