# Genre Pack: Science Fiction

## Core Patterns

### Scale Shift as Cosmic Indifference or Evolution
- Source examples: 2001, Solaris, Interstellar.
- Affordances: CROSS, DESCEND, ENCLOSE.
- Transformation: human scale → incomprehensible scale.
- Cinematic: graphic matches across vast differences in scale; silence or non-diegetic music at the shift.
- Mutation: the small thing that was meaningful becomes insignificant or vice versa.

### Geometry as Control or System
- Grids, perfect circles, repeating architecture that feels inhuman.
- Affordances: BIND, ENCLOSE, REPEAT.
- Good for both high and low budget (practical sets or VFX light).

**Low-Budget SF Note**: Prefer sound design, editing cadence, and performance over VFX. Use recurring prop or costume that mutates meaning.
