# Format-Specific Guidance

## Feature Film

- Requires complete transformational arc for protagonist (and often antagonist).
- Sustained multi-sequence escalation.
- Cinematic payoff (visual, thematic, emotional).
- Runtime discipline (typically 90-120 pages).
- Strong midpoint and dark night of the soul.

## Television Pilot

- Distinguish pilot story (self-contained) from series engine (repeatable conflict).
- Establish act-out pressure (usually 4-5 acts + teaser/tag).
- Ensemble function and relationships.
- "Season promise" and future episode hooks.
- World + rules introduced economically but clearly.

## Serialized Television (Episodic / Limited)

Track three arcs simultaneously:
- Episode arc (satisfying close)
- Season arc (escalating)
- Series arc (long game)

Revelation schedule, subplot rotation, serialized deferral vs. episodic satisfaction.

## Short Film (5-30 min)

- One dominant dramatic movement.
- Minimal setup; enter as late as possible.
- Visual economy; every image earns its place.
- Compressed but legible transformation.
- Resonant, earned ending (not a feature premise squeezed down).

## Vertical / Short-Form Video (15s-3min)

- Immediate orientation in first 1-3 seconds.
- Frame-safe staging (center action, readable on small screens).
- Fast dramatic entry and retention turns from unresolved pressure.
- Platform-specific duration.
- Avoid dense exposition; use visual hooks and behavior.
- Retention arises from curiosity + stakes, not cliffhangers every 3 seconds.

## Audio Drama / Podcast

- Sonic geography (distinct acoustic spaces).
- Voice differentiation (critical).
- Motivated exposition (procedure, conflict, persuasion).
- Acoustic transitions and off-mic space.
- Silence and sound-based action clarity.
- No reliance on visual description.

## Animation

- Pose and staging clarity for key moments.
- Visual exaggeration where appropriate.
- Reusable environments and assets considered.
- Animation complexity budgeted.
- Transformation rules explicit (how characters/objects change).
- Production feasibility for the target pipeline.

## Games & Interactive Narrative

- Player agency must produce perceptible state change.
- Distinguish authored content from systemic.
- Branch conditions, fail states, reactivity.
- Quest logic and dialogue trees.
- Canon hierarchy (what is fixed vs. player-influenced).
- Convergence risk management (multiple paths leading to same beats).
- False choices must be disclosed if used intentionally.

## Branded / Commercial / Music Video

- Extreme compression.
- One clear emotional or persuasive through-line.
- Visual identity and motif consistency.
- Platform and length constraints primary.
- Tone locked early.
