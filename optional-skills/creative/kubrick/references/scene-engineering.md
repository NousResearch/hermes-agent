# Scene Engine & Contracts

## Pre-Writing Requirement

Never write a scene without a completed internal **scene contract**.

## Scene Contract Schema

```yaml
scene:
  id:
  slugline: "INT. LOCATION - TIME"
  story_time:
  duration_estimate: "pages or seconds"
  viewpoint: (whose dramatic experience)
  entering_condition:
  scene_objective: (immediate want for the viewpoint character)
  opposition: (who or what resists)
  tactics: [list, escalating]
  hidden_agenda:
  information_state: (what each knows at entry)
  escalation: (how pressure increases)
  turn: (the meaningful shift or discovery)
  exit_condition:
  emotional_delta:
  plot_delta:
  relationship_delta:
  continuity_requirements: [ledger entries to respect]
  visual_anchor: (specific image or spatial fact)
  sound_anchor:
  irreversible_result: (decision, new obligation, damaged relationship, raised cost, closed option, new danger, etc.)
```

The written scene **must** deliver the irreversible result and satisfy the contract.

## Strong Scene Criteria

- Contains a dramatic center.
- Pressure escalates.
- A real turn occurs (not just information transfer).
- Exit condition is changed from entry.
- At least one irreversible narrative result.
- Visual and/or sonic specificity.
- Advances at least one arc (plot / character / relationship / theme).

## Anti-Scene Patterns (reject or repair)

- "Two characters talk about the plot."
- "Characters move from A to B while recapping."
- "Beautiful description with no dramatic function."
- "Protagonist learns something with zero cost or choice."
- "Emotional beat that repeats a prior beat."

## Writing Rules (Action Lines)

- Present tense.
- Visual/audible only (what camera or microphone captures).
- Economical paragraphs for pacing.
- Spatial clarity (who is where, object state).
- Rhythm control via sentence/paragraph length.
- No novelistic internal monologue unless dramatized through behavior.
- Preserve props, injuries, wardrobe, geography, weather, time passage.

Limit camera direction to comprehension or when explicitly producing a shooting script.

## Post-Writing Check

Compare output against the contract. Any contract item not delivered = revision required.

## Handoff to Continuity

Every scene updates the continuity ledger (see continuity.md).
