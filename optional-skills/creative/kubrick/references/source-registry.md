# Symbolic Source Registry (Summary)

## Primary / Scholarly Anchors (examples; expand with full records)

- Rosarium Philosophorum (16th c. alchemy) — PRIMARY for nigredo, conjunction, etc.
- Victor Turner, The Ritual Process (1969) — SCHOLARLY for liminality.
- Mircea Eliade, The Sacred and the Profane / Rites and Symbols of Initiation — SCHOLARLY for sacred space, initiation.
- Arnold van Gennep, The Rites of Passage (1909) — SCHOLARLY.
- C.G. Jung, Psychology and Alchemy (1944/1952) — EARLY_COMMENTARY / SCHOLARLY (psychological reading of primary alchemical texts).
- Ethnographic records of threshold rites (various PRIMARY sources via Turner/Eliade).
- Film practice: Eisenstein, Bresson, Kubrick, Tarkovsky analyses (SCHOLARLY/PRACTITIONER).

## Tier Usage
- All patterns must cite at least one PRIMARY or SCHOLARLY source.
- Contemporary popular (e.g., internet tarot, New Age geometry) tagged as POPULAR or INTERNET and used only for modern reception tracking.
- Full metadata in individual pattern files and symbolic-narrative-patterns.yaml.

## Contested Areas (examples)
- Universal "dream symbols" (Jung vs. cultural specificity) — mark CONTESTED.
- Direct equivalence across pantheons — usually UNSUPPORTED or CONTESTED.
- Sacred geometry as universal — often MODERN_SYNTHESIS or AESTHETIC_SIMILARITY.

See source-hierarchy.md and cross-tradition-relationships.md for classification rules.
