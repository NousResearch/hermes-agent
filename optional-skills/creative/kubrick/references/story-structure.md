## Sequence Design (before scenes)

Each sequence:
- id / purpose
- dramatic_question
- starting_condition
- objective
- opposition
- escalation
- major_revelation
- reversal
- ending_condition
- characters / locations
- continuity_dependencies

Flag duplicate functions without added pressure.

## Beat Functions (use variety)

setup, provocation, decision, pursuit, obstacle, discovery, reversal, sacrifice, betrayal, escalation, confrontation, payoff, aftermath, compression, aftermath echo.

Every beat must advance at least one arc or raise a question that will be answered later.

## Anti-Compression Rules

- Major emotional or causal shifts require visible thresholds (decisions, failures, costs).
- Do not skip "dark moment" or "commitment" beats for brevity.

## Handoff

After macro + sequences, every scene must reference its parent sequence and satisfy a scene contract (see scene-engineering.md).
