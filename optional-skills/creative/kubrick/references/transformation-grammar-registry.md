# Transformation Grammar Registry
# Process-based symbolic records. Each process maps physical/narrative/character/relationship operations to cinematic forms.

## Core Alchemical-Adjacent Processes (adapted as dramatic grammars)

### Calcination / Burning Away
- Physical: reduction by fire/heat to essential matter.
- Narrative: stripping away illusion, social mask, false self through crisis.
- Character: protagonist loses protective layers, status, or belief.
- Relationship: bond purified by removal of pretense.
- Visual form: fire, ash, desaturated palette, harsh light, reduction of set dressing.
- Blocking: character forced into smaller space or stripped of props.
- Shot structure: tight close-ups on face/hands, wide shots of empty space after.
- Editing: rapid cuts to burning or collapsing, then long holds on remains.
- Sound: crackle, silence after, low drone.
- Failure mode: total destruction without rebirth.
- Inversion: over-calcination (nothing left to transform).

### Dissolution / Melting
- Physical: solid to liquid, boundaries dissolve.
- Narrative: identity or relationship liquefies under pressure or emotion.
- Cinematic: soft focus, fluid camera, color bleed, sound becoming diffuse.
- Blocking: characters lose distinct positions, merge movements.
- Mutation example: initial clear boundary becomes shared liquid space.

### Separation / Division
- See DIVIDE in affordance registry.
- Cinematic: split compositions, opposing movements, cut on action that divides.

### Conjunction / Binding
- See BIND.
- Physical: union of opposites.
- Cinematic: convergence of lines, matched movements, shared frame that was previously divided.

### Fermentation / Rotting into New Life
- Physical: decay that produces new substance.
- Narrative: breakdown of old self/relationship that generates new form.
- Cinematic: mold, fermentation visuals, sound of decay becoming music, color shift from rot to new growth.
- Failure: pure rot without new form.

### Distillation / Purification by Vapor
- Physical: heating to separate pure essence.
- Narrative: extracting essential truth through repeated pressure.
- Cinematic: rising camera or steam, clear light after haze, isolated pure sound.
- Shot: repeated similar shots with progressive clarity.

### Coagulation / Solidification
- Physical: liquid to solid.
- Narrative: ideas/emotions become concrete action or object.
- Cinematic: hardening of light, fixed camera after movement, object that was fluid becomes solid prop.
- Blocking: previously fluid movement becomes fixed formation.

### Sublimation / Direct Transformation (solid to vapor without liquid)
- Physical: elevation without intermediate state.
- Narrative: leap in consciousness or status without gradual process.
- Cinematic: jump cut or match cut that elevates, light from below, removal of ground.
- Risk: feels unearned if not prepared by prior mutation.

### Putrefaction / Blackening
- Physical: death and decomposition.
- Narrative: dark night of the soul, total loss.
- Cinematic: darkness, silence, decomposition imagery, negative space dominance.
- Must lead to later stage or remain as residue.

### Fixation / Stabilization
- Physical: making the volatile permanent.
- Narrative: new state locked in by action or vow.
- Cinematic: final stable composition, locked camera, sound that settles.

### Circulation / Repetition with Refinement
- See REPEAT with mutation. Each cycle refines.
- Cinematic: repeated camera move with progressive change.

### Multiplication / Expansion of Essence
- Physical: small amount produces large effect.
- Narrative: single choice or motif scales to transform whole world/relationship.
- Cinematic: motif starts small, then fills frame, sound grows.

### Projection / Externalization of Inner State
- Physical: inner onto outer.
- Narrative: psychological state becomes environmental fact.
- Cinematic: weather, architecture, light that matches inner state but is not explained as such.

### Initiation / Death and Rebirth
- See INITIATE and SACRIFICE.
- Full cycle: descent, death of old, return with difference.

### Contamination / Purification Cycle
- See CONTAMINATE.
- Repeated contamination and attempted purification drives the arc.

### Fragmentation / Integration
- Break apart then reassemble (dismember/recombine).
- Cinematic: rapid cutting to fragments, then slow assembly of new whole.

### Descent / Return
- Katabasis structure. Down into underworld/unknown, return changed.
- Cinematic: downward camera or frame, dark to light return with mutation.

**Usage Rule**: Never name the alchemical stage in dialogue. Use the process to structure observed behavior, space, sound, and editing. Record which stage governs each major beat in the symbolic_architecture.

**Source Note**: Grounded in historical alchemy (e.g., *Rosarium Philosophorum*, Jung's *Psychology and Alchemy*) but translated strictly into observable dramatic and cinematic operations. Not literal chemistry.
