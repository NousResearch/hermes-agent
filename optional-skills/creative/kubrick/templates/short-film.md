# Short Film Development Starter

## Constraints Emphasis
- One dominant dramatic movement
- Enter late, exit changed
- Visual economy
- Resonant ending (earned transformation or ironic reversal)

## Typical Flow
Intake (tight premise) → Logline → Character (1-2 deep) → 3-5 beat structure → Scene contracts (very few) → Draft with strong visual anchors.

Avoid worldbuilding or subplot proliferation.
