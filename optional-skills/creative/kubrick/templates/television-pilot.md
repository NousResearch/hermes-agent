# TV Pilot Development Starter

## Distinctions
- Pilot story (complete arc in episode)
- Series engine (repeatable conflict + stakes)
- Act outs (usually 4-5 + teaser)
- Ensemble introduction + relationships
- World + rules introduced clearly but economically
- "Season promise" + episode potential

## Key Questions
- What makes this episode satisfying on its own?
- What repeatable engine will sustain a season?
- What is the series-long thematic question?
