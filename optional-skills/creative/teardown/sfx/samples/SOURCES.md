# Recorded samples

Real recordings for what synthesis does badly: keyboards, room tone, a mouth pop, a tape eject.
All 12 are CC0 1.0. Each Freesound page was checked for the CC0 licence on 2026-10-18.
Taken from the `sound-effects` skill of github.com/Barty-Bart/motion-graphics (MIT), which had already trimmed them, normalised them (one-shots to -3 dBFS peak, ambience to -35 LUFS) and encoded them as OGG.

Load one in a cue sheet with `sample("<file>")` from `sfx.synth`.
