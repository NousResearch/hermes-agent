# Budgeting-method fitting (Petasos)

Recipe for answering "does our spending match method X?" — plus one-line fit verdicts per method for a household with stable income, hard-deadline goals, seasonal one-off spend, and an automated ledger. Measure every percentage from the ledger at analysis time; never store household figures here.

## Mapping recipe (any %-based method)

1. Base = monthly INCOME averaged over the same window as the spend audit (salary + rental rows; `oneoff` excluded).
2. Category → bucket mapping in code, with these rules:
   - Debt minimum payments (mortgage instalment) → needs/NEC — they are obligations, not lifestyle.
   - PLANNED flows not yet in the ledger (DCA, settlement-fund contribution) → savings/FFA — the method judges the plan, and omitting them undercounts savings by the plan's size.
   - Mixed buckets split by desc keywords: CC-statement rows split groceries(needs)/dining-out(wants) by a stated assumption (e.g. 45/55) — state it, don't hide it.
   - Gifts/ceremonies → GIVE-class bucket verbatim; do not reclassify as discretionary.
3. Compare bucket % vs the method's targets, in a one-screen table, then give the VERDICT on the operating model — most goal-ledger households come out "right spirit, different letter": savings far above target, entertainment above, education below. That is a diagnostic, not a failure.
4. Prescribe rules, not frameworks: a cap (PLAY ≤ ~10% of income), a floor (EDU ~10% once instalments end), an envelope for seasonal GIVE. Switching the whole household onto %-bookkeeping when a goal system already runs is a regression.

## Method fit table (stable income + deadline goals + seasonal one-offs)

| Method | Assumes | Fit verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Pay Yourself First (auto) | Savings automated on payday | Matches the operating model (contributions before flex); the only upgrade is standing orders so transfers are bank-automatic, not agent-remembered |
| 6 Jars (55/<date>/<date>/5) | Percent bookkeeping per transaction | Good diagnostic lens; too much counting to run as the system — borrow its PLAY cap and EDU floor |
| 50/30/20 | Simple needs/wants/save split | No slot for large deadline debt or deposit-vs-loan rate arbitrage; rejects behavior that is correct for a household running either |
| Barefoot (Blow/Mojo/Grow/Smile) | Buckets + EF in months of spend | Closest bucket shape: Mojo=EF (size on committed outflow), Grow=settlement fund; adds the deliberate-splurge bucket most goal-ledger households lack |
| Zero-based budgeting | Every dong assigned monthly | Use episodically for one-off-heavy months (weddings, Tết) and envelope funding, not as the standing system |
| Ramsey Baby Steps | Debt-averse, cash-only | Conflicts with deliberate deposit-vs-loan rate arbitrage; reject the arbitrage-breaking parts where that trade is intentional |
| Kakebo | Manual ledger + monthly reflection | Redundant with the automated ledger; reflection habit already lives in Monthly Review |

## Standing warnings

- All-in spend as the EF base overstates the target by the one-off average — see SKILL.md EF sizing (committed outflow only).
- A method's savings % target judged against a household mid-payoff plan reads as "oversaving" — that IS the plan working; do not recommend dialing contributions down to hit a textbook band.
