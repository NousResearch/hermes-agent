## 1. Core Framing

Category 07 is the cleanest **service-first vertical wedge** in the Isenberg list.

Local and SMB service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, dental, clinics, salons, restaurants, home services, real estate) systematically miss high-value calls — especially after hours. An AI voice agent that answers, qualifies, and **books the job** into a real calendar produces an externally checkable outcome (booked slot + confirmation) and recovers revenue that previously went to voicemail or competitors.

**Blocked transition:**  
Missed / delayed human answer → 24/7 voice agent that completes a bookable action with measurable attribution.

**Signal classes:** economic friction · repeated workarounds · technology frontier

---

## 2. Market Reality (OBSERVED)

- Missed-call rates for SMBs commonly cited in the 35–62% range during business hours; substantially higher after hours (~90% in some home-services reports).
- Speed-to-answer is a primary hiring factor for many homeowners and local customers.
- After-hours emergency or urgent calls in home services often carry $300–600+ job value at risk per unanswered call.
- AI voice agents in 2026 routinely answer in <1 s, hold multi-turn conversation at ~600 ms response latency, and write back to calendars/CRMs via function calling.
- Pricing has crossed the economic threshold: ~$0.07–0.30/min usage or $25–299/mo flat tiers — far below a human receptionist ($28–35k+/yr fully loaded).

## 4. Completion Proof (Strong)

Externally checkable outcomes already exist:

- Optional deposit or prepayment link under policy (ties to #02)

This makes #07 unusually clean for opportunity-mining gates relative to more abstract agent surfaces.

---

## 5. Remaining Whitespace / Gaps

1. **Deep vertical packaging** — domain scripts, parts/deposit handling, and technician dispatch logic for specific trades (not generic receptionist).
2. **Scoped spend integration (#02)** — agent can take a deposit or authorize a small parts order within policy limits.
3. **True after-hours emergency triage** — severity routing + human escalation with full context handoff.
4. **Multi-location / multi-tech dispatch** that respects real availability, travel time, and skills.
5. **Audit trail + decision logging** that survives “the agent said…” disputes.
6. **Agent-readable legibility** for discovery by donor/consumer agents (Agentic Giving / local service discovery).

Competitive density is rising at the horizontal layer; differentiation is shifting to vertical depth, completion quality, and integration with spend/governance controls.
