# Fragmentation Opportunity Profile

Search where the world works but does not work together.

## Triggers

many portals, repeated handoffs, incompatible systems, coordination problem, integration tax.

## Fragmentation classes

authority, identity, credential, payment, data, workflow, interface, standards, ownership, temporal, geographic, market, compliance, attention.

## Runes

- `RUNE.NG.FRAGMENT.SCAN`
- `RUNE.NG.FRAGMENT.MEASURE`
- `RUNE.NG.FRICTION.LOCATE`
- `RUNE.NG.DEFRAG.SHAPE`
- `RUNE.NG.DEFRAG.SCORE`
- `RUNE.NG.DEFRAG.ROUTE`

## Measure

For each friction site: frequency, severity, who pays the tax, who holds authority to fix, data/API access required, reversibility of a defrag layer.

## Defragmentation archetypes

universal interface, entitlement router, identity portability layer, payment orchestrator, status unifier, translation layer, exception broker, optimizer, compliance compiler, fragmented-market aggregator, portable physical key.

## Hard reject

Reject concepts where **integration burden exceeds capturable value**, or where critical integration access is unknown (Gate K).

## CLEAR rules

- Do not propose a platform “because everything is fragmented” without a measured coordination gap.
- Classify wrappers vs silent subsystem duplicates (Gate M).
