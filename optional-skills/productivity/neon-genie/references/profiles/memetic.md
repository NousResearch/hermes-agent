# Memetic Pressure Profile

Neon Genie exposes language candidates; Level 99 remains the public-language execution authority when that system is present. Absence of Level 99 does not block local candidate generation.

## Triggers

name, hook, pitch language, public framing, shareability, brand compression.

## Runes

- `RUNE.NG.MEMETIC.HOOK`
- `RUNE.NG.MEMETIC.NAME`
- `RUNE.NG.MEMETIC.PRESSURE_TEST`
- `RUNE.NG.MEMETIC.ROUTE_LEVEL99`

## Measures

name stickiness, hook strength, compression, shareability, absurdity advantage, audience fit, hype drift risk, and **claim defensibility**.

## Hard rule (Gate D)

Memetic strength must **never** increase promotion readiness when evidence or feasibility gates fail. A brilliant name cannot promote a `NOT_COMPUTABLE` or gate-failed concept.

## Pressure test

For each candidate: what claim it implies, whether that claim is `OBSERVED`/`INFERRED`/`SPECULATIVE`, audience misfit risks, and hype-drift failure modes.

## Outputs

- `MemeticPressurePacket`
- `NeonGenieRunReceipt`

Schema: `schemas/memetic-pressure-packet.schema.json`

## CLEAR rules

- Reject candidates that force untrue claims.
- Route approved public language downstream; Neon does not publish.
