# LinkedIn Anti-Slop Corrections

## Failure Mode Example
A draft using "not X, but Y" construction was flagged by the critic as HF-1 (binary reversal formula). The draft was discarded and rewritten from scratch with a direct specific observation as the lead.

## Corrected Behavior
Instead of "It's not about X, it's about Y", the corrected draft leads with: "I spent four hours this week watching someone export SCORM packages from a legacy LMS. The workflow had seven steps. That's before they even uploaded the content to the new platform."

## Key Insight
The pattern "not X, but Y" trains the reader to expect a sales pivot. A concrete observation trains them to pay attention. Always prefer the latter.
