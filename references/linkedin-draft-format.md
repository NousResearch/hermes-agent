# LinkedIn Post Draft Format

## Required Fields
- Topic
- Channel: Tyler personal / Built LMS company / both (different angles)
- Strategic purpose
- Target audience/wedge: customer education / partner enablement / internal/corporate / cross-wedge
- CTA type: none / soft discovery / follow Built LMS / visit site / early access / product update
- Draft body

## Length Guidelines
- Under 1,300 characters
- 500-900 characters preferred
- 0-2 hashtags max (0 is fine)
- Natural 2-4 sentence paragraphs

## Banned
- "not X, but Y" or "not just X, but Y"
- One-sentence paragraph stacking
- SCORM as content topic
- Generic openers
- Engagement-bait endings
- Product-internal engineering
- Banned words: revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changing, disruptive
