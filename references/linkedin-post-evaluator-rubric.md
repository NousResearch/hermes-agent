# LinkedIn Post Evaluator Rubric

## Dimensions
1. **Usefulness** — Does this give the reader something specific they didn't already have?
2. **Specificity** — Is the claim concrete, grounded in a real workflow/tradeoff/observation?
3. **Factual Support** — Is the assertion defensible from vault research or real discovery?
4. **Zero Fluff** — No filler, no generic warnings, no padding.

## Score Levels
- **FAIL** — Violates minimum bar.
- **WEAK** — Barely passes, needs significant work.
- **PASS** — Acceptable, minor nits.
- **STRONG** — Excellent, no changes needed.

## Overall Verdict
A single FAIL in any dimension produces overall FAIL for the post.
