# Middle East & North Africa (Gulf, Levant, Egypt, Maghreb, Türkiye)

Schema: Snapshot / Communication / Trust / Decisions / Negotiation & contracts / Etiquette / Taboos / Variation / Field moves.

## Shared pattern

Relationship-first, high-context, hierarchical, affective (emotion is legitimate in business), flexible time, and bargaining as heritage: Arab trading cultures are among the world's most skilled negotiators (Commisceo; Global Negotiator). GLOBE Middle East cluster: participative leadership ranked LOWEST of all clusters — authority is personal and paternal; self-protective + humane-oriented styles endorsed.

## The concepts that carry the region

- **Wasta (واسطة)** — mediation through personal connections: a normalized trust-shortcut where institutions are slow, neither shameful nor fringe (documented: York St John "Guanxi and Wasta"; Northeastern interview with Alnajadah & Khakhar). Access, permits, introductions, and sometimes decisions route through who knows whom. Favors borrowed must be returned; a favor asked of you should be honored or visibly attempted.
- **Majlis (مجلس)** — the traditional sitting room (Gulf): the informal venue where relationships and real decisions happen, before/beside the boardroom. An unhurried coffee or long lunch invitation IS the meeting.
- **Insha'Allah (إن شاء الله)** — "God willing": tone and context decide whether it means yes, maybe, or a polite no (Khakhar: in Saudi often literal; in Lebanon can mean "you'll be lucky" or "give me more"). Read the follow-through, not the phrase.

## Communication & trust

- Indirection preserves dignity: disagreement is signaled by digression, deferral, or alternatives — never flat contradiction in the group.
- Trust: personal, familial, tribal. Your counterpart wants to know you as a person: family, origins, food, football. Multiple teas, meals, hospital visits. First-name warmth once established, but titles always until then.
- Face and honor: never cause public embarrassment; never criticize anyone in front of others; hardball pressure tactics are counterproductive and remembered.

## Decisions

- Slow, concentrated at the top, often the patriarch/owner/minister; western-style "empowered delegate" is rare — the person across the table usually must sell the deal upward privately. Ramadan and prayer times (Salat, five daily) shape every calendar; Friday is the holy day (Fri–Sat or Sat–Sun weekends by country).

## Negotiation & contracts

- Expect hospitality, then endurance bargaining: high openings, slow concessions, dramatic pauses, last-round reopening. The relationship expectation: contracts express intent of parties who trust each other; the guarantee is the relationship, not litigation (Weir's diwan analysis). Insisting on every contingency in writing can read as distrust — balance with relationship maintenance and practical enforcement (escrow, milestones, local partner).

## Etiquette

- Right hand for giving/receiving/eating; stand when an elder enters; coffee/tea ritual (accept at least one cup; tap cup or shake side-to-side to decline more in Gulf); dress modestly and well; inquire after health/family (general, not specific-female); gifts: quality, not lavish (cash never); shoes off when indicated; expect personal questions — warm, general answers reciprocate.

## Taboos

- Alcohol/pork offered or assumed with Muslim counterparts; discussing women in the counterpart's family specifically; Israel/Palestine, regional politics, religion criticism; public anger; bottoms-up pointing of shoes; asking about wasta directly ("through whom?") rather than discovering it; photographing people/government buildings without consent; left-hand exchanges.

## Variation

- **Gulf (KSA, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait):** wasta/majlis strongest; UAE most fast/global hybrid. **Levant (Lebanon, Jordan, Syria):** more indirect pragmatism; French/English layered. **Egypt:** bureaucratic patience + warm formality. **Maghreb (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia):** French-influenced formality, Franco-Arab bicultural manners. **Türkiye:** bridging Europe/Middle East — relationship + hospitality, more direct than Gulf, strong bargaining; tea (çay) culture. **Israel (regional neighbor, distinct file):** direct, informal, fast, debate-welcoming ("dugri" straight talk), chutzpah-valued — closer to a US/Israel hybrid; Friday afternoon–Saturday offline (Shabbat).

## Field moves

1. Accept the long coffee/meal; do not open with agenda — let the host signal business time.
2. Find the wasta path: a respected mutual who introduces you is worth any pitch deck.
3. Bargain with patience and warmth; concede slowly, conditionally, and visibly — generosity of spirit is scored.
4. Read insha'Allah + follow-through; convert soft agreements into private follow-ups.
5. Plan around Ramadan (shorter hours, no food/water in public daylight, evening energy), prayer times, and Thu/Fri–Sat weekends per country (verify with `web_search` — dates shift).
