# Audit & Measurement

GEO's honest core technique is the **manual prompt-audit** — it's free, it's ground truth, and it's
how you both diagnose and measure. The agent can't see inside the models or pull live citation data,
so this is how you work from reality instead of guesses.

> Re-verify tool names quarterly (the GEO-tooling market is young and churning).

## The manual prompt-audit (the method)

This is the most revealing GEO activity and it costs nothing:

1. **List your 10–30 most important queries** — especially **bottom-of-funnel, buyer-intent** prompts
   ("best social scheduler for solopreneurs", "Buffer vs [you]", "how to schedule posts for free").
   Use the **query fan-out** idea: include the sub-questions an engine would break your topic into.
2. **Ask them across engines** — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude (and Copilot/Grok if relevant),
   in **fresh/incognito** sessions to avoid personalization.
3. **Document for each:** Does your brand appear? **How is it described** (accurate? positive?)? **Which
   sources are cited** in the answer (Reddit threads? a competitor's blog? a listicle? YouTube?)? Who
   are the competitors that show up?
4. **Read the cited sources** — they tell you *exactly* where to earn mentions (if a Reddit thread or a
   "best X" listicle is being cited and you're absent, that's your target).
5. **Re-run monthly, track the delta.** GEO has a **multi-week lag** (often ~4–8 weeks between effort
   and appearing) and compounds over **3–12 months** — so measure trends, not single snapshots.

The audit doubles as strategy: the **sources cited** become your Lever-2 target list, and the **gaps**
(questions where you're absent or mis-described) become your content list.

## Tools (optional supplements)

A fast-maturing category of GEO/AI-visibility trackers automates the audit at scale — citation
frequency, "share of voice" vs competitors, sentiment, and alerts when competitors displace you. Names
in market in 2026 include Profound, Otterly, Scrunch, Peec, Semrush's AI-visibility module, and Ahrefs
Brand Radar, among others. Useful at scale, but **the manual audit already gives you ground truth** —
don't wait on a tool to start.

## Referral traffic

Perplexity and ChatGPT send some click-through traffic. In analytics (e.g. GA4) you can **identify
"chatgpt" / "perplexity" as referrers** to see AI-driven sessions — a partial but real signal, and
those visitors often convert well.

## Honesty rules (do not violate)

- **Never fabricate a "share of voice" percentage or citation frequency.** The agent cannot see inside
  the models or pull live citation data. Give the user the **method** (run the audit / use a tool /
  check referrals); report only what an actual audit or tool returns.
- **Set probabilistic expectations.** GEO **influences the odds** of being cited; it doesn't guarantee
  it. Engines change, citations decay, and results lag.
- **No WoopSocial analytics.** WoopSocial publishes social content; it has **no AI-visibility surface.**
  All measurement here is the manual audit + third-party tools + referral analytics.

## What to track over time

Citation presence on your priority queries (yes/no per engine), how you're **described** (sentiment),
which **sources** carry you, competitor presence, and AI **referral traffic/conversions** — reviewed
monthly, judged over quarters.
