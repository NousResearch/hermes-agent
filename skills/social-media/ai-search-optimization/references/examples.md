# Examples — GEO in practice

Worked examples showing the audit → levers → measurement flow, plus honest scope. Subject: a solo
founder building a SaaS social scheduler. Voice context is the blunt indie-founder voice; your output
uses the loaded `voice.md`, `brand-profile.md`, `audience.md`.

---

## Example 1 — the audit (always start here)

**Queries (buyer-intent + fan-out):** "best social media scheduler for solopreneurs," "free social
scheduler," "[competitor] alternative," "how to schedule a week of posts."
**Run across** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini (incognito). **Findings (illustrative — from an actual run,
not invented):** competitor named in 3/4; you're absent; **cited sources** = a Reddit r/socialmedia
thread, a "7 best schedulers 2026" listicle, and a YouTube review. Perplexity leaned on Reddit.
**Read-out:** the citation sources *are* the to-do list → get authentically into that Reddit
conversation, earn listicle inclusion, get a YouTube review. Re-run monthly.

## Example 2 — the Reddit play (authentic)

The audit shows Perplexity citing Reddit. **Play:** the founder genuinely participates in the
buyer-intent threads ("what scheduler do you use?") with specific, helpful, experience-based answers —
disclosing they built one where relevant, not spamming. Over time those threads (and the brand
mention in them) become citable. **Not:** ten sockpuppets posting "try [product]!" — that's
astroturfing (detectable, removed, trust-destroying).

## Example 3 — the YouTube play

Brand mentions in **video titles + transcripts** are a top AI-Overview correlate. **Play:** publish a
video titled "How to Schedule a Week of Social Posts in 20 Minutes (Free)" — say the brand + the
category keywords in the script (transcript), caption it. Now it can surface in AI Overviews *and* in
YouTube/Google search. → `reels-script`, `social-seo`, `tiktok-growth`.

## Example 4 — making a page extractable (before → after)

**Before:** a meandering blog post, no headers, the answer buried in paragraph 6.
**After:** opens with a **one-sentence TL;DR answer**, **question-shaped H2s** ("How much does it cost?
", "Is there a free plan?"), a **comparison table**, two **verifiable stats with sources**, a quotable
line, **FAQ schema**, and a **named author + update date.** Same facts — now liftable into an answer.

## Example 5 — entity clarity

The model describes the brand vaguely/wrongly in the audit. **Fix:** make the **one-line description**
identical across the site, social bios, and listings ("the social scheduler for solo founders who hate
scheduling"); claim listings; ensure consistent naming. Now the model has a clean entity to recommend
for the category.

## Honest scope (say this)

- **Probabilistic, not guaranteed** — you influence the odds of being cited; engines change and
  citations decay.
- **No invented numbers** — no made-up "share of voice" or citation %; run the audit / a tool.
- **No astroturfing** — authentic mentions and real reviews only; fakes are detectable and backfire.
- **Earned > owned** — most citations aren't your pages; invest in community/earned mentions.
- **Spans the web too** — the full discipline includes your site/blog structure + schema, broader than
  social; pair with `social-seo` for the retrieval foundation.
- **No WoopSocial analytics** — measure via the manual audit + GEO tools + AI referral traffic.

---

## What the examples share

- They **start with the audit** (real findings, not guesses) and let the **cited sources set the
  strategy.**
- They lead with **earned/community mentions** (Reddit/YouTube/listicles) where AI actually looks.
- They make content **extractable** and the **entity** clear.
- They stay **authentic** and **honest about measurement**.
