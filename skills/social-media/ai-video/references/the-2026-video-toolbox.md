# The 2026 video toolbox — by job, not by hype

*Highly volatile. Re-verify quarterly — the text-to-video leaderboard reshuffles roughly weekly
and any rank claim has a half-life of months. Test on YOUR prompts, never vendor demo reels.*

**The router's first move is to name the JOB, then pick the tool. There is no single best AI
video tool — only a best tool per job.** Each tool below maps to a tool skill.

## Job 1 — Generative B-roll / scenes (text- or image-to-video) → skills: veo-3, kling, luma
- **Google Veo 3.1** — best all-rounder; strongest prompt adherence; native synchronized audio
  incl. **dialogue with lip-sync**; 720p–4K, vertical + landscape; **8s per generation** (4/6/8),
  extendable/stitchable to ~2.5 min. Tiers Lite/Fast/Quality; ~$0.03–0.50/sec. *Pick for all-round
  quality and any talking/scripted scene.* → **veo-3**
- **Kling 3.0** — best **value + iteration**; native 4K/60fps; multi-shot storyboards (6 cuts,
  ~15s); Motion Control (motion transfer); multilingual lip-sync (guide-track quality);
  ~$0.07–0.11/sec. *Pick for 4K social at scale, multi-shot, motion.* → **kling**
- **Luma Ray3 / Ray3.14** (Dream Machine) — first 16-bit **HDR**; atmospheric/nature/mood; Ray3
  Modify restyles real footage; **no native audio** (sound in post). *Pick for HDR finishing and
  calm, environment-heavy shots.* → **luma**
- **Runway Gen-4.5** (→ `runway`) — best **control surface** (motion brush, camera moves, reference character
  consistency); the ads/client-deliverable pick; 2–10s per generation. *Led the benchmark at launch, since
  displaced — control, not raw rank, is why you'd choose it.* (Tool only — no skill yet; route via
  this router.)
- **MiniMax Hailuo 2.3** — fast (~2.5×) and cheap; good for ideation/volume. **Legal caution:** an
  active copyright lawsuit (Reuters, May 2026) — review rights/resemblance before commercial use.
- Context (don't over-route): Seedance 2.0 (top of benchmark but access-limited), Pika 2.5 (fast
  social effects, ~10s, lower realism), Gemini Omni (auto multi-shot), Wan/LTX/Hunyuan (open-weight,
  self-host). **Multi-model hubs** (Higgsfield, fal, Krea, Hedra, Leonardo) give one workflow across
  many models — useful for staying vendor-agnostic.

## Job 2 — Talking-head / avatar / faceless presenter → skills: heygen, synthesia
- **HeyGen** (creator/social lane: digital twins, 175+-language translation, personalized at
  scale) → **heygen**. **Synthesia** (enterprise/L&D lane: 240+ consented stock avatars,
  training/localization, SCORM) → **synthesia**. **D-ID** for real-time interactive avatars.
  *The #1 faceless format; a different job from generative B-roll.* A **real human on camera**
  (founder/trust content) is not an avatar job → **talking-head-and-piece-to-camera**.

## Job 3 — Voiceover / narration → skill: ai-voiceover
- **ElevenLabs** — the leader for natural multilingual VO. Most generative video tools **need audio
  added in post** (only Veo 3.1 and Kling 2.6+ do native synced audio; Luma is silent), so VO is
  usually a separate step.

## Job 4 — Edit / captions / long→Short clipping → skill: captions-and-clipping
- **Opus Clip, CapCut, Submagic** — turn long-form into Shorts and add sound-off captions (most
  social video is watched on mute). InVideo AI for fully automated assembly.

## Never route here
- **OpenAI Sora — DISCONTINUED** (app shut April 26 2026; API ends September 24 2026). Never list it
  as a buying route or build a new pipeline on it. It is the cautionary tale, not an option.

## Cross-cutting truths the router applies
Most models cap at short clips (Veo 8s/gen, extendable; Kling ~15s; Luma 5–10s; Runway 2–10s) →
**long pieces = stitch clips**, not one take. **Concept-test** on cheap/fast tools (Pika, Hailuo, Luma), **finalize** on quality tools
(Veo, Kling, Runway). AI video is a **production accelerator, not a finished campaign** — always
plan post (trim, color, captions, audio, upscale).
