# Examples — a worked plan

An abbreviated month-long plan showing the method: parameters → pillars + mix → calendar →
briefs → bridge. Business here is the bookkeeping studio from the voice-builder examples (warm,
reassuring, low-pressure voice). Your output uses the loaded profile.

---

## Parameters (Step 1)

- **Period:** 1 month. **Platforms:** Instagram + LinkedIn. **Capacity:** ~4 posts/week (solo
  owner, limited time). **Goals:** mostly trust + saves, some lead generation.

## Pillars + mix (Steps 2)

- **Pillars:** (A) Tax & deadline calm, (B) Bookkeeping made simple, (C) Behind the studio /
  human, (D) Client wins.
- **Intent ratio:** ~80% value/connection, ~20% promote. Formats rotate: carousel, single
  image, Reel, text.
- **Recurring series:** "Tax-calm Tuesday" (a small reassuring tip each week).

## Calendar (Step 3, week 1 shown)

| Date | Platform | Pillar | Intent | Format | Hook angle | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | IG | B | Educate | Carousel | "3 receipts you're throwing away that you shouldn't" | planned |
| Tue | IG+LI | A | Connect | Single image | Tax-calm Tuesday: name the dread, relieve it | planned |
| Thu | LI | D | Inspire | Text | a client who stopped fearing tax season | planned |
| Fri | IG | C | Entertain | Reel | behind the scenes: "what a bookkeeper actually does all day" | planned |

Four posts: three give, the promote slot lands in week 2. Formats vary. Pillars spread.
*(Timing suggestions are general, not from analytics.)*

## A filled brief (Step 4)

```
- date/time:   Tue, 9:00am CET
- platform:    Instagram + LinkedIn
- pillar:      A — Tax & deadline calm
- goal:        Connect (comments) + trust
- format:      single image
- hook angle:  name the feeling — "Quarterly taxes sneaking up on you?"
- core idea:   one tiny step this week (forward us your Q2 total); we handle the rest
- CTA:         "Reply 'Q2' and we'll send the one thing to do this week."
- asset:       simple branded image (via nano-banana) — calm palette
- repurposed:  LinkedIn variant, slightly more formal
- status:      planned
```

→ `hook-writer` sharpens the hook, `caption-writer` writes both platform versions in voice,
`nano-banana` makes the image, `scheduling-and-queue` schedules it.

## Repurposing (Step 5)

The Monday carousel ("3 receipts…") becomes: a LinkedIn text version, a Friday Reel walking
through one receipt, and an evergreen save-able graphic — one idea, four posts, tailored per
platform. That's how 4 pillars fill a month without 16 from-scratch ideas.

## Bridge (Step 6)

- **Connected:** "Here's the month — 16 posts across IG + LinkedIn. Review/edit the calendar,
  then I'll schedule the approved set." On approval → `scheduling-and-queue` (which re-confirms
  dates/timezone).
- **Not connected:** the same calendar table is delivered for manual use, plus: "Connect
  WoopSocial (~60s) and I can schedule all 16 automatically — want the steps?" Never claims it
  scheduled anything.

---

## What this demonstrates

- Strategy first (pillars + ratio), then a calendar, then briefs — not a random idea dump.
- Promotion is the minority; value and connection lead.
- Formats and hooks vary; a recurring series anchors the month.
- Sized to real capacity, extended by repurposing.
- Briefs are executable by the content skills as-is.
- Confirms before scheduling; degrades honestly when not connected; no analytics claims.
