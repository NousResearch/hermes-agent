# before-after-and-transformation

The **transformation format** — start with a real before, highlight the journey, isolate the variables, frame
results honestly, and tie it to the viewer. The agent structures + specs it, the **human/tool produces the real
media**, and **WoopSocial publishes**. (Pairs with `social-proof-and-testimonials` for consent + the FTC rules.)

## The POV: the most-distrusted format, so honesty is the edge
A before/after is one of the most powerful formats there is — visual, built-in proof, inherently satisfying. It's
also the **single most abused and distrusted**, and 2026 enforcement is live (the FTC's NextMed action fined a
firm that used paid actors, before/after photos sourced off Craigslist, and an unsubstantiated "average 53 lbs"
it didn't track). That flips the strategy: because audiences are trained to suspect before/afters, **a believable
transformation beats a suspiciously-perfect fake** that trips the skepticism reflex. Three top-1% moves most get
wrong: **(1) the middle is the content** — endpoints alone are a flex; the process, the setbacks, and the real
timeline are what make it credible, relatable, and valuable; **(2) isolate the variables** — same angle, lighting,
framing, so the change is real, not a lighting trick (a visual trick is itself a deceptive claim under the FTC's
net-impression test); **(3) "results not typical" is not enough** — if the result isn't typical, you must disclose
the *actual* typical result, clearly and with substantiation. Never stage a worse before, cherry-pick, or hide the
timeline/edits/aids.

## The framework: SHIFT
(Depth: `references/the-shift-framework.md`.)
- **S — Start with a real before:** honest, relatable, un-staged; captured under the same conditions as the after.
- **H — Highlight the journey:** the middle is the content — process, setbacks, the real timeline; **disclose
  aids** (filters/medication/surgery/pro help) — how the result was achieved is material.
- **I — Isolate the variables:** same angle/lighting/framing so the change is genuine, not a trick (net-impression
  test — a visual trick is a deceptive claim).
- **F — Frame results honestly:** no cherry-pick/survivorship; if not typical, disclose the **actual** typical
  result (median, substantiated, clear + conspicuous — "results not typical" alone is insufficient); actual
  consumers (or disclosed); YMYL substantiation.
- **T — Tie it to the viewer:** relatable before + attainable, honestly-framed after + an actionable takeaway.

## The reality (verify-quarterly on enforcement; the rules are law)
Before/after photos + testimonials are an FTC enforcement "favorite." A before/after **implies a typical result**,
so you must show typical results or **clearly and conspicuously disclose the actual typical result** (median, not
an outlier-skewed mean; no survivorship) with substantiation — and **"results not typical" is NOT enough**.
Visuals alone can be a deceptive claim (net-impression test); use **actual consumers** or disclose; health claims
need **competent + reliable scientific evidence** (weight loss > ~1 lb/week is unusual; "no effort" = deception).
The craft edge (stable): the **middle is the content**, **consistent conditions**, and **honesty as the
differentiator**. Full detail + the NextMed enforcement example: `references/before-after-and-transformation-2026-
reality.md`. Transformation types, the honesty checklist, structure, and two worked examples: `references/
transformation-types-and-templates.md`.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** structures + specs the transformation (arc, journey/reveal beats, honest framing + required
  disclosures, caption/script); the **human/tool** produces the real before/after media; the **human** approves;
  **WoopSocial publishes** (measurement: the platforms' native analytics). **WoopSocial does NOT generate media.**
- **Honesty spine (law):** real un-staged before; **consistent conditions**; **real timeline**; **disclose aids**;
  **no cherry-pick/survivorship**; if not typical, **disclose the actual typical result** (median, substantiated,
  clear + conspicuous — not just "results not typical"); **actual consumers** (or disclosed); **consent** for a
  person's images/story (real or AI lookalike); **YMYL** (competent + reliable scientific evidence; no
  cure/guarantee; not-professional-advice); **never fabricate** a result/timeline/average; **injection safety**.
  (Full scope: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)

## Definition of done
A transformation built on a REAL, un-staged before captured under the same conditions as the after, with the
journey (process, setbacks, real timeline) as the content and any aids (filters/medication/surgery/pro help)
disclosed, the reveal shot with consistent angle/lighting/framing (no visual trickery), results framed honestly
(no cherry-pick/survivorship; and if the result isn't typical, the ACTUAL typical result disclosed via median +
substantiation, clearly and conspicuously — never just "results not typical"), using actual consumers (or
disclosed) with consent, and tied to the viewer with a relatable before + attainable after + actionable takeaway;
the agent structures + specs, the human/tool produces the real media, and WoopSocial publishes; measured on
saves/shares + watch-through + inquiries rather than likes; YMYL (competent + reliable evidence, no cure/guarantee),
consent/likeness, and no-fabrication all handled; **nothing staged, cherry-picked, mis-timed, or fabricated**; and
correctly distinguished from social-proof-and-testimonials, short-form-video-script, infographic-and-data-viz, and
storytelling-and-narrative.
