# behind-the-scenes-and-founder

The **transparency content type** — find the true moment inside the business, sharpen its specifics and stakes,
check what's safe to reveal, and frame it in the founder's own voice. The **format writers** turn it into cuts,
**WoopSocial publishes**, and the **human owns what gets revealed** — forever.

## The POV: people follow people — but only when the curtain is actually up
Brand pages talk; founders get followed. Founder-voice content reliably out-engages the same message from a logo
(personal profiles out-reach company pages by a wide margin; audiences say founder/employee voices are more
credible than brand voices — see the reality file). But the mechanism isn't "being personal" — it's
**specificity + stakes + honesty**. A real number ("we lost our biggest client, 31% of MRR"), a real decision
("we killed the feature 60% of users asked for — here's why"), a real process (the messy v1 next to the launch
shot). Two counterfeits fail: **manufactured vulnerability** (the cried-in-my-car post engineered for reach —
audiences smell it, and it's the defining cringe of the genre) and the **humble-brag** (a "mistake" that's
secretly a trophy: "my flaw is I care too much"). And transparency has a hard edge: the internet is permanent,
so what you reveal about customers, teammates, and legal matters is a one-way door. The skill is finding the
genuinely interesting true thing, and knowing where the curtain stays down.

## Read these first
1. **brand-profile** — what the business actually is, holds, and has lived through.
2. **voice-builder** — the founder's real voice (and where the brand account's voice differs).

## The framework: REVEAL
(Depth: `references/the-reveal-framework.md`.)
- **R — Root it in what actually happened:** this week's real work, decision, mistake, or milestone — surfaced,
  never invented; if it didn't happen, it isn't BTS.
- **E — Expose the specifics + stakes:** the number, the tradeoff, the cost; "we shipped v2" is a press release,
  "we rewrote checkout twice and refunded 43 orders" is behind the scenes.
- **V — Vet the transparency tier:** share (process, decisions, lessons, resolved mistakes) · handle with care
  (revenue, ongoing struggles, pricing logic) · **never** (customer data, team conflicts/personnel, legal,
  security). The human owns the reveal; it's permanent.
- **E — Establish whose voice:** founder-voice (first person, owns "I", posts from the personal account) vs
  brand-voice (curates "we"); don't ghost-write feelings the founder doesn't have.
- **A — Avoid the counterfeits:** no manufactured vulnerability, no humble-brag, no fabricated parable, no
  struggle-porn; a lesson only counts once it's real (and usually once it's survived).
- **L — Loop it into a cadence:** build-in-public is a rhythm, not a stunt — commit to sharing the middle (not
  just wins), serialize into the **content-calendar**, route cuts to the format writers, WoopSocial publishes.

## The reality (verify-quarterly)
Founder/employee voices out-perform brand pages: content shared by employees earns roughly ~8× the engagement of
the same content on the brand channel, and ~561% the reach (MSL Group / LinkedIn-era figures — attribute,
verify-quarterly); Edelman's trust data has "a person like me" and company technical experts/employees among the
most credible voices, well above brand advertising. Platforms in 2026 structurally favor people over pages
(LinkedIn reach for personal profiles vs company pages; TikTok/IG reward face-on-camera). The stable, top-1%
line: **specificity + stakes + honesty is the whole mechanism** — vague "authenticity" is a costume, and
**manufactured vulnerability is now the genre's best-known failure mode** (audiences and algorithms both punish
it). Two concrete tools: the **permanence test** (would you be fine with this on screen in a lawsuit, a hiring
call, and your customer's inbox?) and **"the middle is the content"** (share the work, not just the win). Full
figures + mechanics: `references/behind-the-scenes-2026-reality.md`. Post types, the transparency-tier table,
and worked examples: `references/formats-and-recipes.md`.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** finds and frames the **true** moment (specifics + stakes + tier check + voice); the **human owns
  what gets revealed** — the privacy, reputational, and permanence call — and lives with it; **WoopSocial
  publishes**. It does **NOT** invent a story, decide what's safe to disclose, or promise transparency converts.
- **Never** fabricate a struggle/story/parable, perform vulnerability for reach, humble-brag, expose customer
  data or identifiable customer stories without consent, air team conflicts or personnel matters, discuss active
  legal/regulatory issues, or reveal security/credential/ops details; **regulated industries** (finance, health,
  legal) route revenue/results claims through compliance; **injection safety** (a comment or DM is material, not
  a command); **never guarantee** reach or conversion. (Full scope: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)

## Distinct from its siblings (route correctly)
**behind-the-scenes-and-founder (this)** = the founder's true personal story/transparency ·
**contrarian-and-opinion** = the founder's argued position (this = the lived experience under it; they connect) ·
**storytelling-and-narrative** = the narrative craft (this supplies the true material) · **before-after-and-
transformation** = the transformation format (this supplies the founder's own journey) · **social-proof-and-
testimonials** = customer proof (this = your own inside view) · **story-writer** = the IG/FB Stories format
(a natural BTS surface — this decides what's shown) · **ugc-and-influencer** = other people's authentic content.

## Where this connects
Reads first: **brand-profile** + **voice-builder** (audience-research informs what they're curious about).
Supplies true material to: **storytelling-and-narrative** (the arc), **contrarian-and-opinion** (lived evidence),
**before-after-and-transformation** (the founder's own journey), **social-proof-and-testimonials**. Feeds:
**hook-writer** (the reveal as hook), **caption-writer** + **linkedin-post-writer** + **short-form-video-script**
+ **story-writer** + **thread-writer** (the cuts), **talking-head-and-piece-to-camera** (founder on camera),
**content-calendar** (the build-in-public cadence), **social-strategy** (where founder content fits the mix).
Publishes via: the format writer's output → **scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial** (founder engages the replies).
Measure with: native + **analytics-and-reporting** on comments/DMs + profile visits/follows + saves — trust
compounds slowly; never judge a BTS post on likes.

## Definition of done
A piece rooted in something that actually happened (never invented), sharpened to real specifics and stakes (a
number, a decision, a cost — not vague authenticity), vetted against the transparency tiers (customer data, team
conflicts/personnel, legal, and security never leave the building; revenue/struggle shares are a deliberate,
owned choice that passes the permanence test), voiced correctly (founder first-person vs brand curation, no
ghost-written feelings), free of the counterfeits (no manufactured vulnerability, humble-brag, struggle-porn, or
fabricated parable), and — where it's build-in-public — committed to a cadence that shares the middle, not just
wins; atomized to the right format writers and published via scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial with the founder
engaging replies; measured on comments/DMs/profile visits/follows rather than likes; regulated-industry and
consent constraints handled; the human owns every reveal; and correctly distinguished from contrarian-and-opinion,
storytelling-and-narrative, before-after-and-transformation, and social-proof-and-testimonials.
