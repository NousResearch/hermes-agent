# Campaigns & launches 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (platform features + launch tactics move).*

## The POV: the spotlight event, told as one story
Your content calendar **keeps the lights on**; a **campaign is the spotlight event** — time-boxed, with
planned phases + dedicated assets toward **one primary goal.** The strongest launches run a **three-part
funnel — pre-launch → launch → post-launch (tease → launch → sustain)** — told as **ONE story**, not three
separate campaigns by different people. The classic failure: **pushing hard in week one, then going quiet
while customers are still deciding.**

## Pre-launch — build anticipation + collect intent
- **Anticipation is the engine** (Apple orchestrates it; the Zeigarnik effect — people fixate on unfinished
  narratives). The goal isn't awareness, it's **emotional investment**: when the audience wants it before it
  exists, **launch day becomes a confirmation, not a cold intro.**
- **Waitlist** = the cleanest mechanism (launches with real pre-launch marketing see **~30% higher day-one
  sales** — Waitlister; vendor, directional, verify-quarterly). Make the promise specific
  ("first access to the beta + a launch-week discount" > "join the waitlist"). A **referral waitlist** adds
  momentum (unique links, reward referrals).
- **One campaign hub:** every profile link (X pin, IG bio, LinkedIn featured, email signature) points to the
  **same** hub (waitlist + offer + referral + proof). Don't make people hunt.
- **BTS/teasers/proof drops** ("people follow launches because they like watching things get built").
  **Beta/early-access group** (50–200 from the waitlist) for validation + proof. **Personalized press/creator
  outreach** (10–20 relevant, with early access — **not** a mass press release).

## Launch — concentrate conversion behind one CTA
- **Velocity is rewarded:** app-store/social algorithms + press cycles reward **concentrated attention in a
  short window** — a coordinated push across channels in a single morning beats the same activities spread
  over a week. (Threads hit 100M in 5 days via coordinated activation — the principle scales down.)
- **One CTA, remove friction** — landing page, ad copy, email, checkout/signup all point to **one action**;
  don't split traffic across offers.
- **Credible urgency only:** artificial/resetting countdowns **erode trust**. Use **real scarcity** (first
  100), **meaningful bonuses** over deep discounts, an honest deadline; "when urgency is real, conversions
  spike — especially the final 24 hours." **Timed drops, countdowns, livestreams** create the peak moment.

## Post-launch — reinforce with proof + sustain
- **This is where teams compound trust or confirm it was noise.** Convert attention into **proof**:
  testimonials, UGC, demos, objection-answers, FAQs. **Sustain** community engagement; mind the gap between
  **"signed up" and "used meaningfully"** (simplify onboarding). Debrief: numbers vs targets, what
  worked/broke, thank early adopters.

## Launch types + orchestration + discipline
- **Soft** (limited/controlled group — lower risk, real feedback) · **Hard** (full audience at once,
  multi-channel + press — needs high confidence) · **Phased** (progressive by segment/geo/use-case — learn
  + adjust).
- **Focus on 1–3 platforms**; **repurpose, don't copy** (a 60s video → 15s Story / 9:16 TikTok / 30s
  LinkedIn cut, each optimized); channels **reinforce** each other; **1–2 hero posts per phase** + evergreen
  support. Run a **parallel email sequence** (teaser → priority-access → launch → objection → onboarding →
  proof), segmented (VIP early access).
- **Discipline over speed:** a **one-page launch brief** to all teams + a readiness meeting (timeline,
  owners, blockers); **map the campaign week-by-week**; readiness checks (QA, support, checkout, analytics).
  "Without a clear timeline, campaigns drift." **Metrics:** day-one conversions, waitlist signups, referral
  behavior, engagement, traffic, **ROI per channel**, numbers vs targets.
