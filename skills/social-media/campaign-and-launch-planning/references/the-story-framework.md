# The STORY framework — run a launch as one story across the phases

The #1 lesson: a launch is **one story across three phases**, not three disconnected campaigns. STORY wraps
that arc with a goal up front and measurement at the end. The agent **plans + writes the posts + the brief**;
**WoopSocial schedules + publishes the coordinated social posts** on the timeline; the **waitlist/hub, email,
ads, checkout, press, and livestream are external/native.**

## S — Set one goal + the brief
- **One primary goal** (the success metric). Pick the **launch type** (soft / hard / phased). **1–3
  platforms.** Write the **one-page launch brief** (what's launching, who it's for, why now, the offer, who
  owns what, the timeline). **Discipline over speed.**

## T — Tease to build demand (pre-launch)
- **Emotional investment** before the offer exists. **Waitlist** (specific promise; pre-launch marketing ≈
  +30% day-one sales — vendor, directional) + **referral.** **One campaign hub** all links point to. **BTS / teasers / proof drops**; a **beta group**
  (50–200); **personalized press/creator outreach** (10–20, not a mass release).

## O — One coordinated launch (launch day)
- **Concentrate the push** across channels in a short window (**velocity is rewarded**). **One CTA**, remove
  friction. **Credible urgency** — real scarcity / meaningful bonus / honest deadline, **never fake/resetting
  timers.** A **timed drop / countdown / live** for the peak.

## R — Reinforce with proof (post-launch)
- **Compound trust:** testimonials, UGC, demos, objection-answers, FAQs. **Sustain** — don't go quiet while
  people are deciding. Close the **signed-up → used** gap (simplify onboarding).

## Y — Yield: measure + debrief
- Read **numbers vs targets per channel** (day-one conversions, signups, referral, ROI). Debrief what
  worked/broke; **feed the next campaign.** Never fabricate results.

## The launch plan a request should fill
```
GOAL: one primary goal + metric; launch type (soft/hard/phased); 1-3 platforms; one-page brief
TEASE: waitlist (specific promise) + referral; one hub; BTS/teasers/proof; beta 50-200; personalized press/creator outreach
LAUNCH: coordinated concentrated push; ONE CTA + remove friction; credible urgency (no fake timers); drop/countdown/live
REINFORCE: proof (testimonials/UGC/demos/objections); sustain; onboarding to first value
YIELD: numbers vs targets per channel; debrief; never fabricate. WoopSocial schedules/publishes the social posts; hub/email/ads/checkout/press/live external
```
