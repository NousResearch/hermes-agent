## What Canva is now (five surfaces, one Brand Kit)
Canva in 2026 spans **five integrated surfaces**: the design editor, **Sheets** (+ Magic Charts/Insights),
**Code** (no-code widgets), **Video 2.0**, and **Affinity** (the pro suite Canva acquired in 2024; relaunched as
one unified free app Oct 2025, AI features behind Pro). All AI lives under **Magic Studio**; the through-line is that it reads
your **Brand Kit** before generating. (per 2026 reviews — eesel, AI Tool Analysis; verify-quarterly)

## The 2026 AI stack (attribute; verify-quarterly)
- **Canva AI 2.0** (Apr 2026, Canva Create): conversational design that outputs **fully editable design
  objects**, not flat images — powered by the Canva Design Model (plus partner models; verify the current mix
  in-app).
- **Magic Layers** (Mar 2026): turns a static AI image into **editable layers**; ~9M uses in its first month;
  public-beta rollout limited to US/UK/CA/AU as of mid-2026.
- **Dream Lab** (Leonardo.ai-powered) images; **Magic Design** layouts; **Magic Write** copy; **Magic
  Eraser/Expand/Grab** photo edits; **Magic Switch** (format transforms + 150+ languages); **Bulk Create**
  (CSV/Excel → variants at scale); **Magic Resize**.
- **The agent can operate Canva**: the **Canva MCP server / AI Connector** works inside Claude, ChatGPT, and
  Copilot (launched for Claude Jul 2025; **Brand-Kit-aware since Jan 2026**); **Connect APIs** now include a GA
  Design Editing API, Resize API, Assets API, and Data Connectors ("Magic Studio at Scale").

## Honest trade-offs (stable + sourced)
- **Dedicated generators beat Dream Lab on pure image quality** (reviews consistently rank Midjourney above it);
  Canva wins on the **integrated, brand-aware workflow** — generate, drop into a template, brand it, resize,
  export without leaving the editor.
- **Magic Write is a second-pass editor, not a first-draft voice** — unedited AI copy is the giveaway; the brand
  voice comes from `voice-builder`.
- Pro designers doing color-managed prepress/broadcast motion still need the Adobe stack; Canva covers the
  80–90% case.

## Plans + credits (sources conflict — reconcile honestly; verify-quarterly)
Free = genuinely usable but tight (limited AI uses, 1 Brand Kit w/ 3 colors). **Pro ≈ $12–15/mo** unlocks the
full Magic Studio, Brand Kit depth, Bulk Create, Magic Resize + the Affinity AI features. Teams/Business adds
brand controls, template locking, approvals (≈$10/seat, 3-seat min; higher tiers bundle Leonardo.Ai + Flourish).
**Credit figures differ across sources** (a flat 500/mo vs tiered 2,000/200/40-style allowances) — don't quote a
number as fact; check the in-app tracker.

## The two facts that drive the framework
- **Brand Kit first:** Canva's own data reports consistent Brand Kit usage correlates with up to ~23% improved
  brand recognition, and practitioners report it's the difference between ~20% and ~80% on-brand *first drafts*
  (attribute both; verify-quarterly).
- **Bulk Create is the most underrated feature** — practitioners call it the biggest time-saver "almost nobody
  uses": one master template + a CSV = hundreds of on-brand variants in one run.
