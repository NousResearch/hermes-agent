# Workflows, checklists & worked examples

## The weekly social-set workflow (repeatable loop)
Brand Kit locked → pick/adapt ONE master layout per format (quote card, carousel, promo) → swap all stock for
brand imagery → Magic Resize to the platform set → QA at thumb-size → export → scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial.
Boring on purpose: the repeatable loop is what makes six platform versions still look like one campaign.

## The Bulk Create pattern (the leverage move)
1. Build ONE master template; mark the variable fields (headline, tip text, price, image).
2. Prepare the CSV/Excel — one row per asset; image URLs if imagery varies.
3. Connect fields → generate all variants in one run.
4. QA pass: text overflow, widows, line breaks, contrast on every variant.
Use for: tip series, product cards, stat cards, event speaker cards, localized sets (pair with Magic Switch).

## The de-templating checklist (kill the "Canva look")
Stock imagery replaced with yours ✓ · layout bones changed (something moved/merged/deleted) ✓ · Brand Kit fonts +
colors applied over defaults ✓ · template decorations stripped ✓ · a stranger can't name the template ✓ ·
consistent with your other posts (the system, not the template, is the consistency) ✓.

## Legibility floor (before any export)
≤2 fonts · WCAG contrast · min readable sizes at thumb-scale · text never over busy imagery without a
panel/overlay · safe zones respected per platform · alt text drafted.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (Bulk Create)
"had 52 pricing myths in a spreadsheet. built one black card template, wired 3 fields, bulk-created all 52 in
about 20 minutes. qa caught 4 overflows. a year of tuesday posts done before lunch. the feature's been sitting
in canva the whole time — i just never clicked it." — one master, CSV, QA pass, honest time math.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (de-templating)
"We loved a template — and that was the problem, because three competitors clearly loved it too. So we kept its
spacing, swapped every photo for our own desk-and-receipts shots, set our serif over the default font, and
deleted the swooshes. Same bones, finally our face. One small step: replace just the photos this week — it does
more than you'd think." — scaffolding kept, look replaced, one small step.

## Never
Skip the Brand Kit and prompt "in my brand colors" every time · ship the untouched template · rip a premium
template from a screenshot · strip stock watermarks / evade licenses · impersonate another brand's look/logo ·
trust a blog's credit numbers over the in-app tracker · let Magic Write's first draft be the voice · claim
WoopSocial generates or edits designs · skip the human review of a visual.
