# Workflows, checklists & worked examples

## The daily short-form loop (repeatable)
Script beats in → rough cut (kill dead air, hook first) → pace pass (change every ~2-4s, beat-sync) → caption
pass (auto + accuracy correction + saved style preset) → sound pass (license-checked) → spec export → WoopSocial.
Save the caption style + intro/outro treatments as templates so the loop compresses to minutes.

## The pre-edit tier check (avoids the export-paywall trap)
Before building the edit: are the effects/transitions/assets you plan to use on your tier? Is your export target
(4K?) on your tier? Is the music from the commercial library and cleared for YOUR destination platform? Checking
for 2 minutes up front beats discovering at export that "99% of the transitions were Pro-only" (a documented
complaint pattern — attribute).

## The client-work hygiene checklist (the ToS gate)
Editing client/NDA/confidential material? → local editing only ✓ · cloud sync/backup OFF for those projects ✓ ·
no client assets in shared templates ✓ · client contract checked against platform-license terms ✓ · high-stakes →
counsel ✓ · or use a licensing-safe editor (Resolve/Premiere) for that work ✓.

## Long-form routing
>~15 min → not CapCut (by design). Talk-content long-form → Descript (text-based editing); cinematic/pro →
Resolve/Premiere. Then bring the master BACK for shorts: Long-Video-to-Shorts or captions-and-clipping /
opus-clip for the cutdowns, CapCut for caption styling + pace.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (the retention edit)
"90 seconds of me rambling became 34 seconds that hold. cut the first 11 seconds (the warm-up nobody needed),
punch-in every time i make a point, captions corrected — it wrote 'in voice' as 'invoice', which for once was
right. music from the commercial library, checked it's cleared for more than tiktok. it wasn't. swapped it.
export, queue, done." — beats-first, accuracy pass, the license trap caught.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (the caption preset)
"Captioning used to take us longer than filming — so we styled it once: our serif, cream on deep green, two lines,
inside the safe zone, saved as a preset. Now every video is auto-caption → a careful read-through (it misspells
'ledger' constantly, bless it) → apply the preset. One small step this week: build your preset once, and
tomorrow-you gets twenty minutes back." — style once, reuse, the gentle accuracy pass.

## Never
Ship uncorrected auto-captions · build the edit before checking asset tiers · assume "Commercial use" music is
cleared beyond TikTok/CapCut · sync client/NDA material to the cloud · use pirated template packs or unlicensed
songs · lip-sync/deepfake a real person without consent · force long-form into a short-form tool · state a CapCut
price or feature gate as fact without verifying in-app · claim WoopSocial edits video.
