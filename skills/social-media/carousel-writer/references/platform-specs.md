# Carousel Platform Specs

Each platform's carousel has its own specs and culture. Match the build to the target.

> Specs change; treat numbers as approximate and re-verify quarterly. The principles are durable.

## Instagram carousel

- **Slides:** up to 20 (raised from 10 in 2024). Mixed images/video allowed. The sweet spot is
  still ~5–10; reserve 15–20 for photo dumps / deep walkthroughs that earn it.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square) or **4:5 (portrait)** — 4:5 takes more feed space and is usually
  better. Keep all slides the same ratio.
- **Cover:** shows in the feed and on the grid — must work at thumbnail size.
- **Caption:** up to ~2,200 chars; the caption pulls people into slide 1 (hand to `caption-writer`).
- **Saves & shares** are the goal signals; design reference-value content.
- **Links:** not clickable → "link in bio."

## LinkedIn document (PDF) carousel

- **Format:** a **PDF uploaded as a document** — renders as a swipeable carousel. A LinkedIn power
  format: very high dwell and saves.
- **Aspect ratio:** square (1:1) or portrait works; design for mobile legibility.
- **Slides:** more generous than IG; still keep it tight and one-idea-per-slide.
- **Post copy:** a short, strong text post pulls people into the PDF (hand to
  `linkedin-post-writer`). Link in the first comment if any.
- Great for frameworks, how-tos, and breakdowns to a professional audience.

## TikTok photo carousel

- **Format:** photo mode (swipeable images), often set to a sound.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical.
- **Style:** more casual/native than IG; can be screenshots, memes, or designed slides.
- **Caption + keywords** matter for search; the cover still hooks.
- **AI-disclosure** auto-handled by WoopSocial on TikTok for AI-generated media.

## Cross-platform note

A carousel can be repurposed across these, but **re-fit per platform** (ratio, slide count, register)
rather than posting the identical file everywhere — see `cross-platform-repurposing`. Design once in
a flexible template, then export per platform's ratio.

## Quick reference

| Platform | Format | Ratio | Slides | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | image/video carousel | 4:5 or 1:1 | up to 20 | saves, teaching, story |
| LinkedIn | PDF document | 1:1 or portrait | generous | frameworks, B2B authority |
| TikTok | photo mode | 9:16 | several | casual, native, reach |
