# Scope, distinctions + connections

collabs-and-cross-promotion is a **strategy/relationship skill** — it picks the partner, plans the
cross-promo, and writes both sides. The agent **advises + drafts + plans**; **WoopSocial publishes each
partner's own posts** on the coordinated schedule; the **co-created content + the relationship are
native/human.**

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** picks a complementary, same-size, values-aligned partner; chooses the format; **drafts the
  outreach**; plans the **cross-promo** (timing/CTA/what-to-say/who-does-what); and **writes each partner's
  posts.**
- **WoopSocial publishes each partner's OWN posts to their OWN accounts** on the agreed schedule, with the
  **mention in the caption.** It does **NOT**: create a **native IG Collab co-authored post** (native-only —
  not in the spec's Instagram fields), **run giveaway mechanics**, **duet/stitch** (native / via the video
  file), **DM to find or pitch partners** (no DM surface), or **manage the agreement.**
- **The co-created/co-authored content** (joint video, live, duet, IG Collab, YouTube's native Collaboration
  invite) and the **relationship** are **native/human.**
- **Disclosure:** a **pure mutual collab (no payment) is NOT a sponsorship** — no #ad. **If money/free
  product changes hands → it's a sponsorship → disclose** → route to `ugc-and-influencer`/
  `creator-monetization`. **Partnerships must be genuine** — no bought/faked engagement (fraud; detectable;
  hurts both). **Never fabricate metrics** — per-source native analytics only. **Verify-quarterly.**

## Distinct from its siblings
- **collabs-and-cross-promotion (this)** — **creator-to-creator MUTUAL** partnership (unpaid; both
  contribute + both promote).
- **ugc-and-influencer** — **brand→creator** (the brand buys an asset/reach; **paid/licensed + disclosed**).
- **creator-monetization** — the **creator's own income** streams (membership/products/sponsorships).
- **social-selling-and-dm** — the **DM outreach mechanics** (could be used to pitch a partner; this skill
  decides who/why, that one runs the conversation).
- **campaign-and-launch-planning** — the **campaign wrapper** a collab may sit inside.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (niche/audience/values), `goals-and-kpis` (the growth goal + success
  metric).
- **Pairs with:** `caption-writer`/`hook-writer` (each side's posts), the **format skills**
  (`youtube-publishing-and-metadata`, `tiktok-video-publishing`, `carousel-writer`, …) for the actual posts,
  `social-selling-and-dm` (the outreach conversation), `lead-magnets-and-funnels`/`email-and-newsletter`
  (newsletter swaps).
- **Publishes via:** `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` (each partner's own posts, coordinated) +
  `platform-specs-and-validation` (validate each post). `content-calendar` slots the ~1–2/month cadence.
- **Measures with:** `analytics-and-reporting` (per-source CTR/subs/retention). **Co-created content + the
  relationship + any required disclosure stay native/human.**
