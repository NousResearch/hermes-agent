# Audit template, math + two worked examples

## The capture template (one row per competitor)
| Field | What to note (public only) |
|---|---|
| Type | direct / aspirational / adjacent |
| Positioning | the one-line promise they make |
| Pillars | 3–5 recurring themes |
| Formats & mix | Reels / carousels / text / video / Stories — rough % |
| Cadence | posts/week per platform |
| Hooks & tone | how they open; voice |
| Top posts (public) | 3–5 high-engagement posts + the likely *why* |
| Engagement rate | est. ER (engagements ÷ followers ÷ posts) — flag as estimate |
| Growth trend | rising / flat / falling (directional) |
| Paid | any ad-library creative running |
| AI/LLM visibility | cited by ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude for category queries? |

## The math (keep it honest)
- **Engagement rate (public est.):** `engagements ÷ followers ÷ posts` over a window. It's an
  **estimate** (you can't see reach/saves on others) — state that.
- **Share of voice:** `your mentions ÷ total mentions across the set` for a topic/keyword.
- **Relative read:** compare each rival to the **set median**, and your trend vs theirs — not to the
  platform average.

## The white-space matrix
Plot **content pillars (rows) × formats or funnel stages (columns)**; mark where each competitor is
strong. **Empty/weak cells the audience wants = your white space.** Also scan for: an unclaimed POV, a
neglected platform, a service/voice gap, an underserved sub-audience.

## Worked example 1 — find the gap (blunt indie-founder voice)
```
FIELD: 6 bookkeeping/finance-tool accounts (3 direct, 2 aspirational, 1 adjacent creator)
WORKS: the set wins on carousels of "tips"; one aspirational account dominates LinkedIn SOV.
WHITE SPACE: nobody does short, dry founder-POV video on the *emotional* side of tax (all how-to, no relief).
TRANSLATE: claim "calm finance" POV; pillar = "the panic, fixed"; format = 15s Reels (route ai-video).
TEST: 4 Reels over 4 weeks; KPI = saves + profile visits vs our carousel baseline. -> social-strategy
```

## Worked example 2 — relative benchmark, not vanity (warm studio voice)
```
QUESTION: "Competitor has 500k followers, we have 12k -- are we losing?"
READ: their est. ER ~0.3% (large but low engagement); ours ~4% on saves. Relative, we over-index.
SOV: we hold ~22% of category conversation on "first-time freelancer tax" -- a niche they ignore.
WHITE SPACE: that exact beginner sub-audience; their content skews advanced.
TRANSLATE: double down on beginner pillar in our voice; don't chase their follower count. -> social-strategy
```

Both: read brand-profile + audience-research first; public signals only; engagement-weighted +
relative; estimates flagged; gap translated into the brand's own voice and tested with KPIs. Feeds
`social-strategy`, `content-pillars`, and `content-recycling` (which proven angles to double down on).
