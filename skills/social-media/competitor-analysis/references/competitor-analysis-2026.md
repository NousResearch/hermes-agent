# Competitor analysis 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (benchmarks and platform norms shift).*

## The point (don't miss it)
The goal is **not to copy** rivals — it's to gather public intelligence and **carve out a space you can
own.** "Who is winning?" matters far less than **"why are they winning, and how do we do better?"**
Output = your *differentiated* strategy, not a carbon copy.

## Who to analyse (roster the right set)
- **Direct** — sell a similar product / target the same audience.
- **Aspirational** — brands you admire or that dominate the space.
- **Adjacent / indirect** — analogous niches competing for the same attention, plus the incumbent
  behaviour or "do nothing" you're displacing.
- **~5–8 profiles** is the benchmarking sweet spot. **Match by industry, audience, and format** — a
  B2B SaaS account vs a consumer-fashion brand teaches you nothing.

## What to track (signals, not vanity)
Benchmarking is **not tallying likes or followers.** Track: **engagement rate**, **share of voice
(SOV)**, **follower-growth trend**, **posting cadence**, **content formats/mix**, **hooks & tone**,
**visual style & CTAs**, **sentiment**, **paid vs organic mix** (via ad libraries), and **how content
maps to funnel stages** (awareness vs conversion). **SWOT** is the standard wrap-up frame.

## Benchmarks (2026 — relative beats absolute; verify-quarterly)
Median **follower-based** engagement rates vary ~25× across platforms: **TikTok ~3.7%**, **Instagram
~0.48%**, **Facebook ~0.15%**, **X ~0.12%** (Socialinsider 2026). So a "2% on X" looks weak but is
~16× the median. **Watch the denominator:** LinkedIn's oft-quoted **~5%** is engagement *per
impression* (LinkedIn's native formula), and Buffer's 2026 benchmarks are *by reach* (LinkedIn ~6%,
Facebook ~5.6%) — impression/reach-based numbers are NOT comparable to follower-based ones. Compute
every account in your set on **one formula**. **The useful benchmark is relative** — your trend vs
your *direct set* over time, not platform-wide averages.

## The AI-era layer (new, important)
Also analyse competitors' **AI/LLM visibility** — how often they're surfaced/cited by ChatGPT,
Gemini, Claude — and their **GEO / AI-search** presence (ties to `ai-search-optimization`). Plus their
**influencer/UGC partnerships** and **paid creative** (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center).

## Pitfalls
Comparing unlike things · ignoring platform context · chasing platform averages instead of the direct
set · vanity metrics over outcome-linked ones · one-off instead of a **monthly/quarterly** loop.

## The hard limit — public data only
Competitor tools and native views surface **public** signals; their data is **shallower than your own
analytics** and partly **estimated.** You **cannot** see a rival's private analytics, revenue, or ROI
— estimate ranges with stated uncertainty; **never fabricate** a competitor metric. No scraping
private/non-public surfaces, no fake/sockpuppet monitoring accounts, no content theft.
