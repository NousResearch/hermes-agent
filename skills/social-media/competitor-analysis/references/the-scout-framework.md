# The SCOUT framework — recon for the gap, not a copy

Most accounts either ignore competitors or mimic them — both lose. SCOUT is **reconnaissance**: read
the public landscape to find the **white space you can own**, then differentiate. Public data only; no
scraping, impersonation, or copying.

## S — Select the field
- Roster **direct + aspirational + adjacent** competitors (~5–8). Include the incumbent behaviour or
  "do nothing" you're displacing.
- **Match by industry, audience, and format** — don't compare unlike things.

## C — Capture public signals
- For each: **positioning/message, content pillars, formats & mix, posting cadence, hooks & tone,
  visual style, CTAs**, and **paid** creative (Meta Ad Library / TikTok Creative Center).
- **Public only.** Capture what any user can see; note where you're inferring vs observing.

## O — Observe what works
- Read **engagement RATE, share of voice, and growth trend** — *not* follower counts. When a post
  spikes, ask **why** (giveaway? influencer share? trend? format?).
- **Benchmark relative** to the direct set over time (platform averages mislead — TikTok ~3.7% vs
  Facebook ~0.15%). Add **AI/LLM visibility** (are they cited by ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude?).

## U — Uncover the white space
- Map the **gap**: topics that earn engagement nobody in the set covers; **formats** they ignore;
  **audiences** underserved; a **POV** unclaimed; a **funnel stage** (strong awareness, weak
  conversion) left open; a **service/voice** gap (rivals formal → you human).
- The win is **different, not louder.**

## T — Translate to your strategy
- Convert the gap into **your pillars, positioning, cadence, and format mix** — staying in *your*
  brand voice (don't adopt theirs). **Test a gap with clear KPIs** before scaling.
- Feed **social-strategy** and **content-pillars**; re-run the loop **monthly/quarterly**.

## The competitor-analysis brief a request should fill
```
FIELD: 5–8 competitors (direct / aspirational / adjacent), matched by industry+audience+format
SIGNALS: positioning, pillars, formats, cadence, hooks, tone, visuals, CTAs, paid (public only)
WORKS: engagement rate + SOV + growth trend (not followers); why the spikes; AI/LLM visibility
WHITE SPACE: the topic/format/audience/POV/funnel/service gap you can own
TRANSLATE: into your pillars/positioning/cadence (your voice); test with KPIs -> social-strategy
```

> Not SCOUT's job: why a *single post* worked → **viral-reverse-engineering**; *your* audience →
> **audience-research**; timely cultural moments → **trend-jacking**; *your* plan → **social-strategy**.
