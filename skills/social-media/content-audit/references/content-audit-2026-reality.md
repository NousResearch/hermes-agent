# Content audit 2026 — verified

*Method is stable; re-verify platform/format specifics quarterly.*

## What it is (and isn't)
A content audit is a **retrospective reality-check.** Where **strategy is forward-looking** (goals,
pillars, schedule), the audit is **retrospective** — it checks how effectively you're *executing* that
plan, then **informs a smarter one.** The trap it breaks: **activity isn't progress.** Plenty of accounts
look busy — daily reels, carousels, likes trickling in, followers inching up — while **nothing actually
moves.** The audit shows what's happening **beneath the surface** (what gets *saved* vs skipped, when the
audience shows up) so you adjust *what* and *when*.

## Scope of THIS skill
The **content + pillars + voice + profile** slice. It **routes out**: rivals → `competitor-analysis`;
the metric definitions/measurement → `analytics-and-reporting`; profile execution → `profile-optimization`.
The audit *reads* native analytics; it doesn't re-derive them.

## Score on signal, not vanity
Judge content on **saves, shares, replies** + the **goal-mapped KPI** — these "signal algorithm-worthy
content far more reliably than impressions or follower counts." A high-*like* post can be low-value, and
a modest-like post can be a quiet winner. Surface **top + bottom performers AND the patterns** (by
format, posting time, pillar).

## The core decision: keep / kill / refresh
- **KEEP / scale** — proven winners and high-leverage formats → double down (→ `content-recycling`).
- **KILL / cut** — chronic underperformers, off-brand posts, dead or cross-posted non-native formats.
- **REFRESH / revive** — good-but-dated or underseen content → update and re-publish.
Platform-native formats (vertical video, carousels) beat cross-posted static — flag cross-posts to
refresh or kill.

## Diagnose pillars, voice & profile
- **Pillar coverage:** map ~90 days of output to your **content pillars**; find **over/under-served**
  pillars and **whitespace** (→ `content-pillars`).
- **Voice drift — both ways:** are posts drifting **off-brand**? Or are well-performing posts a signal the
  **brand voice should evolve** toward what's working? (→ `voice-builder`.)
- **Profile fundamentals (compound, zero spend):** clear bio with keywords, working/strategic links,
  pinned/featured posts showcasing winners, consistent handle/visuals (→ `profile-optimization`).
- **Cadence:** posting frequency by platform — consistent without ghosting or overwhelming.

## Window, frequency, output
Pull **~90 days** (3–6 months) of data. **Deep audit quarterly** (catches drift) + **light monthly**
benchmarks; small brands ~6-monthly; run one after any **major change** (rebrand, platform launch,
campaign, algorithm shift). Output = **strengths / weaknesses / opportunities** + **quick wins vs
long-term**, with **owners + timelines.** Don't let it become a **spreadsheet graveyard** — it must
become an action plan that feeds the strategy, recycling queue, and profile.
