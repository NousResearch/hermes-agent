# Examples — content calendars

Two worked calendars showing cadence, the recurring skeleton, series, and a sample week — plus an
over-ambitious version for contrast. Voice in the sample posts is the blunt indie-founder voice from
earlier skills. Your output uses the loaded `voice.md`, `content-pillars.md`, and `social-strategy.md`.

---

## Example 1 — Solo SaaS founder · 1 primary + 1 secondary platform

**Capacity:** solo, ~4 hrs/week. **Pillars:** A = building in public, B = SaaS lessons, C = the
category POV. **Platforms:** LinkedIn (primary), TikTok (secondary).

**Cadence (sustainable floor):** LinkedIn 3×/week, TikTok 2×/week. (Not daily — held for the long
run beats heroic-then-quit.)

**Recurring skeleton:**

| Slot | Platform | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | LinkedIn | B (lessons) | text post / mini-story |
| Wed | LinkedIn | C (POV) | contrarian post |
| Tue | TikTok | A (build log) | talking-head storytime |
| Fri | LinkedIn | A (build in public) | weekly "Build Log" update |
| Fri | TikTok | open | **reactive/trend slot** |

**Series:** "Build Log" (Fri LinkedIn) — weekly build-in-public update. Anticipation + zero
decision fatigue.
**Planned/reactive:** ~80/20 (the Fri TikTok is the open reactive slot).
**Rhythm:** Mon 30-min weekly fill → `batch-content-plan`; batch-film TikToks every other week;
keep a 1-week buffer in WoopSocial; 10 min/day engaging.

**Sample week (filled):** Mon — "We deleted our most-requested feature. Here's what we learned."
(Pillar B) · Tue — storytime version for TikTok · Wed — "Posting daily is hurting your reach."
(Pillar C) · Fri — Build Log #14 + a TikTok riding a relevant founder trend.

## Example 2 — Small team (2–3) · 3 platforms with repurposing

**Capacity:** founder + a part-time editor. **Platforms:** LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram.

**Cadence:** LinkedIn 4×/week, TikTok 4×/week, Instagram 3×/week — **made feasible by repurposing**,
not by 11 from-scratch pieces.

**Repurposing spine:** each week, **2 core ideas** → each becomes a TikTok + a Reel + a LinkedIn
post + a carousel (via `cross-platform-repurposing`). That's most of the week from 2 origins.

**Series:** "Teardown Tuesday" (TikTok + Reel) — critique one thing weekly. "#FixItFriday"
(carousel) — one mistake + the fix.

**Skeleton (abbreviated):** Mon LinkedIn POV · Tue Teardown (TikTok+Reel) · Wed LinkedIn teaching
+ carousel · Thu TikTok tip · Fri #FixItFriday carousel + LinkedIn build-update · one open reactive
slot.

**Rhythm:** monthly planning (anchors + arcs) → weekly fill → `batch-content-plan`; editor batches
edits Thu; buffer = 1 week; founder engages daily. Roles: founder creates + engages, editor edits +
schedules.

## Contrast — the over-ambitious calendar (do NOT do this)

- **5 posts/day across 6 platforms**, all original, solo founder.
- Every slot pre-locked for 90 days, no reactive room.
- No buffer, no series, no repurposing — pure from-scratch volume.

→ Sustained for ~2 weeks, then abandoned. The fix: cut to 1–2 platforms, a sustainable floor,
repurposing, a series or two, reactive room, and a buffer. A calendar you keep beats a calendar
that impresses on paper.

---

## What the good examples share

- **Cadence sized to capacity** (the floor, not the ceiling).
- A **recurring skeleton** of buckets (pillar × format × slot), not a one-off list.
- **One or two named series** for anticipation + less decision fatigue.
- **Reactive room** (~20–30%) and sensible **anchors**.
- A **planning rhythm + buffer**, and clean hand-offs to `batch-content-plan` and the content skills.
- Multi-platform made feasible by **repurposing**, not heroics. No analytics claims.
