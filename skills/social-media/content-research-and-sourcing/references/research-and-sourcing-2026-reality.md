# The reality of research & sourcing in 2026 (verify-quarterly)

The numbers below are the case FOR verification — attribute them, and **verify-quarterly** (this field moves
fast; the discipline doesn't).

## The 2026 verification crisis (attribute)
- **Generative search is not a source:** Columbia Journalism Review found eight generative search tools gave
  **incorrect answers on more than 60% of tested news-citation queries.** There is no single "hallucination
  rate" — reliability collapses on exactly the tasks content research needs: citation retrieval, multi-turn
  research, niche facts (Suprmind 2026 roundup).
- **Fabricated citations went mainstream:** the Columbia University audit published in The Lancet (May 2026)
  scanned 2.5M biomedical papers and found fake references **surged ~12-fold since 2023** — by early 2026,
  **1 in 277 PubMed-indexed papers** contained at least one fabricated reference (vs 1 in 2,828 in 2023). A
  parallel 111M-reference arXiv audit found the surge beginning mid-2024, spreading into government reports,
  legal filings, and news articles.
- **The craft-critical failure mode — identifier hijacking:** the NeurIPS 2025 fabricated-citation taxonomy
  found 66% of hallucinated citations were total fabrications and **29% carried working DOI links that resolve
  to unrelated real papers.** A clickable link is NOT verification — the source must **exist AND say what's
  claimed.**
- **The corporate cautionary tale:** KPMG's October 2025 agentic-AI report was found to have **only 5 of 45
  citations accurate** (GPTZero investigation via OECD.AI) — reputational damage at consultancy scale.
- **Citogenesis in the wild:** Google's AI Overview cited an April Fool's satire ("microscopic bees powering
  computers") as fact (HKS Misinformation Review) — AI now launders errors into apparent consensus, which
  later AI answers then cite. Stanford HAI: even purpose-built legal AI hallucinated 17–34% on hard queries;
  1,450+ court cases now involve AI hallucinations (Charlotin database). ECRI ranked AI-chatbot misuse the
  **#1 health-technology hazard for 2026.**

## The zombie-stat problem (stable craft knowledge)
Some "facts" survive on repetition alone: the goldfish-attention-span claim (no traceable primary study), the
misapplied Mehrabian "93% of communication is nonverbal" (his studies measured something far narrower), and
decades-stale figures quoted as current. **The tell:** every citation leads to another blog post, never to a
named study with a method. Ubiquity is not evidence — and one debunkable stat can discredit an otherwise
excellent piece, because the comment section checks.

## The strategic upside (why the top 1% over-invest here)
Being the precisely-sourced account in a sloppy niche is a moat twice over: audiences learn whose numbers hold
(trust compounds), and **AI answer engines preferentially cite content with verifiable sourcing** — the GEO
flywheel `data-and-original-research` documents. Citing the primary source most competitors never read is
differentiation at near-zero cost. And where no source exists, the gap is an invitation: commission the data
and become the citation.

## House metrics
Zero corrections/retractions on published claims + the source log's reuse rate + citations earned (who quotes
your numbers) via native signals — never fabricated.
