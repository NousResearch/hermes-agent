# Escalation, Pause the Queue & Safety

The high-stakes operational core: who decides, the one real WoopSocial action in a crisis, and the
rules that keep an AI agent in its lane when things are on fire.

## Human-in-the-loop (non-negotiable)

A crisis is exactly when the agent **must not act autonomously.** The split:

- **The agent:** triages severity, **drafts** holding statements/responses in **approved brand-voice
  language**, suggests moderation actions, helps **pause the queue**, and flags what to escalate.
- **The human** (and, for high-severity, **legal + leadership + PR professionals**): **approves and
  posts** every public word, makes the judgment calls, and does the **in-platform moderation**.
- **Never auto-respond to a crisis. Never fabricate facts. Never apologize for the unverified.** Never
  speak beyond authorized language.

## The approval chain / escalation paths

Decide *before* you need it (and apply in the moment):

- **Low** → social/community handles it with care.
- **Medium** → loop in PR + support; a measured response with approved wording.
- **High** → **escalate to legal review + executive visibility**; use trained spokespeople and
  **pre-approved language**. For legal, safety, regulatory, or serious-harm matters, **involve
  professionals** — this skill is not a substitute for legal counsel.

If unsure whether to escalate, **escalate.** The cost of an extra human review is small; the cost of an
agent freelancing a high-severity response is not.

## Pause the queue (the real WoopSocial action)

This is the one concrete, honest thing WoopSocial does in a crisis. During a crisis **or a sensitive
news moment** (a tragedy, disaster, public mourning), your scheduled promo posts can publish
automatically and make you look tone-deaf. So:

- **Pause / reschedule the scheduled posts** before they fire — via `scheduling-and-queue` (WoopSocial
  can **delete or reschedule** pending posts; there's no in-place edit, so it's delete-and-recreate).
- **Confirm with the human** which posts to hold and for how long (the confirmation contract from
  `scheduling-and-queue`).
- **Resume** deliberately when the moment has passed.

This is genuinely useful and fully within WoopSocial's real capabilities — surface it early in any
sensitive moment.

## What WoopSocial can and can't do here (honest scope)

- **Can:** publish/schedule, and **pause/delete/reschedule your own posts** (the queue).
- **Cannot:** hide or delete others' comments, block users, pull mentions/DMs, monitor, listen, or show
  analytics. There's **no inbox/moderation/listening surface.** Monitoring and moderation happen via
  the **platform's native tools** (and third-party listening tools), done by the **human**.

Never imply WoopSocial monitors mentions, manages an inbox, or moderates.

## Wellbeing (a crisis is stressful)

- **Designate responders and windows** — don't let everyone doomscroll the pile-on; assign who watches
  and who drafts.
- **Protect the people** — a coordinated attack is draining; step back, don't engage every troll, lean
  on the team.
- **Genuine only** — don't fight a pile-on with bought support, sockpuppets, or fake positivity; it
  backfires. Real, calm, factual responses win.
