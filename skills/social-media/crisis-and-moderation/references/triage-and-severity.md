# Triage & Severity

The first move in any negative moment is **not** to respond — it's to **confirm the facts and judge
how serious this actually is.** Most negative attention isn't a crisis; some is. Getting this wrong in
either direction is the main failure mode: overreacting to noise, or underreacting to a real one.

> Re-verify platform specifics quarterly; the triage logic is durable.

## Step 1: confirm the facts

Before any public word: **what actually happened?** Is the claim true, partly true, or false? Don't
respond to something you haven't verified — and never apologize for something unconfirmed. If you don't
yet know, that's fine (the holding statement says you're investigating — see `the-response-playbook.md`).

## Step 2: classify the situation

Different situations need different responses, not one template:

- **A single complaint / snark** → **not a crisis.** Routine engagement (→ `reply-and-comment-writer`):
  reply calmly or ignore. Don't escalate.
- **A legitimate complaint with traction** (a defective product, a bad interaction going semi-viral) →
  service recovery + empathy; handle well before it grows.
- **Misinformation / a false claim** → **correct the record with evidence**, don't apologize (see the
  deepfake note below and `the-response-playbook.md`).
- **A deepfake / synthetic outrage / coordinated attack** → AI-faked screenshots, bot-amplified
  "backlash." **Distinguish genuine dissatisfaction from a manufactured attack**; correct with evidence,
  report it, get ahead of it, and escalate.
- **An offensive / tone-deaf post of yours** → own it quickly and genuinely; don't get defensive.
- **An outage / product failure** → transparency + timestamped updates + a real fix.
- **A systemic crisis** (attacks your core values, exec/employee misstep, safety/legal issue) → full
  response + escalation.

## Step 3: rate severity (sets urgency + who's involved)

- **Low** — isolated negativity, no real spread. **Community management + customer care** handle it
  (`reply-and-comment-writer`, `engagement-routine`). No alarm.
- **Medium** — gaining volume/velocity, a real grievance. **Align PR + support**, a measured public
  response, monitor closely.
- **High** — fast-spreading, reputational/values/legal exposure, or safety implications. **Legal review
  + executive visibility + a controlled response.** Escalate to people (`escalation-pause-and-safety.md`).

## Speed vs accuracy

Negative sentiment can reach most of your audience **before a statement is drafted**, and algorithms
amplify trending negativity. So **acknowledge fast** — but **accuracy still governs** what you commit
to. The resolution: a fast *acknowledgment* ("we're aware and looking into it") buys time without
asserting unverified facts. (Full timing in `the-response-playbook.md`.)

## Output of triage

A one-line read: *"This is a [type], severity [low/medium/high]; response = [route], escalate to
[whom], pause the queue? [y/n]."* Then proceed to the playbook — with a human in the loop.
