# The reality of original-research content in 2026 (verify-quarterly)

Volatile figures — attribute, mark **verify-quarterly**, never state as settled fact. The *principle*
(original data is the highest-leverage citable asset) is stable; the *numbers* move.

## Why original data is the #1 linkable + citable asset
- **Data-led content is the backbone of digital PR.** Per BuzzStream's 2025 link-building survey, ~94.8% of
  practitioners name data-led content as their primary digital-PR tactic; per the same source, campaigns that
  include original data earn ~41% more media coverage. (verify-quarterly)
- **Data studies out-earn other formats.** Per Backlinko data cited in Searchlab's 2026 round-up, data studies
  and original research attract ~3.2× more links than opinion or how-to pieces; top digital-PR campaigns can
  generate ~50–200 links in a single cycle. (verify-quarterly)
- **Scarcity is the moat.** Per Backlinko, ~94% of published content earns zero external links and only ~2.2%
  earns links from multiple domains — most content is undifferentiated, so a number nobody else has stands out.
- **Publications link to stories, not products.** A data finding is a news angle; a product page is not. Original
  research is the asset that earns links *passively*, because other writers need to cite your number.

## Why it dominates AI search (GEO)
- **Statistics are among the most effective GEO tactics.** The Princeton/Georgia-Tech/IIT-Delhi GEO study (KDD
  2024) found that adding citations, quotations, and statistics can lift a source's visibility in AI answers by
  ~30–40%+; adding statistics is repeatedly cited as the single strongest lever (~41%). Original data *is*
  statistics nobody else owns. (cited research — attribute; verify-quarterly)
- **Mentions beat raw links for AI.** Ahrefs' analysis of ~75,000 brands found brand web mentions/anchors
  correlate more strongly with AI-Overview visibility than backlink volume (one widely-shared figure: ~0.66 vs
  ~0.22). A cited stat travels as a mention even without a link. (verify-quarterly)
- **Distribution multiplies citations.** Seeding the same finding across many publications, rather than only
  your own site, is reported to raise AI citations dramatically (one 2026 figure: up to ~325%); a large share of
  AI citations trace to earned media. (verify-quarterly)
- **Freshness cliff.** ~50% of content cited in AI answers is reported to be <13 weeks old — so a recurring or
  refreshed study (visible "Last Updated" stamp) compounds, while publish-and-forget decays. (verify-quarterly)

## The integrity reality (stable — this is the spine)
- A data study is worth nothing the moment one number is wrong; rigor is the value, not pedantry.
- The 2026 risk: fabricated or "AI-synthesized" data points. Journalists and AI systems increasingly verify;
  a single invented stat that gets checked destroys the asset and the brand's credibility. Never fabricate.
- Sound method beats big claims: defined population + sample frame, adequate n, neutral (non-leading) questions,
  no p-hacking or cherry-picking, and disclosed limitations. Honest "directional, n=42" beats fake "most people."

## House metrics for this content type
Referring domains + brand mentions, AI-citation share, report referral traffic, and saves/shares on the cuts —
not vanity likes, and never a fabricated number.
