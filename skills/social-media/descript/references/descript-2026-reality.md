# The reality of Descript in 2026 (verify-quarterly)

Plans, credits, and feature gates shifted hard in late 2025 — attribute everything, **verify-quarterly**, and
treat pre-2025 reviews as misleading on pricing.

## The paradigm (why it wins for talk content)
Descript edits **the transcript, not the timeline**: delete a sentence and the clip disappears; rearrange a
paragraph and the footage follows. For dialogue-heavy content — podcasts, interviews, talking-head video,
courses, webinars — this is faster than any traditional NLE and makes editing accessible to non-editors. Reviews
report ~60–70% editing-time reductions for talk content (attribute; directional). It is **not** a
transcription-at-scale service, a beat-synced short-form editor (→ CapCut), or a cinematic finisher (→
Resolve/Premiere) — and it's **cloud-dependent** (no offline editing).

## The 2026 stack (attribute; verify-quarterly)
- **Underlord** (the agentic AI co-editor, Season 6 2025): natural-language editing — one-step filler-word +
  silence removal, bad-take flagging, B-roll suggestions, show notes, chapter markers, social-clip flagging.
- **The 2026 public API (open beta)** exposes projects, media import, and **Underlord actions — including via
  MCP connections** — so an agent can genuinely trigger edits programmatically where connected.
- **Overdub** (voice cloning): consent-verified, **own-voice-only by design**; training ~24–48h (source-audio
  requirements have varied — some cite ~10–30 min, Descript's help has cited as little as 30s; verify current);
  shines on **single words/short phrases**, drifts synthetic on full paragraphs; lower tiers report a
  ~1,000-word vocabulary cap; per-use AI credits.
- **Studio Sound:** one-click noise/echo/EQ — laptop audio to near-studio; ~10 AI credits per application;
  struggles with extreme noise. Cleaner audio also **improves transcription**.
- **Automatic Multicam** (auto-cut between speakers on separate tracks), Eye Contact AI, Green Screen, screen
  recording, SquadCast remote recording, YouTube/Zoom import, captions, 4K export on upper tiers.
- **Transcription accuracy:** ~92–95% clean single-speaker English; ~75–85% with noise/accents/crosstalk/jargon
  — the correction pass is mandatory (errors become wrong edits AND wrong captions). ~23 languages. SOC 2 Type II.

## The pricing reality (the Sept 2025 overhaul — the honesty gate)
Descript moved from transcription-minutes to **media minutes** (ALL media brought in counts, transcribed or not)
plus **AI-credit metering** of formerly-unlimited features (Underlord, Studio Sound, Overdub). Top-ups expire in
12 months. Documented consequences (G2/Reddit, attributed): **bill shock** ($30/mo plans jumping to "hundreds"
in a heavy month), formerly-"unlimited" features now metered, and no proration credit on mid-cycle upgrades.
**Tier figures conflict across 2026 sources** (Free ≈ 60 media min or 1hr + watermark; Hobbyist ≈ $16–24;
Creator ≈ $24–35; Business ≈ $50–65; annual ~25–35% cheaper) — **never quote a tier as fact; verify in-app.**
Credit-aware workflow: import only what you'll edit; run Studio Sound once per source; batch Underlord actions.

## House metrics
Listen-through/watch-through on the published episode + clip performance (native) — the edit's job is a tighter
listen; never fabricate a metric.
