# The WORDS framework — talk-content editing at the speed of text

Descript's leverage is that the transcript IS the timeline — so the edit is a writing task, with two integrity
gates unique to this tool (whose voice, whose meaning). Each letter is a gate; most edits fail at W (editing
audio they should have edited as text) or the consent/meaning gates in O and the interview rule.

## W — Write the edit
Transcribe, run the **accuracy pass first** (names/jargon — errors become wrong edits and wrong captions), then
edit like a document: cut tangents and bad takes, delete filler words + shorten silences in one Underlord step,
restructure by moving paragraphs. The discipline: **decide in text, verify by ear** — the transcript finds the
cut fast, but the human listens to the actual audio before approving (pacing and tone don't live in text).

## O — Overdub with consent
Fix flubs by typing — the correction regenerates in your **consent-verified, own-voice-only** clone.
- **Craft limit:** single words and short phrases only; full paragraphs drift synthetic — re-record those.
- **Practical limits:** ~24–48h training; lower-tier vocabulary caps; per-use credits (verify current).
- **Integrity:** never clone anyone else's voice (Descript's verification blocks it by design — that's the
  model); Overdub output is synthetic speech in a real voice, so honor AI-disclosure norms where they apply.

## R — Refine the sound
**Studio Sound** turns laptop audio near-studio in one click — run it **once per source file** (it costs credits
per application, and cleaner audio also improves the transcript), not per revision. Level across speakers;
shorten dead air; fix by re-take or Overdub-word, not by layering effects. Extreme noise is a re-record, not a
rescue.

## D — Dress the visuals
For video: **Automatic Multicam** cuts between speakers on separate tracks (record separate tracks on purpose);
captions from the corrected transcript; B-roll where Underlord suggests and the human agrees; Eye Contact AI for
off-camera reads (used honestly — a read is still a read); brand layout/templates from `design-and-templates`.
Descript is not the place for beat-synced effects (→ `capcut`) or color finishing (→ Resolve/Premiere).

## S — Ship the cuts
One recording → many assets, all from the same corrected transcript: the edited long-form export, AI-flagged
**highlight clips** (Underlord flags; the **human picks** what represents people fairly), show notes, chapters,
a text post from the best exchange. Route clip-at-scale to `captions-and-clipping`/`opus-clip`, short-form
styling to `capcut`, the written cut to `text-post-and-microblog` — then `scheduling-and-queue` → **WoopSocial
publishes** the finished files (it does not edit media).

## The interview rule (runs through every letter)
Editing someone else's words: **concision yes, meaning-flips never.** Text-editing makes it dangerously easy to
rearrange a guest into saying something they didn't — preserve meaning, keep clip context, offer approval on
significant edits, and let the human own the final cut of anyone else's words.
