# Editions, repurposing + two worked examples

## Edition structure (scannable, mobile-first)
```
SUBJECT: short, specific, honest (A/B test it)
HOOK: 1-2 lines — why this edition matters now
BODY: one core idea, digestible chunks, subheads + short paragraphs (70% value)
PERSONAL: a short story/POV (20%) — the personality moat
ONE CTA: reply, click, or a soft offer (10%) — not three asks
P.S.: an extra hook / reply prompt (often the most-read line)
```
Win **edition 2** (the weeks 2–3 churn window): make it your single most valuable send.

## Welcome sequence (retention)
1. Deliver the lead magnet + set the promise (what/when they'll get). 2. Edition 2 = your best work.
3. Milestone recognition + subscriber spotlights turn readers into participants. Respond to every reply.

## The social ↔ email repurposing engine (both ways)
- **Edition → social (1 → a week):** core idea → a short video (`reels-script`/`ai-video`), 3–5
  carousels (`carousel-writer`), 8–10 captions/hooks (`caption-writer`/`hook-writer`), a LinkedIn post.
  Route via `cross-platform-repurposing`; evergreen ones via `content-recycling`.
- **Social → edition:** your best-performing posts/themes (from `analytics-and-reporting`) become the
  next edition's spine. The newsletter is both a **destination and a content hub.**

## Worked example 1 — signup-driving social + edition (blunt indie-founder voice)
```
SOCIAL (drives signups): "I save freelancers ~30% on tax. Free 1-page checklist + a weekly note. Link in bio." 
EDITION 1 subject: "The 30% rule" -> hook -> the rule + 2 steps (value) -> a short founder aside -> CTA: "reply with your setup".
WELCOME: deliver the checklist; edition 2 = the full system. MEASURE: signups + clicks from the ESP. -> link-in-bio-and-traffic
```

## Worked example 2 — repurpose one edition into a week (warm studio voice)
```
EDITION: "Calm bookkeeping: the one rule we give every client."
-> Reel (the rule, 20s), 1 carousel (the steps), 3 captions, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 Story poll.
SCHEDULE: stagger over the week via scheduling-and-queue -> WoopSocial. BEST POST -> seed next edition.
```

Both: read brand/voice/pillars first; one problem/one reader; capture from social; 70-20-10; loop both
ways; measured by clicks/replies/signups from the ESP (never opens-as-vanity, never fabricated).
