# Scope, distinctions + connections

email-and-newsletter is the **owned channel** — the newsletter the audience layer social can't take
away, plus the email↔social loop. The agent **strategizes + drafts**; the **ESP is external + human-run**;
WoopSocial **publishes the social side** (signup-driving + repurposed posts).

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **WoopSocial does not send email.** No ESP, no list, no capture form, no open/click tracking. Sending
  happens in an **external ESP** (beehiiv/Substack/LinkedIn/Kit/etc.), **set up by a human.**
- **The agent's job:** newsletter strategy (niche, value mix, subject lines, welcome sequence, the
  repurposing plan, platform choice) + **drafting editions and the social-promo posts.** **WoopSocial
  publishes** the social posts that **grow the list** and **repurpose the newsletter.**
- **Metrics come from the ESP + GA4** — clicks/replies/signups (not opens-as-vanity). **Never fabricate**
  a metric.
- **Consent is mandatory** — permission-based opt-in, visible **unsubscribe**, sender identity
  (**CAN-SPAM / GDPR**). **No bought lists, no sending without opt-in, no misleading subjects, no spam.**
- **Monetize honestly** — aligned sponsors in-voice, paid subs, products; never promise income.

## Distinct from its siblings
- **email-and-newsletter (this)** — the **owned email channel** itself + the email↔social loop.
- **link-in-bio-and-traffic** — the **capture/handoff** that drives signups *into* this channel.
- **cross-platform-repurposing** — **same-moment** multi-platform adaptation (this is the **email↔social**
  axis specifically).
- **content-recycling** — **evergreen reuse over time** (pairs when recycling editions).
- **lead-magnets-and-funnels** — **designs the magnet/funnel** that fills the list.
- **social-selling-and-dm** — the **DM** conversation funnel (a different capture path).

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile`, `voice-builder`, `content-pillars`.
- **Fed by:** `link-in-bio-and-traffic` (signups), `analytics-and-reporting` (best social → next edition;
  ESP/GA4 readout), `lead-magnets-and-funnels` (the magnet).
- **Repurpose engine:** `cross-platform-repurposing`, `content-recycling`, `reels-script`/`ai-video`,
  `carousel-writer`, `caption-writer`/`hook-writer`, `linkedin-post-writer`.
- **Discovery:** `ai-search-optimization` / `social-seo` (the web archive), `linkedin-growth` (LinkedIn
  newsletter).
- **Publish (social side):** `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial`. Measure/plan: `analytics-and-reporting`,
  `goals-and-kpis`, `experimentation-and-ab-testing` (A/B subjects). **The email send itself stays in the external ESP.**
