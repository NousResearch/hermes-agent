# Sustainability & Boundaries

The half of "engagement routine" that most advice skips — and the reason routines fail. Engagement is
the activity most likely to bleed into every waking hour, and burnout is rampant: a large share of
social pros report dropping motivation/creativity, and **~46% have considered leaving the work over
stress.** A routine that isn't sustainable doesn't get followed. **Sustainability is a performance
requirement, not a soft extra.**

## The core boundaries

- **Notification discipline.** Don't check constantly. **Turn off non-essential notifications outside
  your windows.** Engagement happens in your scheduled blocks + the golden hour — not in a 14-hour
  ambient drip.
- **Set response windows — and communicate them.** "We reply within a day" is fine. Decide your
  realistic response time and let it be okay not to be instant on everything (true crises excepted →
  `crisis-and-moderation`).
- **Protect off-hours.** Evenings/weekends aren't for clearing comments. The scheduled queue keeps the
  brand present (→ `the-scheduling-engagement-flywheel.md`) so you don't have to be "on" to stay
  visible.
- **Small, repeatable systems over heroics.** A modest routine you actually keep beats an ambitious one
  you abandon in a week. Templates, recurring windows, and a short checklist reduce the daily decision
  load.

## Consistency, sustainably

Showing up consistently matters (the "no-post penalty" is real) — but **consistency means a pace you
can hold for months, not a sprint you crash out of.** Pick the smallest routine that's genuinely
consistent (even 2×10 min/day), protect the golden hour, and grow it only if it stays sustainable.

## Scale to capacity (honestly)

- **Busy week?** Shrink the windows, keep the golden hour, let proactive commenting drop first. Don't
  abandon the routine — downsize it.
- **More capacity / more platforms?** Add a window or a platform, but **concentrate where your audience
  is** rather than thinning across all of them.
- **Team?** Define who covers which window and what gets escalated — same triage, shared.

## Genuine only (the line that protects you)

The temptation under time pressure is to **automate or fake** engagement — bots, mass auto-comments,
bought interactions. Don't. It's **detected, penalized, and self-defeating**, and it's the fast road to
a damaged account. The sustainable answer is a **smaller, genuine routine**, with the agent **drafting**
to save time and the **human** doing the real interacting. Less, real, and consistent wins.
