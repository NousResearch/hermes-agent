# The Daily & Weekly Routine

The actual cadence. Engagement is the highest-return activity in social (replying lifts engagement on
every platform), but only if it's a **habit, not a heroic burst.** This is the operating system that
turns the craft (`reply-and-comment-writer`) into a repeatable routine.

> Re-verify timing specifics quarterly; the structure (windows + golden hour, batched not all-day) is
> durable.

## The principle: windows, not all-day

Don't graze notifications all day — it wrecks focus and burns you out. **Batch engagement into a few
dedicated windows.** A common, sustainable shape:

- **~20 minutes in the morning** and **~20 minutes in the afternoon** (adjust to your capacity).
- Plus a **golden-hour pass** right after each post publishes.

## The golden hour (non-negotiable)

The **first ~15–20 minutes** after a post goes live is the highest-leverage engagement window — early
replies build the comment thread and signal the algorithm, lifting reach. **Post when you can be
present**, and spend that window replying to early comments. (This is why you schedule posts for times
you can show up — see `the-scheduling-engagement-flywheel.md`.)

## Order of operations (each window)

Spend the time in priority order (full triage in `triage-priorities-and-goals.md`):

1. **Reply to comments** on your recent/fresh posts (golden hour first).
2. **Respond to DMs** — where relationships and conversions happen.
3. **Handle anything negative/urgent** quickly.
4. **Proactive commenting** — add value on 3–5 posts from **ICP, peers, and niche leaders** (borrows
   their audience; strategic commenting in `reply-and-comment-writer`).

The agent **drafts** these (in brand voice, via `reply-and-comment-writer`); the **human reviews and
posts** them in-app.

## A worked daily template (solo brand, ~40 min/day)

- **Morning (20 min):** reply to overnight comments (5) → reply to DMs (3) → 3 proactive comments on
  ICP/leader posts.
- **At publish time (golden hour, ~15 min):** reply to every early comment on the new post; ask a
  question back to keep threads alive.
- **Afternoon (20 min):** clear new comments/DMs → 2–3 more proactive comments → note any recurring
  question to turn into content.

Scale up/down by capacity (see below).

## Weekly rhythm

- **Once a week (~30 min):** find 10–15 accounts/threads worth engaging (ICP, peers, leaders) for the
  week; note recurring questions to convert into posts (→ `caption-writer`); skim what landed
  (natively — no WoopSocial analytics) and do more of it.
- **Protect the boundaries** (`sustainability-and-boundaries.md`).

## Scale the routine to reality

- **Time:** an hour a day is a healthy ceiling for most solo brands; 40 min works; even 2×10 min beats
  sporadic bursts. **Consistency > intensity.**
- **Platforms:** more platforms = more time. **Concentrate where your audience actually is** rather than
  spreading thin across every platform you publish to.
- **Capacity:** when busy, shrink the windows but **keep the golden hour** — it's the highest return.
