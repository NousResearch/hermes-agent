# Facebook Groups 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (Meta ships algorithm + product changes often).*

## The POV: Groups are Facebook's last organic goldmine — community engineering, not broadcasting
**Pages are dying organically** (~**1.37–1.65%** reach/post). **Groups reach 40–50% of members per post**
(conservative estimates say 20–40% — either way a **10–30× multiplier**) — a 10k-member group reaches
**2,000–5,000** vs a Page's ~137–600. **1.8B monthly Group
users** across **10M+ active groups**; **700M+ visit Groups daily.** Groups generate **~5× the engagement** of
Pages, members **return 2× as often**, and group audiences **convert at 3–5× cold** (well-managed niche groups
hit **20–23%** on direct offers; **$3K–15K/mo** is common). **Facebook deliberately favors Groups** ("Groups
for Good" / community strategy as a counterbalance to messaging apps + rival platforms — expected through
2027): Group posts outrank Page posts because the **Andromeda AI reads back-and-forth conversation as
"meaningful social interaction" (MSI).** The mindset shift: **"broadcast marketing" → "community
engineering."** A group is a **"watering hole"** for your most qualified leads — **value first, trust second,
sales third** (lead-with-promotion groups fail).

## Setup + structure
- **Name:** keyword-rich + searchable ("Social Media Marketing Tips," not "Our Cool Group"). **Privacy:
  Private (Visible)** for most — **discoverable in search but content members-only**; private groups see ~40%
  higher engagement (exclusivity → openness). **Description:** what members get + the rules. **Branded cover**;
  **tags** for discovery; **2–3 membership questions** to filter quality members (+ capture emails); **admin
  approval** at first.

## Content + engagement (inside the group)
- **Two-way conversation, not broadcast** (treating it like a Page kills it). **Post daily** with varied
  content — educational, discussions, **polls** (one-click engagement that punches above its weight), occasional
  promos. **Consistency > frequency.** **Short-form video/Reels ~3× the reach** of static; photos high
  engagement. **Engagement velocity: the first 30–60 min** (10 genuine comments in 30 min reaches 3–5× more
  than over 24h — notify your most engaged members). **Saves + shares > likes.** **Don't sell in the first 30
  days**; **never post-and-ghost** (respond, or reach tanks). Sub-groups for scale; automated welcome messages
  + pinned resources.

## Growth (quality, not blasts)
- **200–500 members/month** is sustainable + healthy ratios (fast low-quality invite blasts tank reach — big
  audience, no interaction). **~1,000 members in 2–4 months** with active effort (2–4 weeks with an email
  list; 6–12 months organic-only). **Levers:** invite your **email list + social followers** (target ~30%
  acceptance, ~40% 30-day retention), **non-competing admin partnerships** (co-host/giveaways/shout-outs), a
  **lead magnet**, and **ads to the group join link** (you can't run ads inside a group). **Genuine
  cross-posting** of value in related groups drives discovery — but **NOT bulk-poster/automation blasting**
  (ToS violation; Facebook's aggressive AI detection → bans / group removal). **AI content** is treated
  identically to human content (no penalty) — but low-quality AI spam is detected.
