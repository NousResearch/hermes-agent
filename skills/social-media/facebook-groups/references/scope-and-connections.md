# Scope, distinctions + connections

facebook-groups is the **Groups-as-organic-surface strategy** — build a niche group as a community asset
(10–30× Page reach) and grow it value-first. The agent **designs the strategy + drafts the group posts +
drafts the Page/cross-channel content that drives joins**; **WoopSocial publishes the Page + cross-channel
funnel content (not group posts)**; **in-group posting, admin, and moderation are human/native →
`community-management`.**

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** designs the group strategy (niche, name/privacy/questions, content calendar, growth levers,
  moderation SOPs, value-first monetization), **drafts the group posts**, and **drafts the Page/cross-channel
  content that drives joins.**
- **WoopSocial does NOT post to Groups.** The **`FACEBOOK` platform is the Page** (postType TEXT_ONLY/IMAGE/
  VIDEO/REEL/STORY + optional link/locationId) — **there is no Group target.** **WoopSocial publishes the
  Page + cross-channel content that funnels people to JOIN the group.**
- **Native/human (→ `community-management` + `crisis-and-moderation`):** in-group **posting**, **admin**,
  **membership questions**, **moderation**, and **Group analytics.**
- **No bulk-poster/automation blasting** (ToS violation; Facebook's aggressive detection → bans/group
  removal; tanks reach); **no fake members/bots**; **value-first, not promotion-first** (no selling the first
  30 days); **respond, don't post-and-ghost**; **quality growth over invite blasts**; **never fabricate
  metrics or revenue.** **Verify-quarterly.**

## Distinct from its siblings
- **facebook-groups (this)** — the **Groups-as-organic-surface STRATEGY** (the community asset, 10–30× reach,
  value-first growth + monetization).
- **facebook-strategy** — the **FB Page/account play** (the REACH framework: the Page, Reels, the feed). **The
  Page funnels here.**
- **community-management** — the **cross-platform engagement/moderation ROUTINE** (the **in-group execution
  runs here**).
- **crisis-and-moderation** — **moderation/crisis** (the group-moderation execution).
- **lead-magnets-and-funnels** / **creator-monetization** — the **funnel/monetization** the group feeds.
- **link-in-bio-and-traffic** — driving to the **join link.**

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (niche/voice), `goals-and-kpis` (community vs leads vs membership).
- **Routes execution to:** `community-management` (in-group engagement + admin), `crisis-and-moderation`
  (moderation), `reply-and-comment-writer` (the replies).
- **Funnels from / to:** `facebook-strategy` (the **Page** that drives joins — what WoopSocial publishes),
  `lead-magnets-and-funnels`/`email-and-newsletter` (capture via membership questions), `creator-monetization`
  (offers to the warm audience), `link-in-bio-and-traffic` (the join link), `caption-writer`/`reels-script`/
  `nano-banana` (the Page + group content).
- **Publishes via:** `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` — **the Page + cross-channel join-funnel content
  only** (the group itself is native). **Measures with:** native **Group Insights** + `analytics-and-
  reporting` (acceptance, retention, engaged members). **In-group posting, admin, moderation + analytics stay
  native/human.**
