# Facebook 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly.*

## The shift: an AI discovery engine, not a Page-broadcast feed
Page **organic reach has collapsed** to ~**1.65%** (range **1.6–5.9%** of followers) — but this isn't
Facebook declining. The algorithm **fully shifted to proactive AI recommendation**: up to **35–50% of
feed views are "unconnected" content** from accounts users don't follow ("Andromeda"; UTIS Reels survey,
Jan 2026). It ranks in four steps — **Inventory → Signals → Predictions → Relevance Score** — and uses
**NLP to read captions and video transcripts**, so Facebook now behaves like a **search/discovery
engine**. Distribution depends on how well the AI understands your content, not how many follow you.

## The signals that matter
- **Saves & shares > likes/reactions** (the most powerful signals); meaningful comments (MSI),
  long-form replies, shares to Stories, dwell time, and **watch time** rank highest.
- **Engagement velocity in the first 30–60 min** is decisive — ~10 genuine comments in 30 min can
  reach **3–5× more** people than the same spread over 24h. Reply to every comment early.
- **Thematic consistency:** the algorithm scans your **last 9–12 posts** to tag the account. Scattered
  topics confuse it → lower reach. **Focus ~2 core themes** (occasional off-topic is fine).

## Surfaces + formats
- **All videos are now Reels** (mid-2025). **Reels = highest reach/discovery** — pushed to non-followers
  **by content category, not brand**; **Page Reels often beat Page posts 5–10×**. First 3 seconds,
  retention, completion decide distribution.
- **Photos = highest feed engagement**; **carousels** for storytelling. **Video is shared ~1,200% more**
  than other formats. **Longer 3–8 min video** is now also boosted ("time spent").
- **Links perform worst** (off-platform "leaks") → put the **link in the first comment** (or a native Shop).

## Groups = the reach goldmine
**1.8B monthly Group users.** Groups reach **~20–40% of members** vs **~1–6% for Pages** — a post in a
10k-member Group reaches ~2,000–4,000 vs ~200–600 for a same-size Page. Group posts **rank higher** in
Feed (community signal). The 2026 experience is **Groups-first**; Meta **algorithmically favors Groups**
(they keep people on-platform) while **monetizing Pages via ads**. Building/owning Groups captures
materially more reach.

## Honest fit + caveats
- **Small/specialized Pages can out-reach large ones** — large Pages are penalized for scale; niche
  relevance wins.
- **AI content is treated identically to human** (no penalty) — **but** low-quality AI-spam detection
  is rising and there's an **authenticity premium** (BTS, personal stories, founder-led outperform).
- **3B+ users, older-skewing**; great for **community, local, B2C, discovery**; weak for pure Page-feed
  broadcasting. **Engagement-bait** ("like & share") is penalized. New: **creator affiliate program**
  (Meta's TikTok-Shop answer) lets creators attach affiliate links to Reels.

## The strategic pattern
**Reels for unconnected discovery → Feed for connected depth/trust → Groups for community.** Reels gets
you in front of new people; Feed gives them a reason to stay and trust; Groups turns them into a community.
