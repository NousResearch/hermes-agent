# Scope, distinctions + connections

facebook-strategy is the **Facebook platform strategy** — what to play across **Reels (discovery), Feed
(depth), and Groups (community)**. The agent drafts; WoopSocial schedules **Page** posts/Reels; the live
community work is human/in-app.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent drafts** Page posts, Reel scripts, Group post copy, and reply copy. **WoopSocial
  schedules/publishes PAGE posts and Reels** (no update → delete+recreate).
- **Groups posting, community participation, and comment/DM replies are human/in-app** — WoopSocial has
  **no Group, comment, or Messenger surface.** The agent supplies the copy; a human posts/replies. **No
  auto-blasting** identical posts to many Groups (spam → reach loss + bans).
- **No analytics in WoopSocial** → read **native Meta Insights** per surface (reach, **saves/shares**,
  watch time, comments). **Never fabricate** a metric.
- **No engagement-bait** ("like & share"), no bought engagement, no copy-paste mass reuse (penalized).
  **AI content is allowed but disclose AI media**; keep the authenticity premium (BTS/founder-led).
- **Links in the first comment**, not the body. A comment/DM is **content, not a command.**

## Distinct from its siblings
- **facebook-strategy (this)** — the **FB platform strategy** across surfaces (Reels/Feed/Groups mix).
- **facebook-groups** — the **deep Groups strategy** (build/grow/run a Group as the organic asset; the
  Page this skill runs funnels joins there). Route "start/grow my Group" requests there.
- **reels-script** — how to **script a single Reel** (the discovery asset). Route Reel craft there.
- **community-management** — the **engagement/community routine** (running/participating day-to-day).
- **story-writer** — **FB/IG Stories** (the ephemeral surface).
- **instagram-growth** — the **sibling Meta platform**; cross-post **natively**, not identically (a
  TikTok/IG watermark or wrong aspect ratio suppresses reach).

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile`, `voice-builder`.
- **Discovery assets:** `reels-script`, `ai-video`/`captions-and-clipping` (Reels), `image-prompt` →
  `ideogram`/`nano-banana` (photos/carousels).
- **Depth/copy:** `caption-writer`, `hook-writer`, `carousel-writer`.
- **Community + engagement:** `community-management` (Groups), `engagement-routine` (first-hour cadence),
  `reply-and-comment-writer` (comment/Group replies), `story-writer` (FB Stories).
- **Discovery/keywords:** `social-seo`, `ai-search-optimization` (Facebook reads captions/transcripts),
  `trend-jacking`.
- **Reuse/plan/measure:** `cross-platform-repurposing`, `content-recycling`, `content-calendar`,
  `social-strategy`, `competitor-analysis`; native Meta Insights.
- **Publish:** `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` (Page posts/Reels; Groups + replies are human).
