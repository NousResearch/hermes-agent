# The REACH framework — play the surfaces, not the dead feed

Facebook is no longer a Page-broadcast feed — it's an **AI discovery engine plus a community platform.**
Stop spraying Page posts at followers (≈1.65% reach) and play the **three surfaces**: Reels for
discovery, Feed for depth, Groups for community. REACH is the 2026 engine.

## R — Reels for discovery
- Reels are the **unconnected-discovery** engine — pushed to non-followers **by content category**
  (NLP reads your captions/transcripts). **Page Reels often beat Page posts 5–10×.**
- Win the **first 3 seconds**, **retention**, and **completion**. All videos are Reels now — design
  vertical and native (script via `reels-script`).

## E — Engage the first hour
- **Velocity in the first 30–60 min** decides reach (10 comments in 30 min → 3–5× more reach). Post in
  your audience's active window; **reply to every comment** fast.
- Optimise for **saves & shares + meaningful comments** (MSI) — not likes.

## A — Anchor ~2 themes
- The algorithm tags your account from your **last 9–12 posts** — **focus ~2 core themes** so the AI
  can confidently match you to an audience. Use **social-SEO keywords** naturally (Facebook reads text).
- Pick **one clear viewer**; occasional off-topic (team moment) won't derail you.

## C — Community in Groups
- **Groups are the goldmine** (20–40% member reach vs 1–6% Pages; Meta favors them). **Build or actively
  participate** in niche Groups; Group posts rank higher (community signal).
- Community is **human-run, in-app** — never auto-blasted. Route the **build/grow/run-a-Group strategy**
  to `facebook-groups`; the day-to-day engagement discipline to `community-management`.

## H — Hold trust in Feed + handle links natively
- **Feed = connected depth/trust:** photos (highest feed engagement), carousels (storytelling), longer
  **3–8 min video** ("time spent"). **Pair a Reel (discovery) with a Feed post (depth)** on the theme.
- **Links perform worst → put the link in the first comment** (or native Shop). **No engagement-bait**;
  lean on the **authenticity premium** (BTS, founder-led).

## The Facebook brief a request should fill
```
SURFACES: Reel (discovery) + Feed post (depth) per theme; Group plan (human)
THEMES: ~2 core themes; one clear viewer; social-SEO keywords in captions
REEL: vertical, first-3-seconds hook, retention/completion (reels-script)
FEED: photo/carousel/long video; comment/save/share-worthy; link in first comment
ENGAGE: post in active window; reply every comment in the first hour
MEASURE: native Meta Insights (saves/shares/reach per surface)
```
