# goals-and-kpis

The **target-setting layer** — turn business outcomes into a measurable cascade with one **North Star
per channel**, benchmark-set targets, and action thresholds. This skill **sets** the win conditions;
**analytics-and-reporting measures** against them.

## The POV: goals, not wishes
"Grow our following," "go viral," "do better on Instagram" are **wishes** — unmeasurable, unowned, and
disconnected from the business. Build a **cascade** instead: **business goal → SMART objective → KPI →
supporting metrics**, with **one North Star per channel** tied to its real job, a **target from your own
benchmark** (achievable yet ambitious), a **3–5 cap** for focus, and **thresholds that trigger action**.
A KPI must be **controllable, non-vanity, and mapped to an outcome** — and a well-set one (a follower
target paired with an engagement floor) **prevents gaming.**

2. **social-strategy** — the **business outcomes** every goal must ladder up to.

## The framework: NORTH
(Depth: `references/the-north-framework.md`.)
- **N — Name the business goal:** anchor every social goal to a real outcome (awareness/leads/sales/
  retention); if it doesn't ladder up, cut it.
- **O — One North Star per channel:** the single win-condition metric (awareness → reach; consideration
  → CTR; conversion → CVR) + 1–2 supporting.
- **R — Render it SMART:** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound — a number + a deadline
  + a method.
- **T — Target from benchmarks:** set the number from your **baseline**/comparable benchmarks (no data →
  a **learning goal** first); leading + lagging; only **controllable** KPIs.
- **H — Hold to 3–5 + set triggers:** cap KPIs/objectives at 3–5; action thresholds ("if X holds but Y
  drops → do Z"); review weekly/monthly/quarterly; hand to analytics-and-reporting.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **Sets, doesn't measure** — measuring progress is **analytics-and-reporting** (native analytics;
  WoopSocial has none). This skill defines targets/North Stars/thresholds, then hands off.
- **Targets from real benchmarks** — **no fabricated baselines**; no data → a **learning goal** first;
  comparable benchmarks are **provisional/flagged**.
- **Controllable, non-vanity, outcome-mapped KPIs only.** Pair growth with a **quality guardrail** — a
  well-set KPI **prevents gaming** (no follower-buying/pods).
- **Realistic, not hype** — achievable yet ambitious; flag impossible asks. **Miss → pivot tactics first**,
  don't move the goalposts or fudge numbers.
  (Scope, distinctions + connections: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)

## Distinct from its siblings (route correctly)
**goals-and-kpis (this)** = SET targets/North Star/thresholds · **analytics-and-reporting** = MEASURE
against them · **social-strategy** = the whole plan (this is its goals/KPI layer) · **experimentation** =
test tactics to move a KPI · **competitor-analysis** = benchmark context, not your own goals.

## Where this connects

(baseline), **competitor-analysis** (relative), the **\*-growth** skills (per-platform norms). Hands
targets to: **analytics-and-reporting** (measurement), **content-calendar**/**batch-content-plan**
(cadence), **experimentation** (tests), **campaign-and-launch-planning** (campaign KPIs). Per-channel
North Stars draw on the per-platform growth skills. No publishing/tool surface — a planning skill.

## Definition of done
A cascade per goal (business outcome → SMART objective with number+deadline+method → one North Star per
channel + 1–2 supporting); targets set from real baselines/benchmarks (or a learning goal if none),
achievable yet ambitious, with a quality guardrail; capped at 3–5 with action thresholds and a weekly/
monthly/quarterly review cadence; controllable, non-vanity, outcome-mapped KPIs; handed to
analytics-and-reporting to measure; nothing fabricated, correctly distinguished from analytics-and-reporting,
social-strategy, and experimentation.
