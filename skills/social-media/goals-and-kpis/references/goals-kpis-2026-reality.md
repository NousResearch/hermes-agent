# Goals & KPIs 2026 — verified

*Volatile-ish. Re-verify benchmarks quarterly; the frameworks are stable.*

## The cascade (the core structure)
**Business goal → objective → KPI → supporting metrics.** Goals are broad and directional ("build brand
awareness"); **objectives are specific, measurable, time-bound** ("grow IG engagement 1.8% → 3.5% by Q4
2026"); **KPIs** are the metrics that prove the objective; supporting metrics are diagnostics. *"An
objective without a number is a wish; without a deadline, a dream."* If a social goal doesn't **ladder
up** to a business outcome, cut it.

## North Star — one per channel
Pick **one North Star metric per channel**, tied to that channel's **win condition / role**:
- **Awareness-first** (short-form video) → **reach / qualified impressions**.
- **Consideration-first** (LinkedIn) → **CTR / link clicks** to an offer.
- **Conversion-first** (retargeting, link-led) → **conversions / CPA**.
Cross-channel comparison only works when **each channel has its own win condition.** Add 1–2 supporting
metrics per North Star.

## SMART (the litmus test)
**S**pecific · **M**easurable · **A**chievable · **R**elevant · **T**ime-bound. "Increase Reels
engagement from 2.5% to 4% by end of Q2 2026" is SMART; "do better on Instagram" is not. A good target
also **prevents gaming** — "grow 10k → 11.5k **while holding 3%+ engagement rate**" stops you buying fake
followers to hit a number.

## The KPI map = the funnel
Mirror how the business grows: **awareness → engagement → clicks → conversions → retention.** Reach =
distribution; clicks = intent; conversions = value. Anchor each objective to the right inflection point.

## Hierarchy + the 3–5 rule
**1 North Star → 3–5 strategic KPIs → operational KPIs → unlimited diagnostics.** Cap **objectives at
3–5 per quarter** too (fewer = lack of ambition; more = fragmentation → prioritize ruthlessly). Too many
KPIs dilute focus; too few hide problems.

## Choosing a KPI (the criteria)
Start from the **business objective** → identify **leading** (predict: reach, saves, engagement) vs
**lagging** (confirm: revenue, conversions, churn) → **actionable** (you can take concrete steps) →
**measurable** (data available consistently) → **correlated** (improving it drives results) →
**controllable** (your team can actually influence it). Lead-gen must specify **quality** — doubling
leads while halving conversion isn't progress.

## Targets from benchmarks + action thresholds
Set the number from a **benchmark**: your own **baseline** (extrapolate past performance) or comparable
accounts — **achievable yet ambitious.** Make targets **trigger choices**: "if reach holds but clicks
drop → change the hook/offer; if clicks rise but conversions fall → fix the landing page/tracking before
optimizing content."

## Review cadence
**Weekly** (tactical metric check) · **monthly** (tactic performance) · **quarterly** (do the goals
still make sense?). **If a goal is missed, pivot tactics first — don't move the goalposts.** An **OKR**
view helps tie social to revenue/pipeline/brand health for stakeholders. Setting targets is **this
skill**; measuring against them is **analytics-and-reporting.**
