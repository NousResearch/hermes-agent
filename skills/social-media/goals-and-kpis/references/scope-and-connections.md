# Scope, distinctions + connections

goals-and-kpis is the **target-setting layer** — it turns business outcomes into a measurable cascade
(goal → SMART objective → North Star KPI → supporting metrics) with benchmark-set targets and action
thresholds. It **sets** the win conditions; **analytics-and-reporting measures** against them.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **Sets, doesn't measure.** This skill defines targets/North Stars/thresholds; **measuring progress is
  `analytics-and-reporting`** (which reads native platform analytics — WoopSocial has no analytics surface).
- **Targets come from real benchmarks**, never fantasy. **No fabricated baselines** — if there's no data,
  set a **learning goal** to establish one first; comparable benchmarks are **provisional, flagged**.
- **KPIs must be controllable, non-vanity, and mapped to a business outcome.** Refuse pure vanity goals
  (raw follower count) and **gameable** targets; pair growth metrics with a **quality guardrail** (e.g.
  ER floor) — a well-set KPI is what **prevents gaming** (buying followers, engagement pods).
- **Realistic, not hype** — achievable yet ambitious; if a request demands an impossible number, say so.
- **Miss a goal → pivot tactics first**, don't quietly move the goalposts or fudge the number.

## Distinct from its siblings
- **goals-and-kpis (this)** — **SET** the targets, North Star per channel, and action thresholds.
- **analytics-and-reporting** — **MEASURE** native performance against those targets + report.
- **social-strategy** — the **whole plan**; this is its **goals/KPI layer** (it supplies the business outcomes).
- **experimentation** — **test tactics** to move a KPI toward its target.
- **competitor-analysis** — supplies **benchmark context** (relative targets), not your own goals.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile`, `social-strategy` (the business outcomes to ladder up to).
- **Benchmark inputs:** `analytics-and-reporting` (your baseline), `competitor-analysis` (relative
  context), the `*-growth` skills (per-platform norms).
- **Hands targets to:** `analytics-and-reporting` (measurement), `content-calendar`/`batch-content-plan`
  (cadence to hit them), `experimentation-and-ab-testing` (tests toward them), `campaign-and-launch-planning` (campaign KPIs).
- **Per-channel North Stars draw on:** `instagram-growth`, `tiktok-growth`, `linkedin-growth`,
  `youtube-shorts`/`-long-form`, `x-growth`, `pinterest-growth`, `facebook-strategy`.
- **No publishing/tool surface** — this is a planning skill; outputs feed the measurement + content loop.
