## Goal → objective → KPI cascades (with guardrails)
| Business goal | SMART objective (example) | KPI(s) | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand awareness | unaided awareness 18% → 30% by Dec 2026 | reach, share of voice, branded search | ER not collapsing |
| Community/affinity | IG ER 1.8% → 3.5% + 45k → 80k followers by Q4 | ER, follower-growth-rate, saves/shares | 3%+ ER floor |
| Traffic | organic sessions 85k → 150k/mo by Q4 | clicks/CTR, UTM sessions | bounce rate |

| Sales | social-attributed revenue +20% by Q3 | CVR, social-attributed revenue | CPA ceiling |
| Retention/care | response time 10m → 2m by next quarter | response time, resolution rate | sentiment |

## Setting the target (from benchmark, not fantasy)
1. Pull the **baseline** (last 30–90 days, native analytics). 2. Set **achievable-yet-ambitious** lift
from it (e.g. +15–25% on a leading metric). 3. Add a **guardrail** quality floor. 4. Add an **action
threshold**. No baseline → a **learning goal** (establish the baseline first; comparable benchmarks are
provisional only).

## Worked example 1 — focused quarter (blunt indie-founder voice)
```
BUSINESS GOAL: more trial sign-ups.
OBJECTIVE (SMART): newsletter sign-ups from social 200 → 300/mo by Q3 2026.
NORTH STAR: IG = ER by reach; Pinterest = outbound clicks. SUPPORTING: saves, UTM sessions.
TARGET: from a 210/mo baseline; +43% is a stretch but trend supports it. GUARDRAIL: hold 3%+ ER.
TRIGGER: clicks flat 2 wks -> change the CTA/offer. CADENCE: weekly metric, monthly tactic, quarterly goal.
HANDOFF: analytics-and-reporting measures.
```

## Worked example 2 — new-account learning goal (warm studio voice)
```
SITUATION: brand-new account, no baseline.
LEARNING GOAL (90 days): publish 4x/week, establish baseline reach + ER per platform; no numeric target yet.
PROVISIONAL GUIDE: industry ER ranges (flagged provisional, not a promise).
THEN: set Q2 SMART targets from the 90-day baseline. NORTH STAR (provisional): IG ER, TikTok reach.
```

Both: anchored to a business outcome; SMART; benchmark-set (or learning goal); one North Star per
channel + guardrail; capped + triggered; handed to `analytics-and-reporting` to measure.
