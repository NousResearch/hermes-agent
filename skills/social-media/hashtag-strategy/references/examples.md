# Examples — discoverability sets in practice

Worked examples showing the **keyword/SEO layer first**, then a tight tag set per platform, plus a
branded-tag design — and a contrast with the old 30-tag block. Topic: a SaaS founder's post about
deleting their most-requested feature. Adapt tags to the real account/niche; always vet on-platform.

---

## Example 1 — Instagram Reel (hard cap 5)

**Keyword/SEO layer (the real lever):**
- **Caption (front-loaded):** "Why we deleted our most-requested SaaS feature — and what we built
  instead." (primary keyword: *SaaS feature* / *product decisions*)
- **On-screen text:** "We deleted our #1 requested feature."
- **Spoken:** say "most-requested feature" and "product decisions" aloud.
- **Audio:** a relevant trending sound (does more than tags for Reels reach).

**Hashtags (3–5, niche/mid-tier, in caption):**
`#BuildInPublic #SaaSProduct #ProductManagement #StartupLessons #IndieHackers`
*(branded tag optional, e.g. `#WoopBuildLog`)*

→ 5 tags, all relevant, mid-tier, no generic mega-tags. Respects the IG cap.

---

## Example 2 — TikTok (treat tags as keywords)

**Triple-mention keyword:** "delete a feature" / "SaaS product decisions" — **spoken + on-screen +
caption front**.
**Caption:** "How we decide what to build for our SaaS (we deleted our most-requested feature)."
**Hashtags (3–5, as keywords, 1 broad + niche):**
`#SaaS #BuildInPublic #StartupTok #ProductDecisions #FoundersOfTikTok`

→ `#StartupTok`/`#FoundersOfTikTok` signal the right side of TikTok; no `#fyp`. The keywords, not the
tags, do the ranking.

---

## Example 3 — LinkedIn (3–5 specific)

**Keywords** live in the post body naturally (LinkedIn indexes them).
**Hashtags:** `#SaaS #ProductManagement #BuildInPublic` — specific, not `#Leadership`/`#Business`.

## Example 4 — X / Twitter

**0–1 tag.** The post carries itself; add a single tag only to join a live conversation. Usually
none.

---

## Example 5 — a branded/campaign hashtag

**Branded (ongoing):** `#WoopBuildLog` — short, unique, on-brand; goes in the bio + every build-log
post; followers use it to share their own build updates.
**Campaign (time-bound):** `#WoopLaunchWeek` — for a launch; promoted in the CTA ("post your setup
with #WoopLaunchWeek"); tracked via native search.
*(Both checked first to confirm they're unused and typo-safe.)*

---

## Contrast — the old block (do NOT do this)

`#love #viral #fyp #explore #instagood #follow4follow #marketing #business #entrepreneur
#success #motivation #socialmedia #digitalmarketing #growth #contentcreator …(×30)`

→ Generic mega-tags that bury the post; `#fyp` does nothing; over the IG cap; identical on every
post; zero relevance signal. If a user asks for this, redirect to a tight relevant set + the keyword
layer.

---

## What the good examples share

- **Keywords/SEO done first** (caption front + on-screen + spoken), because that drives discovery.
- A **tight, relevant, mid-tier** tag set, **per platform**, within caps.
- **No generic mega-tags**, no `#fyp`, no 30-tag block.
- A **branded tag** where community/UGC applies.
- Honest framing: tags categorize and aid search; content + engagement drive reach.
