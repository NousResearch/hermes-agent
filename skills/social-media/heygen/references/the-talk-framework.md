# The TALK framework — make an avatar that doesn't feel like one

Avatars are the right tool for **scaled, scripted, multilingual delivery** — explainers, training,
localization, FAQ, faceless channels, personalized sales at volume. They're the **wrong** tool for
spontaneity, personality-led content, and parasocial connection (that's still human territory). The
TALK framework keeps avatar video useful instead of uncanny — with consent and disclosure as the
non-negotiable spine running through all four.

## T — Tailor the avatar to the job
- **Personal brand / founder voice** → a custom **Digital Twin** (Avatar V from ~15s of footage;
  holds identity across long videos). **Consent verification required** — only your own or a
  consented person's likeness.
- **Generic / faceless** → a **stock avatar** (no likeness questions).
- **Quick social clip (<15s)** → a **Talking Photo** is fine; beyond that it turns uncanny — use
  Avatar IV/V.
- Match the avatar's look/demeanor to **brand-profile** and the audience.

## A — Audio that fits
- Pick the **voice and language** deliberately; clone the speaker's voice for consistency, or pick a
  stock voice that matches **voice-builder**.
- For **localization**, use Video Translator (175+ languages, lip-sync); voice cloning keeps the
  speaker's voice across languages. (Standalone narration → ai-voiceover.)

## L — Lean script (write for avatar delivery)
- Avatars deliver **calm, scripted clarity** well — and hype/improv poorly. Write conversational,
  one idea, short sentences, no tongue-twisters or rapid-fire energy.
- Open with the point (no "hey guys"); keep segments tight. A good avatar script reads like a
  confident person explaining one thing clearly.

## K — Keep eyes moving
- A static talking head **fatigues** viewers fast. Cut away: B-roll (via veo-3 / ai-video),
  screen-recordings, on-screen text, shot/background changes, captions (most watch on mute).
- Treat the avatar as the **spine** of the video, not the whole frame. Break long scripts into
  tighter segments.

## The spine: consent + disclosure (never optional)
- **Only consented avatars** — your own twin, a consented person, stock, or licensed talent. Never a
  real non-consenting person (deepfake). HeyGen requires consent verification; enforce it yourself.
- **Always disclose** the avatar is AI (EU AI Act transparency; TikTok auto-disclosure; YouTube
  Altered-Content). Disclose in caption and/or on-screen.

## Don't lean on one twin
AI-avatar feeds crowd fast; a single twin sees **CPMs creep and CTRs flatten within weeks.**
Diversify — multiple avatars, mixed avatar/live-action, statics-and-motion. HeyGen is one input.
