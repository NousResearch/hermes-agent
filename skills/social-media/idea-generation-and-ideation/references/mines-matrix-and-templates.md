# Signal mines, the matrix & worked examples

## The signal-mine checklist (run weekly, ~15 min)
Comments + DMs since last week clustered (confusion / objections / requests) ✓ · best question from any
sales/support conversation ✓ · one niche-forum thread with repeated engagement ✓ · competitor top-3 posts
scanned for pattern + gap ✓ · one People-Also-Ask pull on a pillar ✓ · anything captured mid-week filed ✓.

## The expansion prompt pattern (AI as multiplier, not muse)
"Here are [N] real audience signals from this week: [paste clustered comments/questions]. For each theme,
propose 3 content ideas that would earn saves or shares for [audience], in [brand voice], each mapped to a
pillar and a format." — a real signal in, angled multiples out. Never the naked "10 ideas about X."

## The matrix (pillar × format × angle)
| One idea: "clients under-price their work" | Format | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| The story | text post | the client who doubled prices and lost nobody |
| The how-to | carousel | a 4-step repricing walkthrough |
| The contrarian | text post | "discounting is lying about your value" |
| The proof | quote card / stat | the real before/after number (sourced) |
| The participation | poll | "what stops you raising prices?" (feeds next week's S) |

## The idea-bank card (score each entry)
Idea · source signal (real, linked) · pillar · segment · format(s) · effort (S/M/L) · demand evidence (none /
asked once / asked repeatedly) · status (new / probed / scheduled / parked). Weekly review scores; monthly
refresh prunes; quarterly audit rebalances pillars.

## The probe ladder (cheap → expensive)
Poll on the angle → text post of the core claim → carousel → short video → the series/flagship. Each rung's
real response (saves/comments/DMs) buys the next rung. Silence = re-angle, not necessarily kill.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (signal → post)
"ran the weekly mine. same question showed up in 4 DMs and a sales call: 'do i need an LLC before my first
client?' that's not a content idea, that's a content ORDER. wrote the text post in 20 minutes, it's my best
performer this month. my idea system is mostly just listening with a spreadsheet." — audience signal, demand
evidence, fast execution.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (the matrix stretch)
"We worried we'd said everything about tax-season prep. Then we ran one tired topic through the matrix — the
same advice became a client story, a five-slide checklist, a gentle myth-bust, and a poll asking what scares
people most (which, sweetly, handed us next month's ideas). One small step: pick your most 'finished' topic and
give it four new outfits." — remix over exhaustion, the loop feeding itself.

## Never
Ideate on creation day · ask AI for generic idea lists (expansion only, from real signals) · reword a
competitor's post · invent an 'audience question' · manufacture trend urgency · hoard 200 unscored ideas ·
green-light expensive ideas without a probe · fabricate probe results · claim WoopSocial stores ideas.
