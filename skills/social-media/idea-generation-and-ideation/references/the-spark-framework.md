# The SPARK framework — an idea system, not a muse

Amateurs wait for inspiration; operators run a system whose raw material is real signals. Each letter is a
gate; most "I'm out of ideas" pain is a missing S (no input diet) or R (no bank), and most "my content feels
generic" pain is P done backwards (AI for discovery instead of expansion).

## S — Stock the inputs
An idea system eats signals. The input diet, in priority order: **your audience** (comments, DMs, FAQs, poll
results, sales-call questions — clustered into themes: confusion, objections, requests), **where the audience
talks** (niche forums/subreddits, support tickets), **competitor top-performers** (patterns + gaps, never
rewording), **search demand** (People-Also-Ask, repeated questions), **cross-domain reading** (the non-obvious
analogies nobody else in the niche will have). **Proprietary inputs are the moat** — generic models can't
reproduce your client conversations or your data. Wire the pipes: `audience-research`,
`interactive-content` (every poll is a micro-survey), `community-management` (the human relays what they hear),
`data-and-original-research`.

## P — Produce in volume (AI expands; it doesn't discover)
Divergence first, judgment later. **Capture friction-free** — one tool, seconds, never organize mid-capture.
Then expand deliberately: feed a *specific real signal* (a clustered comment theme, one client question, a
result you measured) to the model and multiply angles from it — never "give me 10 ideas about X," which returns
the same median list every competitor gets. **Separate thinking from making:** a weekly ideation block fills
the bank; creation days execute, they don't ideate.

## A — Angle it to the brand
Raw ideas become *yours* through the matrix and the POV filter:
- **The matrix:** pillar × format × angle — one idea multiplies into a story, a how-to, a contrarian take, a
  listicle entry, a before/after, a poll. Remix engines: new segment × old topic, new format × proven topic,
  this-year update × evergreen, top-performers re-angled.
- **The POV filter:** if a competitor could post it unchanged, it isn't yours yet (→
  `contrarian-and-opinion`). Repetition is a feature — the audience didn't memorize your archive.
- **Trend fit:** trends time your pillars; they don't replace them.

## R — Rank + bank
Convergence: score each idea against **pillar fit + audience evidence + business goal + effort**, kill gently
(a parked idea isn't a dead one), and keep the **bank** at 100+ (3–5 pillars × 20–30). Cadence: capture all
week → **weekly review** organizes + scores in batch → **monthly refresh** → **quarterly pillar audit**. The
bank lives in the user's tools (Notion/notes) — WoopSocial publishes content, not ideas.

## K — Kick-test before production
Expensive ideas earn a cheap probe first: demand evidence (is it asked repeatedly?), then a low-cost test — a
text post or poll on the core angle before the month of video production. Read probes honestly (signal =
saves/comments/DMs; silence = re-angle; a probe is direction, not proof), let the **human** make the
invest/kill call, and feed what shipped back into S via `analytics-and-reporting`. The loop, not the list, is
the system.
