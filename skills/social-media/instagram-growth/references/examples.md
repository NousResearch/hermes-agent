# Examples — growth diagnoses

Worked diagnoses showing the loop, the broken-stage call, and the routing — plus honest
expectations. Subject: a solo founder building a SaaS social scheduler. Voice context is the blunt
indie-founder voice; your output uses the loaded `voice.md`, `social-strategy.md`, `audience.md`.

---

## Diagnosis 1 — "I post but get no reach"

**Signals (from native Insights):** most posts are photos/carousels; few Reels; low non-follower
reach; hooks start slow; some Reels still have a TikTok watermark.
**Broken stage:** **Reach.** Not enough Reels (the discovery engine), weak 3-second hooks, and the
watermark trips the originality gate.
**Prescription:**
- Shift the mix toward **Reels** for non-follower reach. → `reels-script`, `hook-writer`.
- **Remove TikTok watermarks**; post native, made-for-IG versions. → originality gate.
- Tighten the **niche** so the algorithm can categorize and recommend the account.
- Keep carousels for saves/authority, not as the reach engine.
→ Re-check **non-follower reach** and **watch time** in Insights in a few weeks.

## Diagnosis 2 — "Thousands of views, almost no new followers"

**Signals:** strong Reel reach and views; follower count barely moves; profile is a vague bio + a
generic name field.
**Broken stage:** **Follow-conversion.** Reach is fine; the **profile isn't converting** the visits.
**Prescription:** this isn't a content problem — it's the profile. → `profile-optimization` (the
3-second test, a searchable name field, a who/what/why bio, a goal-matched link, pinned posts). More
views won't help until the profile converts.

## Diagnosis 3 — "Decent followers, but nothing travels and they don't engage"

**Signals:** okay follower count; low **sends per reach**; quiet Stories; little community
interaction.
**Broken stage:** **Sends + retention.** Content gets seen but isn't *send-worthy*, and the warm
audience isn't being nurtured.
**Prescription:**
- Engineer **send-worthy** content (relatable / useful / conversation-starting). → `the-sends-lever.md`,
  `viral-reverse-engineering` (what already gets shared in the niche).
- Use **Stories** (polls, questions, replies) to deepen relationship and prompt DMs. → retention.
- Reply fast to comments/DMs (early signals on each new post).
→ Track **sends per reach** (aim past ~1–2%) and Story replies.

---

## Honest expectations (say this)

- **Growth compounds; it's not a hack.** One viral Reel doesn't build a business; a converting
  profile + send-worthy content + retention + consistency does.
- **Follower count is vanity.** Track **reach, sends-per-reach, saves, profile visits, and leads** —
  the things tied to actual outcomes.
- **No guarantees.** You can stack the odds (hook + sends + niche + profile), not guarantee virality.
- **No shortcuts.** Bought followers and pods are fake engagement that hurt reach and risk the
  account.
- **No WoopSocial analytics** — all of the above is read from **native Instagram Insights** the user
  pulls, or qualitative review.

---

## What the diagnoses share

- They name **one broken stage** of the loop, not a generic tip list.
- They **route to the specific skill** that fixes that stage.
- They center **Reels for reach** and **sends for travel**.
- They set **honest, compounding expectations** and **vanity-free metrics**.
