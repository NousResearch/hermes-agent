# The Growth Engine

How Instagram growth actually works in 2026 — the model everything else in this skill builds on. Get
this right and the tactics follow; get it wrong and you optimize the wrong things.

> Platform behavior changes — re-verify quarterly. The direction (discovery-first, sends + watch
> time, multiple algorithms) has been steady and confirmed by Instagram's own statements.

## Instagram is a discovery engine, not a follower feed

The single most important shift: the vast majority of reach now comes from **non-followers** via
**Reels and Explore** — roughly **94% of distribution is AI recommendations**, not your follower
graph. So **reach is earned per post, not borrowed from your follower count.** Follower count is a
lagging vanity number; **reach, sends, saves, and profile visits** are what move.

## The three signals that matter (confirmed by Instagram)

Across surfaces, Instagram's head has confirmed the top ranking signals:

1. **Watch time** — the single most important signal, especially on Reels. The **first ~3 seconds**
   decide whether a Reel is pushed wider or throttled. Completion and **rewatches** are strong.
2. **Sends per reach (DM shares)** — the dominant signal for **reaching new people**; weighted
   **~3–5× more than likes** for discovery. (Its own reference: `the-sends-lever.md`.)
3. **Likes per reach** — still counts, but matters more for **existing followers** than for
   discovery.

The practical read: **strong hook (watch time) + send-worthy content (sends)** is the combination
that travels.

## Multiple algorithms — one per surface

There isn't one algorithm; each surface ranks differently. Match the content to the surface's signal:

| Surface | What it rewards | Its job |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | watch time, rewatches, **sends**, audio fit, low skip | **discovery / growth** (mostly non-followers) |
| **Feed** | relationship, saves, comments, profile taps, time spent | reach **existing followers** |
| **Stories** | closeness, replies, recency | **retention / relationship** (followers only) |
| **Explore** | topic match, non-follower engagement velocity, freshness, **DM shares from non-followers** | **discovery** (all non-followers) |

A Reel can go wide on the Reels tab and barely show in Feed — that's not a contradiction, it's
different surfaces doing different jobs.

## Connected vs unconnected reach

Optimize the two reach types differently:

- **Unconnected (discovery / new people):** shareability (**sends**), hook strength, watch time,
  trending audio, niche clarity. This is where you *grow*. Newer accounts should skew **~70/30 toward
  unconnected-reach tactics** until they build an audience.
- **Connected (your followers):** consistency, community, fast replies, saves/comments, Stories
  relationship. This *retains* and warms the audience, which boosts early signals on new posts.

## The growth loop

```
Reels/Explore reach non-followers  →  strong hook holds them (watch time)
        →  content is send-worthy (sends)  →  Instagram pushes it wider
        →  viewer taps the profile  →  profile converts to a follow
        →  Stories/Feed/community deepen the relationship (retention)
        →  warm followers boost early signals on the next post  →  more reach
```

Every growth problem is a **specific broken stage** in this loop (see `format-and-diagnosis.md`).
Diagnose the stage; don't apply random tips.

## The eligibility gates (before ranking even happens)

To be eligible for recommendations (Explore, non-follower Reels), content must clear gates:

- **Original** — reposts get far less distribution; heavy reposting (e.g., 10+ in 30 days) can
  **exclude the account from recommendations** entirely.
- **No other-platform watermarks** (TikTok/CapCut logos) — a common, avoidable growth killer.
- **Has audio, under the length cap** (Reels up to ~3 min are now eligible for non-follower reach,
  though ~30–90s often performs best), and **suitable/safe** for a broad audience.

Made-for-Instagram, original, watermark-free is the price of entry for reach.

## Trial Reels (a free growth tool)

Instagram lets you post a **Trial Reel** shown **only to non-followers** first. If it performs with a
cold audience, push it to your followers; if not, you learn at no cost to your follower-facing stats.
Use it to test hooks/formats/topics risk-free.
