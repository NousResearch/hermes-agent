# The Sends Lever

If there's one Instagram-specific growth lever to obsess over in 2026, it's **sends** — DM shares.
This is Instagram's equivalent of TikTok rewatches or LinkedIn dwell time: the signal that
disproportionately drives reach to **new** people.

> Re-verify the weighting periodically, but the principle — sends drive discovery — is well
> established and confirmed by Instagram.

## Why sends matter so much

When someone **DMs your post to a friend**, Instagram treats it as the strongest possible quality
endorsement — a person vouched for your content to someone they care about. So:

- **Sends per reach are weighted ~3–5× higher than likes** for reaching non-followers.
- It's the dominant signal on the **discovery** surfaces (Reels, Explore).
- A useful benchmark: **~1–2% sends-per-reach is solid; ~3%+ can unlock much more non-follower
  distribution.** (Track *sends per reach*, not raw send counts.)

Chasing likes is optimizing the weak signal. **Chase sends.**

## What makes content send-worthy

People send content for the same reasons they share anything (see `viral-reverse-engineering` for
the deeper psychology). The send-worthy buckets on Instagram:

- **Relatable / "this is so us"** — content a person sends to a friend with "this is literally you."
  Recognition is the most reliable send trigger.
- **Useful / reference** — a checklist, framework, or how-to worth sending to a colleague ("you need
  this"). Doubles as a **save**.
- **Conversation starter** — a take or question someone forwards to spark a chat ("thoughts?").
- **Identity / values** — content that says what someone believes, sent to signal it.
- **Genuinely funny / surprising** — sent for the reaction.

Before posting, ask: **who sends this, to whom, and why?** If there's no clear answer, it'll get
views (maybe) but won't *travel*.

## Engineering sends into each format

- **Reels** — the hook earns the watch; a relatable or useful payoff earns the send. End on a line
  worth forwarding ("send this to the founder who needs it").
- **Carousels** — reference value (frameworks, checklists) drives sends + saves. A strong, shareable
  cover.
- **A soft prompt** can help ("share this with someone who…") — but only when the content already
  earns it; a prompt on weak content just looks needy.

## The two gates around sends

Sends don't work alone:

1. **Watch time / the hook (the gate before sends).** If the first ~3 seconds don't hold people, they
   never reach the send-worthy payoff. A weak hook caps everything. (Route to `hook-writer` /
   `reels-script`.)
2. **Saves (the cousin of sends).** Save-worthy reference content (how-tos, frameworks) signals
   return-value and often gets sent too. Package practical content as a **resource**, not a throwaway
   tip.

## Measure it honestly

Track **sends per reach** (and saves, watch time) in **native Instagram Insights** — WoopSocial has
no analytics surface. Use it to learn *which* content people share, then make more of that. Don't
present any of this as data WoopSocial supplies.
