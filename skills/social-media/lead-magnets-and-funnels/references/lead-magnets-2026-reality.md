# Lead magnets & funnels 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (formats/tools/benchmarks move).*

## The dead playbook
The **2018 gated-PDF play** (10-page guide → email gate → 12-email blast) now pulls a fraction of its
old leads. Two reasons: **AI assistants answer what PDFs used to** (no email required), and **ad-blockers
+ email apathy** send most form-fills to spam/ignore. A "12-page Ultimate Guide to X" underperforms what's
possible by **3–5×**. The problem usually isn't the funnel — **it's the magnet.**

## What works now: interactive, instant, specific
**Interactive / instant-result magnets** (quizzes, calculators, assessments, generators, specific
templates) convert **~4× static PDFs**. **AI-personalized checklists ≈ 11.4% conversion-to-sale** in a
30-day window vs **4.7%** for long-form PDF guides (CMI 2026 B2C report; attribute). The rules:
- **One audience, one problem, one quick win** — relevance is the single biggest conversion lever; broad
  = converts no one.
- **Instant, on-the-spot result** (a score/plan/recommendation) beats "check your inbox in 10 minutes."
- **Quick to consume, easy to implement, immediately valuable** — minutes/hours, not days.
- **Connects to the paid offer** — solves **one piece** of the larger puzzle so your product is the
  complete solution (so it **qualifies**, not just collects).
- **Specific, outcome-focused title** — e.g. "The 5-Email Sequence That Books Discovery Calls."

## Match the magnet to the funnel stage
| Stage | Magnet | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Top** (cold, list-building) | quiz, calculator, mini-course | low friction, high volume |
| **Mid** (engaged, qualify) | template, framework, benchmark report | higher commitment, qualifies |
| **Bottom** (sales-ready) | case study, product ROI calculator, consultation/audit | converts intent |
One magnet for all three stages is why conversion suffers. **Templates that win:** specific-task ("30/60/90
-day plan" not "productivity"), workflow-tied (Notion/Airtable/Sheets/Figma), industry-specific. **Original
data/benchmark reports compound for years.**

## Design the WHOLE funnel (not just the magnet)
```
benefit-first landing page -> short form -> instant success/thank-you page (sets expectations)
-> delivery email (resends the asset) -> 3-5 email nurture -> soft next step toward the offer
```
- **Short form:** email (or email + first name) only — **each extra field cuts conversion ~5–10%.** Native
  lead forms (Meta instant forms) = lower CPL but **lower quality**; landing pages = higher quality.
- **On-page video** tends to lift landing conversion (the oft-cited "up to 86%" figure is a decade old —
  treat as directional and A/B your own); **multi-step conversational forms +49.3%** completion.

## The nurture (value-first, behavior-branched)
**3–5 emails** is ideal (longer can convert even better): **deliver the asset → one quick win → proof/
social proof → soft next step.** Skimmable, outcome-driven, links relevant to the magnet topic. **Branch
on behavior** (opens/clicks/page-visits), not just calendar drip. Don't pitch early — value-first keeps
unsubscribes low. **Automated campaigns ≈ 2,361%** higher conversion than non-automated; **nurtured leads
= 50% more sales-ready at 33% less cost**; **79% of leads never convert without follow-up.**

## Economics + tools (advisory)
**Create once, ~zero marginal cost per lead** (the 1,000th lead costs the same as the first). Tools (all
external): magnet/quiz builders (Magnetly, Typeform), landing builders (Replo), ESPs (MailerLite/Mailchimp/
Sender/Kit), giveaways (Viral Loops/Vyper). **No dark patterns** — real value, honest proof, visible opt-out.
