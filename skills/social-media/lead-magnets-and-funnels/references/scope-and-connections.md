# Scope, distinctions + connections

lead-magnets-and-funnels is the **offer-design layer** of the monetization cluster — it designs the
magnet the capture paths deliver and the whole social→capture→nurture funnel that ties the cluster
together. The agent **designs + drafts**; **WoopSocial has no landing/form/email/quiz builder** — it
publishes the **promo posts**; capture/delivery/nurture run in **external tools**.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **WoopSocial has no landing-page / form / quiz / email-automation builder.** Those are **external**
  (landing/quiz builders + an ESP), **human-set-up.** The agent **designs the magnet + full funnel** and
  **drafts** the nurture sequence, landing copy, and promo posts; **WoopSocial publishes the promo/trigger
  posts** that drive signups.
- **Metrics from the external tools + GA4** (signups/clicks/conversions) — **never fabricate** a number or
  fake social proof.
- **Real value, no dark patterns** — no bait-and-switch "magnets," **no fake/resetting urgency**, **visible
  unsubscribe**, **clear (un-prechecked) consent** (**GDPR / CAN-SPAM / FTC**). Value-first nurture; don't
  pitch early.
- **Magnet must connect to the paid offer** (or, if there's no offer yet, it's audience-building — keep it
  light). **No bought lists.**

## Distinct from its siblings
- **lead-magnets-and-funnels (this)** — **designs the magnet + the social→capture→nurture funnel.**
- **link-in-bio-and-traffic** / **social-selling-and-dm** — the **capture PATHS** (bio link / DM) that
  **deliver** the magnet.
- **email-and-newsletter** — the **owned channel / ongoing nurture** the funnel feeds into (the ESP-side
  relationship).
- **goals-and-kpis** — sets the conversion target the funnel serves; **analytics-and-reporting** measures it.
- **content-calendar**/**campaign-and-launch-planning** — schedule the promo push.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (audience/offer), `goals-and-kpis` (the conversion goal).
- **Magnet creative:** `image-prompt`/`ideogram`/`nano-banana` (covers/visuals), `carousel-writer` (a carousel
  magnet), `caption-writer`/`hook-writer` (promo + landing copy), `story-writer` (Story promo).
- **Capture paths (deliver the magnet):** `link-in-bio-and-traffic`, `social-selling-and-dm`.
- **Nurture / owned channel:** `email-and-newsletter` (the 3–5 sequence lives + runs here, in the ESP).
- **Promote + publish:** `content-calendar`, `campaign-and-launch-planning`, `scheduling-and-queue →
  WoopSocial` (promo posts only).
- **Measure/test:** `analytics-and-reporting` (external-tool/GA4 readout), `goals-and-kpis`,
  `experimentation-and-ab-testing` (A/B the magnet, landing, subject lines). **The funnel itself runs in external tools.**
