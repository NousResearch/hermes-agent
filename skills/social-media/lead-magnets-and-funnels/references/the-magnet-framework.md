# The MAGNET framework — design the magnet AND the funnel

A lead magnet is the first touch in a relationship, not a destination — and the magnet alone is half the
job. MAGNET designs both: a specific, instant-value magnet tied to your offer, wrapped in a whole
social→capture→nurture funnel. The agent designs + drafts; **WoopSocial has no landing/form/email/quiz
builder** — it publishes the **promo posts**; capture/delivery/nurture run in **external tools**.

## M — Match to one audience + one problem
- **One audience, one problem, one quick win.** Pull the pain from `brand-profile`/audience research; the
  goal from `goals-and-kpis`. Broad = converts no one.

## A — Anchor to the paid offer + the stage
- The magnet **solves one piece** of the larger puzzle the **paid offer** completes (so it qualifies). **Match
  to stage:** top = quiz/calculator; mid = template/benchmark; bottom = case study/ROI calculator.

## G — Give an instant, interactive result
- Prefer **interactive/instant-result** formats (quiz, calculator, assessment, generator, specific template)
  — ~4× a static PDF; **an on-the-spot result beats "check your inbox."** Quick to consume, immediately usable.

## N — Name it for the outcome
- **Specific, outcome-focused title** ("The Pricing Calculator: see what to charge in 60s"), not "The
  Ultimate Guide to X."

## E — Engineer the whole funnel
- **benefit-first landing → short form (email / +first name only) → instant success page → delivery email
  → 3–5 email nurture.** Each extra form field −5–10%; on-page video helps (directional — A/B it). Map the
  path before building.

## T — Tend the nurture (value-first, behavior-branched)
- **Deliver → quick win → proof → soft next step.** **Branch on behavior** (opens/clicks/visits), not just
  drip. Don't pitch early. Measure signups/clicks/conversions from the external tools + GA4 — **never fabricate.**

## The funnel brief a request should fill
```
AUDIENCE + PROBLEM: one reader, one pain, one quick win (tied to a KPI)
MAGNET: stage-matched format + instant result; connects to the paid offer; outcome-focused title
FUNNEL: landing (benefit-first) -> short form -> success page -> delivery email -> 3-5 nurture
NURTURE: deliver -> quick win -> proof -> soft next step; branch on behavior
PROMO: the social posts that drive signups (WoopSocial publishes); capture path = link-in-bio / DM
TOOLS + METRICS: external builder/ESP (human-set-up); signups/conversions from those + GA4 (never fabricated)
```
