# linkedin-company-pages

The **company-page (LINKEDIN_PAGES) strategy** — treat the page as a **credibility hub + paid launchpad** and
build the real organic engine, **employee advocacy.** The agent **strategizes + designs the advocacy program
+ drafts page posts + drafts shareable content**; **WoopSocial publishes the page's own posts**; **employees
post from their own profiles, and the first-hour engagement, paid, and native features are human/native.**

## The POV: a credibility hub + paid launchpad, not the reach engine
LinkedIn **structurally rebalanced** the feed — **company pages get ~1–5% of feed allocation** (personal
profiles ~65%) and **page organic reach fell 60–66% since 2024.** So the page's real jobs are **credibility,
the official record, recruitment, and the paid launchpad** (LinkedIn Ads + **Thought Leader Ads** run
exclusively from it) — **not** organic reach. **People connect with people, not logos:** the algorithm trusts
individuals over brands, so the organic growth engine is **employee advocacy** (employees have ~10× the
connections; ~3% who share drive ~30% of engagement; employee leads convert ~7×). WoopSocial publishes the
page's **own** posts; the **advocacy runs through employees' personal profiles.**

## The framework: HUMAN
(Depth: `references/the-human-framework.md`.)
- **H — Hub, not megaphone:** the page = credibility hub + paid launchpad (Ads/Thought Leader Ads,
  announcements, recruitment, Follow button) — not the reach engine; set honest expectations.
- **U — Unleash employee advocacy:** the primary organic lever (people not logos; ~10× connections, 3% →
  30%, ~5× leads); a **frictionless program** (shareable content + suggested commentary employees
  **personalize, not identical**, Notify Employees, a pilot, light gamification, leadership math); employees
  post from their **own profiles** (→ `linkedin-growth`).
- **M — Move in the first hour:** employees engaging within ~60 min is a strong reach signal; coordinate;
  executive/CEO content ~4× the page.
- **A — Anchor page-appropriate content:** carousel/document (top saves), short vertical video w/ captions,
  custom images — **never text-only** (~0.42×); **links in comments, not body** (−60%); questions/frameworks
  → 437% views + saves (Depth Score). (Post craft → `linkedin-post-writer`; visuals → `ideogram`/`nano-banana`.)
- **N — Nurture cadence + measure:** consistent (weekly+) → ~5.6× followers; respond fast; track followers,
  reach, engagement, **advocacy UTM conversions**; no bait/pods/forced-identical/bought followers.

## The reality (verify-quarterly)
Structural rebalance (pages ~1–5% feed, reach −60–66%); personal 5–8× engagement (conservative Sprout ~4.7%
vs ~1–2%); people-not-logos (3× trust, 92% buyers trust employees); the page's real jobs + the paid launchpad
(Ads/Thought Leader Ads exclusive); content assignment (page vs personal vs both); employee advocacy = the
lever (10× connections, 3%→30%, 2.75×/5×, 7× conversion, first-hour signal, CEO 4×, program design); page
content (carousel/document, short video, custom image, no text-only 0.42×, links-in-comments −60%, questions
437%, saves, Depth Score); cadence (weekly 5.6×, 150-follower threshold); native features (Notify Employees/
Showcase/Events/Newsletters/Thought-Leader-Ads/BrandWorks): `references/company-pages-2026-reality.md`. The

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** strategizes the page, **designs the advocacy program**, **drafts page posts**, and **drafts
  shareable advocacy content + commentary.**
- **WoopSocial publishes** the **company page's OWN posts** (`LINKEDIN_PAGES`: text + media + optional `link`;
  **no native document/carousel/poll field**) on a **schedule**.
- **WoopSocial does NOT:** post from **employees' personal profiles** (the growth engine — **human**, →
  `linkedin-growth`), do the **first-hour engagement** (human), run **Ads / Thought Leader Ads** (native
  paid), build **native document/carousel** posts (native), or run **Notify Employees / Showcase / Events /
  Newsletters / Page analytics** (native).
- **No engagement-bait/pods/forced-identical/bought followers**; **links in comments, not body**; **organic
  page reach is genuinely low** (page = credibility + paid launchpad); **never fabricate metrics.** (Scope,
  distinctions + connections: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)
