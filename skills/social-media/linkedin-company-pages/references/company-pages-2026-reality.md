# LinkedIn company pages 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (LinkedIn ships algorithm + product changes often).*

## The POV: the page is a credibility hub + paid launchpad, not the reach engine
LinkedIn **structurally rebalanced** the feed: **company pages get ~1–5% of feed allocation** (personal
profiles ~65%), and **company-page organic reach fell 60–66% (2024→2026)** — page posts reach only **~1.6–5%
of followers.** **Personal profiles outperform pages by ~5–8× engagement** (headline GaggleAMP figures cite
~561% reach / 5×; the conservative independent benchmark — **Sprout Q1 2026 — is ~4.7% personal vs ~1–2%
company**, directionally the same). **People connect with people, not logos:** the algorithm **trusts
individuals over brands** (personal = authentic voice; company = marketing agenda); **trust in personal
content is ~3× higher**; **92% of B2B buyers trust employee recommendations over advertising.**

## What the page is irreplaceably FOR
**Credibility/legitimacy** (buyers check it), the **official record** (announcements), **recruitment/career
pages**, the **Follow button + website plugin**, **product pages**, **Page analytics** — and critically the
**paid launchpad:** **LinkedIn Ads run exclusively from company pages**, and **Thought Leader Ads** (promote
a *personal* profile's post with paid targeting) require the page. **Content assignment:** announcements /
jobs / paid assets → **page**; thought leadership / opinions / stories → **personal**; product & case studies
→ **both.**

## Employee advocacy = the primary organic lever
- **Employees collectively have ~10× the company's connections.** **Only ~3% of employees share, yet drive
  ~30% of company engagement.** Employee posts get **~2.75× impressions / ~5× engagement** vs the page
  (despite ~46% fewer followers); **employee-sourced leads convert ~7×**, and activating employee networks
  yields **~5× more leads** than the page alone. **CEO/exec content ~4×** an average page post.
- **First-hour signal:** employees engaging with a page post **within ~60 min** reads as a strong quality
  signal and **extends reach.**
- **Run the program right:** leadership buy-in via the math (20 employees × 500 connections = 10,000 reach vs
  5,000 followers); **frictionless** (provide shareable content + suggested commentary employees
  **personalize** — a personal opener/opinion/question dramatically lifts performance; **never identical
  copy**); **pilot** of natural advocates; light **gamification**; LinkedIn's **Notify Employees** for
  high-priority posts. The employees post **from their own profiles** (→ `linkedin-growth`).

## Page content that still works organically
- **Carousel/document posts** (top **saves** — saves ~5× a like) and **short vertical video (<90s) with
  captions** (+80% reach); **custom images get 2× more comments.** **Text-only is dead on pages (~0.42×
  reach).** **External links in the body cut reach ~60% → put links in comments.** **Questions / structured
  frameworks** that spark multi-threaded discussion get **437% more views.** **Depth Score (2026):** dwell
  time, comment depth, saves, private shares — not clicks. **Engagement-bait suppressed; pods → shadowban;
  hashtags minimal (3–5).**

## Cadence, growth + native features
- **Consistency (weekly+) → ~5.6× more followers**; respond to comments fast; the **first 60 min** matters; a
  **150-follower threshold** accelerates growth ~9×. **Native features:** Notify Employees, Showcase Pages,
  Events, Newsletters, Products, the Follow plugin, Page analytics, Thought Leader Ads, and **Creator
  Marketplace / BrandWorks** (B2B creator discovery, launched June 2026). **Track:** followers, reach,
  engagement, and **advocacy reach + UTM-tracked conversions** (prove ROI).
