# linkedin-growth

The **LinkedIn personal-profile growth engine** — the profile as a landing page, what to post and how
often, strategic commenting on target accounts, the golden hour, newsletters/video, and connections
vs followers. The agent drafts posts + comment copy and designs the system; a human does the live
commenting/connecting; **scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial** publishes the posts. Complements
**linkedin-post-writer** (the single-post craft) and **linkedin-company-pages** (the page).

## The POV: dwell + comments, on a personal profile
LinkedIn's algorithm rewards **dwell time** (people actually reading) and **early, meaningful
comments** — likes barely move distribution, and engagement bait is down-ranked. The feed is
structurally tilted to **people, not logos** (personal profiles get ~65% of feed allocation; pages
~1–5%), external **links in the body suppress reach** (put them in the first comment), and posts now
have a **long shelf life** via suggested feeds — a good post travels for days or weeks. The cheapest
growth lever isn't posting more: it's **thoughtful comments on bigger accounts in your niche**, which
put your name in front of their audience daily. And in a feed where ~half the long posts are
AI-written, **a specific, lived, first-person POV is the differentiator** — not volume.

## The framework: LADDER
(Depth: `references/the-ladder-framework.md`.)
- **L — Landing-page profile:** headline = value proposition (not job title); About opens with the
  3 visible lines; Featured = the CTA; Follow-primary once inbound outpaces outbound. Every post's
  job ends at a profile visit — the profile converts it. → **profile-optimization**.
- **A — Author for dwell:** win the "…see more" tap, format for the read, mix text / document
  carousel / native video / newsletter; **link in the first comment**. → **linkedin-post-writer**.
- **D — Deploy strategic comments:** 5–10 substantive comments/day on target accounts in the niche —
  the afterburner. A comment is content, not a courtesy. → **reply-and-comment-writer**.
- **D — Drive the golden hour:** first 60–90 min decides reach; post in the audience window, reply
  to every comment, comment around your own post time. Max **1 post/day**, 3–5/week.
- **E — Expand with native surfaces:** newsletter (notification + email push), native video (the
  2026 push), audio events; SSI as a directional health check, never the goal.
- **R — Relationships & network hygiene:** connections (capped, ~100 invites/week) vs followers
  (unlimited); no-pitch invites; DMs warm, not pitch-slaps. → **social-selling-and-dm**.

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The engagement core is human** — the agent **drafts** posts, comment copy, invite notes, and DM
  openers; a human **posts** the live comments, sends invites, and holds the conversations. No
  auto-commenting, no auto-connect tooling (a restriction risk on LinkedIn specifically).
- **WoopSocial schedules the posts** (text + media + optional link; native document carousels,
  polls, newsletters, and audio events are posted/run natively). No update → delete + recreate. For
  link-in-first-comment, the agent supplies post + comment; the pair is posted by tool/human.
- **No analytics in WoopSocial** → read **native LinkedIn analytics** (impressions, members reached,
  profile viewers, followers) and **SSI** as a directional check. **Never fabricate metrics.**
- **No engagement bait, pods, mass-tagging, bought followers, or broetry theatre** — down-ranked by
  the algorithm and quietly judged by the exact professionals you're trying to reach.
  (Scope, distinctions + connections: `references/scope-and-connections.md`.)

## Definition of done
A profile audited as a landing page (headline = value prop, Featured = CTA, Follow-primary decision
made); a 3–5 posts/week plan, max 1/day, with links in the first comment and format mix mapped to
dwell; a named list of 10–20 target accounts with a 5–10 comments/day playbook (drafted by the
agent, posted by a human); a golden-hour routine (audience window, reply-to-every-comment); native
surfaces decided deliberately (newsletter yes/no, video cadence); connection-request hygiene with
no-pitch invite copy; posts routed to scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial with live engagement flagged
human; metrics from native LinkedIn analytics + SSI directionally; no bait/pods/bought followers/
broetry; correctly distinguished from linkedin-post-writer and linkedin-company-pages.
