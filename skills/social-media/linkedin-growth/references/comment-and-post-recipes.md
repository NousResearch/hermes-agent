# Post types, the commenting playbook + two worked examples

## Native post types (in rough 2026 order of favor)
| Type | When | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Document/PDF carousel | framework, checklist, breakdown | highest dwell + saves; slides → `carousel-writer`; posted natively |
| Native video (<90s, captioned) | POV to camera, demo | LinkedIn's current push; → `talking-head-and-piece-to-camera` |
| Text: story → lesson | something that actually happened | ~1,800–2,100 chars if it earns the read |
| Text: contrarian POV | a take you'll defend | close on a genuine question, not "Agree?" |
| Text + image | proof, data, before/after | image lifts the stop, text does the work |
| Newsletter edition | recurring theme, depth | notification + email push; created natively |
| Poll | a genuinely contested question | engagement yes, follow-conversion weak |

**Link rule:** external links go in the **first comment** ("link in comments"), never the body.
**Cadence:** 3–5/week, max 1/day. Post craft → `linkedin-post-writer`.

## The commenting playbook (the afterburner)
1. **Build the target list (10–20 accounts):** ~60% bigger voices in your niche, ~30% peers,
   ~10% dream clients/partners. Turn on the bell for the big ones.
2. **5–10 substantive comments/day**, ideally within the first hour of their post going live. Each
   comment adds something: a number from your experience, a respectful counterpoint, a sharper
   example, a question that extends the idea. 2–4 sentences. Never "Great post! 🙌".
3. Your **headline travels with every comment** — that's the impression it makes. (Fix the headline
   first: LADDER step L.)
4. After a few real exchanges, **connect with a no-pitch note** referencing the conversation.
5. Drafts → `reply-and-comment-writer`; the daily block → `engagement-routine`; **a human posts
   every comment** — automation risks restriction.

## The golden-hour routine
15 min before posting: leave 2–3 comments on target accounts → post in the audience window →
reply to **every** comment for 60–90 min, ending replies on questions to reopen threads → back at
the 3-hour mark for stragglers. A strong post keeps resurfacing for days — keep answering.

## Connection-request hygiene
~100 invites/week max; spend them on people you've already engaged. Note optional when context is
obvious (they replied to you), otherwise one specific sentence. **Never pitch in the invite or the
first message after accepting** — warm the relationship in public first (→ `social-selling-and-dm`).

## Worked example 1 — consultant with no reach (fix: comments before posts)
```
DIAGNOSIS: 2 posts/week into a cold network; headline = "Managing Director"; zero commenting.
PLAN: Headline → "Pricing strategy for B2B SaaS | I help founders stop discounting" (profile-optimization).
COMMENTS: 12 target accounts (5 big SaaS voices, 5 peers, 2 dream clients); 7/day drafted from
their fresh posts — each adds a pricing data point or counterexample.
POSTS: 3/week — Mon carousel ("The 4 discount traps"), Wed story→lesson, Fri contrarian POV.
Links (case study) in first comment. Golden hour blocked in calendar.
EXPECT: profile visits + follower growth from week 2-3 via comments; post reach compounds after.
```

## Worked example 2 — founder launching a newsletter (warm audience, expertise moment)
```
TRIGGER: new EU regulation hits their industry (trend-jacking moment).
POST (text, no link): "Everyone's panicking about [regulation]. For companies under 50 people,
only one clause actually bites -- here's the two-week fix we're running." Ends: "What's your
counsel telling you? Genuinely curious what advice is circulating."
GOLDEN HOUR: founder answers every comment personally; best question becomes Thursday's post.
FOLLOW-ON: launches the LinkedIn newsletter ("The [industry] Brief") on the spike -- LinkedIn
pushes subscribe prompts to the network; the first-comment link points to the lead magnet
(lead-magnets-and-funnels). Off-platform list mirrored via email-and-newsletter.
```

Both: read `brand-profile` + `voice-builder` first; person not page; links in first comment; human
does the live engagement; posts scheduled via `scheduling-and-queue` → WoopSocial; results read from
native LinkedIn analytics — never fabricated.
