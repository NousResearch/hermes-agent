# The LADDER framework — LinkedIn growth is a system, not a posting habit

LinkedIn rewards **dwell time and meaningful comments** on **personal profiles**, and its posts have
a long shelf life — so growth comes from a small number of strong posts, a daily commenting practice
on target accounts, and a profile that converts the resulting visits. LADDER is the 2026 engine.

## L — Landing-page profile
Every post's job ends at a **profile visit**; the profile converts it or wastes it.
- **Headline = value proposition** ("I help X do Y" / the specific expertise), not a job title —
  it follows you into every comment you leave.
- **About** opens with the ~3 lines visible before the fold; **Featured** holds the one CTA
  (lead magnet, newsletter, site → `link-in-bio-and-traffic`).
- **Follow-primary** once inbound outpaces outbound connecting. Craft → `profile-optimization`;
  keywords → `social-seo`.

## A — Author for dwell
- 3–5 posts/week, **max 1/day**. Win the "…see more" tap (~140 chars), format for the phone read,
  close on a genuine question. Craft → `linkedin-post-writer`, `hook-writer`.
- Format mix by job: **document carousels** (authority + saves), **native video** (the 2026 push),
  **text** (POV/story), occasional poll. **Link in the first comment, never the body.**
- **Topic consistency** — the algorithm must be able to classify what you're the person for.

## D — Deploy strategic comments (the afterburner)
- Pick **10–20 target accounts**: bigger voices in the niche + peers + dream clients' feeds.
- Leave **5–10 substantive comments/day** — add a data point, a counterpoint, a lived example;
  never "Great post!". Your headline travels with every comment into *their* audience.
- Comment early on fresh posts from large accounts. Drafts → `reply-and-comment-writer`; the daily
  block → `engagement-routine`. A human posts them — no automation.

## D — Drive the golden hour
- The first **60–90 minutes** decide initial distribution. Post in the audience's window (weekday
  working hours for most B2B — treat as guidance, not analytics).
- **Reply to every comment** in that window, with replies that reopen the thread (end on a
  question). Be active commenting 15 min before and after your own post — warm accounts get warmer
  starts.

## E — Expand with native surfaces
- **Newsletter** — the notification + email push makes it the best owned-audience feature; launch
  when you have a repeatable theme and ~1k+ followers (pairs with `email-and-newsletter`).
- **Native video** weekly if capacity allows (→ `talking-head-and-piece-to-camera`); **audio
  events** for warm-audience depth. Skip collaborative-article badge-chasing.
- Check **SSI** monthly as a directional gauge — never optimize for the number.

## R — Relationships & network hygiene
- **Connections** (30k cap, ~100 invites/week) = the two-way network; **followers** (unlimited) =
  the audience. Grow followers with content; spend invites on people you've already engaged.
- Invite hygiene: comment first, connect second; short specific note when context isn't obvious;
  **no pitch in the request**; withdraw stale invites.
- DMs continue conversations that already started (→ `social-selling-and-dm`) — a pitch-slap after
  an accept undoes the whole system.

## The LinkedIn-growth brief a request should fill
```
FIT: is LinkedIn a priority (B2B, services, hiring, thought leadership)? Person, not page.
PROFILE: headline = value prop; Featured = CTA; Follow-primary decision made
POSTS: 3-5/week, max 1/day; format mix (text / carousel / video / newsletter); link in first comment
COMMENTS: 10-20 target accounts; 5-10 substantive comments/day, drafted by agent, posted by human
GOLDEN HOUR: audience window; reply to every comment in 60-90 min
NETWORK: invite hygiene (no pitch, ~100/week); newsletter yes/no; SSI directional only
```
