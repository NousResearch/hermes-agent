# The RANKS framework — lists & roundups that earn the slot

A list ranks things, so the skill must rank *honestly* and make every entry earn its place. Each letter is a
gate; weak lists fail at R (padded premise) or N (filler entries).

## R — Right-size the premise
A list people actually search/want, with a **number that serves the content, not a target to hit.** Don't pad to
"25" and don't starve a great list at 5 — the count follows the genuinely-good items. Pick the type that fits:
"best X for [specific Y]", "N ways to [outcome]", expert roundup, product/tool roundup, or a curated link
roundup. The title states the payoff up front (number + promise + who it's for). One premise, one clear promise.

## A — Assemble honestly
Curate real, vetted items and **rank by genuine merit against stated criteria** (say how you judged). The
non-negotiables:
- **No fabricated items, no fake quotes, no invented rankings.**
- **Disclose** affiliate/sponsor/own-product (FTC); don't rank by commission; don't auto-#1 yourself — an
  independent, criteria-based ranking is what compounds and gets cited.
- For roundups: **real contributors, with consent**; the human does outreach, the agent drafts the ask.
- Verify product facts (pricing/specs/claims) — the human checks; the agent doesn't guess.

## N — Nail every entry
Each item is a mini-payload that earns its slot: **the what + the why-it-matters + one specific detail** (a
result, a "Best For" tag, a real example). Cut filler — the **weakest entry sets the perceived quality** of the
whole list. Outcome-anchored beats generic ("the tool we used to do X", not "a good tool"). Name entities
explicitly (tools/brands/people) — it's what both readers and AI extract.

## K — Keep the scroll moving
Order for impact: lead with the best or most-surprising item (or a deliberate arc that builds), front-load the
hook (hands to `hook-writer`), and keep it **ruthlessly scannable** — the format's entire advantage is
skimmability. Consistent structure per entry. A summary line or quick table aids both humans and AI extraction.

## S — Spread + atomize
One list = many cuts, and roundups have built-in reach:
- **Atomize:** whole list → carousel/thread; **1 item = 1 standalone post**; top 3 → short video.
- **Roundups:** contributors amplify → route to `collabs-and-cross-promotion`.
- **"Best of":** engineer for AI citation + place for search; refresh on a cadence (lists decay).
- Route mechanics to the format writers; **WoopSocial publishes**; measure saves/shares + clicks — never a
  fabricated metric or ranking.
