# Workflows, checklists & worked examples

## The weekly episode → shorts loop
Trim the source (pre-show/dead air out — credits bill on source minutes) → upload with genre + length + negative
prompts set → candidates return scored → triage sort → human three-question pass (stands alone? fair? serves?) →
edit top 3-5 / approve clean mid-scorers / discard ~40% without guilt → brand template across the batch →
captions accuracy pass → export + download promptly → drip-schedule → WoopSocial → weekly: real retention vs
scores, recalibrate.

## The per-clip QA checklist (before anything publishes)
Hook's first line lands ✓ · caption errors fixed (names/jargon) ✓ · reframe drift checked (subject in frame) ✓ ·
boundaries clean (no mid-word starts) ✓ · context fair to anyone quoted ✓ · brand template applied ✓ · music =
licensed or added natively at publish (auto-music off) ✓ · right aspect ratio per destination ✓.

## The credit-math worksheet (before subscribing)
Monthly source minutes = episodes × avg length (trimmed). Add +1/post if direct-posting to X. Compare to tier
minutes (≈ Free 60 / Starter 150 / Pro 300 — verify in-app). A weekly 60-min show ≈ 240 min/mo → Starter won't
cover it; Pro annual (~$14.50/mo) is the documented value pick. Editor gating conflicts across sources — confirm
in-app before paying.

## Score recalibration (monthly)
Log each published clip: its score vs its real 3s-hold/watch-through. After ~20 clips you'll know how predictive
the score is for YOUR content — most creators find it useful for ranking, unreliable as prophecy. Adjust how much
triage weight it gets; never let it auto-decide.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (the triage discipline)
"60-minute episode. opus gave me 11 clips. old me would've edited all 11. new me: sorted by score, edited the top
4, approved 3 clean mid-scorers untouched, binned 4 — including a 91 that made my guest sound like he was
endorsing something he spent ten minutes criticizing. the score doesn't know context. i do." — triage not truth,
the fairness catch, ~40% binned.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (the drip)
"One webinar became nine little clips — we didn't post them all at once. Two a week, each one read through first
(the captions spelled a client's name three different ways, bless them), our template on every one, and the music
swapped for a licensed track. Six weeks of gentle presence from one afternoon's recording. One small step: before
your next upload, trim the housekeeping off the front — the meter runs on what goes in, not what comes out." —
QA pass, drip cadence, the credit tip.

## Never
Auto-post unreviewed clips · treat the score as a guarantee or auto-delete threshold · ship a clip that
misrepresents a speaker · skip the caption accuracy pass · feed burned-in-subtitle video · leave auto-music on
for commercial posts without checking rights · process untrimmed sources · assume the editor/API is on your tier
without checking · leave exports undownloaded near renewal · claim WoopSocial clips video.
