# Pinterest 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly.*

## The mindset: a visual search engine, not social media
Pinterest works like **Google for visuals.** Users arrive **with intent** (planning purchases,
projects, recipes, decor) — high purchase intent. Discovery is driven by **keywords, relevance, and
visual similarity — not who you follow.** So **followers matter far less than keyword structure and
fresh pin designs**; small/new accounts can win on strong SEO + creative. (631M MAU, Q1 2026.)

## Evergreen + compounding (the superpower)
Pins last **months to years** (vs 24–48h on IG/TikTok). A well-optimized pin keeps surfacing in search
weeks/months later; old pins **regain relevance** when demand returns. You're building a **long-term
searchable traffic asset base.** Honest: traction usually takes **3–6 months**, then compounds.

## Fresh pins are the #1 rule
Pinterest **heavily favors fresh content** — a new pin gets an early visibility boost and a few-day
**test window** that determines long-term reach. "Fresh" = a **new image/layout/text overlay**, not a
repin. Make **3–5 visually distinct designs per destination** (each counts as fresh; the top 1% of
pins drive ~50% of traffic, so distinct designs raise your odds). **Fresh pin designs drive ~90% of
website traffic; saves/repins now have minimal ranking impact (2026).**

## Keywords / SEO = the top ranking factor
Pinterest scans **pin title, description, board name, alt text, image file name, and linked domain.**
Favor **long-tail, high-intent** phrases; 2026 uses **semantic matching** (entities/clusters), so
**clarity beats cleverness** — make it unmistakably about a real search query. Research with **Pinterest
Trends/Predicts, autocomplete, related pins** (ties to `social-seo`). **Hashtags are nearly dead
(~1% of ranking power)** — deprioritize; 2–5 max if any.

## Boards = topic clusters
Name boards with **specific, keyword-driven themes** ("Eco-Friendly Gift Ideas for Men", not
"Miscellaneous"). Boards are **cluster/entity signals**; pick **one content pillar at a time**, build
themed boards under it, and pin **3–7 fresh pins/week per cluster.** Wrong cluster authority takes
months to correct — structure it right from the start.

## Specs + formats
- **2:3 ratio (1000×1500) is essential** — other ratios are **penalized** (official guidance). Add a
  **keyword text overlay.**
- **Static pins** → most **traffic** (link out). **Video pins** (9:16, **6–15s**) → ~**2× engagement.**
  **Idea Pins are retired** — Pinterest merged everything into one unified Pin format (old Idea Pins
  became video pins), so **every pin can carry an outbound link now.** Collage/Rich/Product pins for
  shopping.

## Links — the differentiator
Unlike X/IG, **Pinterest rewards outbound links** ("free clicks"). Link to a **relevant** product/blog/
landing page that delivers on the pin — **not your homepage** (users expect value after the click).

## Cadence + seasonal
**Steady, not bursts:** ~**3–10 fresh pins/day** (new accounts 3–5; established 5–10). **Don't** mass-pin
the same URL/board at once — **space ~72h between pins to the same URL** (spam filter). **Seasonal:
launch 45–90 days before peak** (45% of users plan 60+ days ahead). Evergreen builds the library;
seasonal captures the wave.

## Fit + AI
Strongest for **fashion, home/decor, DIY, food, beauty, weddings, lifestyle, e-commerce, gift guides,
blog traffic.** Pinterest uses **GenAI** (custom product backgrounds, recommendations) and lets users
**filter GenAI content** — disclose AI imagery. **FTC disclosure** required on affiliate pins.
