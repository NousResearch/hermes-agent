# Pinterest SEO 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (search features + tools move).*

## The POV: a visual search engine — keywords + relevance rank you, not followers
Pinterest is **"Google with pictures"** — a visual search engine (**631M MAU, Q1 2026**, **DA 94**, and pins
also rank in **Google image/web results**). **~97% of top searches are unbranded** (users search for what you
sell without knowing who sells it). **Follower count barely matters; search optimization is everything.**
Pins have a **~4-month lifespan** (some 3–6 mo), so one optimized pin **compounds traffic for months** — ideal
for **evergreen** (recipes, decor, gift guides, tutorials).

## Ranking factors
**Domain quality + Pin quality + Pinner quality + relevance (keywords)** must align. **Engagement signals:
saves and long clicks (30+ sec on the destination) are the most important** (saves > clicks > views;
scroll-past / hide / quick-bounce = negative). Pinterest reads **sequences of saves/clicks** to predict the
user's journey and serve related pins.

## Keyword research (the foundation)
- **Pinterest's own search-bar autocomplete** — type a seed; every suggestion is a real query. **Guided
  Search bubbles** (the coloured pills under results — "modern," "budget," "for beginners," "2026") =
  long-tail modifiers, less competition. **Pinterest Trends (trends.pinterest.com) + Predicts** = seasonal
  search volume + emerging trends.
- **Long-tail (3+ words)** beats broad ("easy dinner recipes for families" > "easy dinners"). **Think like a
  searcher**, not a marketer.

## Where keywords go (keyword layering)
- **Profile:** broad niche keywords in name + bio. **Boards:** keyword-rich **titles + descriptions**,
  specific not cutesy ("Minimalist Home Office Ideas," not "Inspo Board"). **Board semantic alignment matters
  more in 2026** — a precise board ("Keto Meal Prep for Beginners") gives a pin far stronger ranking context
  than a generic one ("Healthy Food"); save fresh pins to the relevant board first.
- **Pin:** lead the **title** with the primary keyword — `[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Benefit] | [Context]`
  (≤100). A natural keyword-rich **description** (1–2 key phrases early, ~100–200 chars, **no stuffing**).
  Plus **on-image/overlay text** (OCR-read), **alt text**, and a descriptive **image file name**.
- **Alignment: Pin → Board → Page all reinforce the same keyword phrase** (clarity → ranking). **Rich Pins**
  (pull live data from your site; require a **claimed site** + tag) boost trust/CTR.
- **Hashtags are deprioritized** — keywords carry the ranking weight; at most 1–2 niche tags (or skip).

## Fresh pins, clusters + cadence
- **Fresh, keyword-optimized pins** (new images/URLs, **not repins/dupes**) — each is a **new chance to rank
  for a new term.** 3–5/day is strong (top accounts 11–12); **volume without unique design/copy gets
  suppressed** (quality scales with quantity or not at all).
- **Publish related pins in clusters** so Pinterest connects the journey. **Seasonal: post 45–90 days ahead**
  (users plan; time it with Trends). **Over-optimization is recognized + suppressed** — keep it natural.
