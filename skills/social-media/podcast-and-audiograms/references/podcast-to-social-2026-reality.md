# Podcast → social 2026 — verified

*Volatile (tools/pricing move). Re-verify quarterly at each tool's site.*

## The POV: a podcast is long-form; social is short, visual, sound-off
The skill isn't "make a podcast" — it's the **audio→social pipeline:** turn the best **30–90 second** moments
into **branded, captioned** clips/audiograms that **drive people back to the full episode.** A 60-min episode
is the whole pizza; a clip is one irresistible slice. **55% of Americans listen to podcasts monthly** (record
high, Edison 2025); **85% watch social video sound-off** → **captions are non-optional.**

## The top-1% lever: select by retention, not vibes
Pull clips from **listener-retention spikes** — Spotify for Podcasters / Apple / **YouTube Studio** show where
people **replayed/rewatched** (a spike = a moment that landed). Map spikes → timestamps → clips. **AI virality
scores are a starting point, not the decider.** And don't re-listen: the **timestamped transcript is your
production map.**

## Two formats (+ a third)
- **Video-podcast clip** (if you recorded video) — **9:16 vertical**, auto-reframe to the **active speaker**,
  burned-in captions. 30–90s.
- **Audiogram** (audio-only) — **cover art/background + animated waveform** (bars/circles/lines/blobs) +
  **captions + title.** Makes audio visual + sound-off-accessible; beats a static image — but **real video
  clips outperform audiograms** (2025–26 practitioner consensus; attribute), so if video exists, clip it, and
  consider recording video going forward. An audio-only audiogram must be **caption-first**: the words carry
  it; the waveform is support, not the star.
- **Quote card** — a single strong line as a graphic (→ `design-and-templates`).

## The pipeline
```
record -> transcribe (Descript/Castmagic) -> timestamp standout moments (claims/data/reactions/stories; + retention spikes)
-> generate 5-8 clips/audiograms -> add hook + captions + branding -> strategic CTA -> publish across platforms on a cadence
```
OpusClip generates ~12–24 clips from a 60-min episode; you keep the best **5–8.** Processing ~10–20 min/hour.

## Tools (verify-quarterly)
- **Clip makers:** **Opus Clip** (repurposing engine; AI Virality Score; active-speaker reframe; free ~60–90

  **Podsqueeze**, Opus.pro audiogram tool.
- **Written repurposing:** **Castmagic** (audio → show notes/blog/captions/quotes). **Distribution:**
  Repurpose.io. **Design:** Canva (→ `design-and-templates`). **Video-clip caption burn-in:** →
  `captions-and-clipping` / `clipping.md`.

## Best practice
Captions on **every** clip; **hook in the first 3s**; **strategic CTA** (a *reason* to watch the full ep — a
teaser/cliffhanger — not "check out the full episode"); unique/relatable/personal-experience snippets get
shared most; consistent branding (logo/colors/templates); SEO keywords in description/tags. **Cadence
example:** episode Mon → clip Tue → blog Wed → quote graphic Thu → newsletter Fri → carousel next Mon (one
episode → a week+ of content, <2 hrs overhead).
