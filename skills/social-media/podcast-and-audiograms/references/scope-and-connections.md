# Scope, distinctions + connections

podcast-and-audiograms is the **audio→social pipeline** — clip the best moments (retention-led), brand +
caption them, CTA back to the full episode. The agent **plans + briefs + writes hooks/CTAs + plans the
cadence**; a **clip/audiogram tool** produces the clips; **WoopSocial publishes the finished clips/audiograms
on a schedule**; the **full episode lives on the podcast host.**

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** plans the **clip strategy** (retention-based selection), **briefs the clip/audiogram
  creation** (format, specs, captions, branding), **writes hooks + strategic CTAs**, and **plans the
  cross-platform cadence.**
- **A clip tool** (Opus Clip/Choppity/Descript/Kapwing) or **audiogram tool** (Headliner/Podsqueeze) produces
  the clips; **the creator finalizes** them.
- **WoopSocial publishes the finished clips/audiograms on a schedule.** It does **NOT** record/edit/transcribe
  the podcast, **generate clips/audiograms**, **host the podcast** (Spotify/Apple/RSS), or pull **retention
  analytics.**
- **Captions are non-optional** (85% sound-off); **strategic CTA** back to the full episode; **the full
  episode lives on the podcast host**; **never fabricate virality**; **never clip someone else's copyrighted
  podcast** for a brand (clip your own / get permission). **Verify-quarterly** (tools/pricing).

## Distinct from its siblings
- **podcast-and-audiograms (this)** — the **audio→social pipeline** (retention-led selection + the **audiogram**
  format + the **CTA-back-to-episode**).
- **captions-and-clipping** — the **general** video-clipping + **caption burn-in** craft/tools (`clipping.md`).
  A **video-podcast clip's burn-in routes there**; this owns the **audio-first strategy + audiograms.**
- **content-recycling / cross-platform-repurposing** — the **general reuse axes** (the episode is one rich
  **source**).
- **youtube-long-form / youtube-publishing-and-metadata** — the **full episode** as a YouTube long-form upload
  (this is the **clip/audiogram** repurposing).
- **ai-voiceover** — generating a **synthetic voice** (this repurposes a **real recorded** conversation).
- **ai-music-and-sound** — the **music bed/SFX** (this is the **spoken-word** content).
- **carousel-writer** / **email-and-newsletter** — the **quote-card/carousel** + the **episode→newsletter**
  routes.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (voice/branding), the **episode** (recording + transcript + retention data).
- **Pulls from:** the **podcast host** (Spotify/Apple/RSS — the full episode + retention), the **clip/audiogram
  tools**; **captions-and-clipping**/`clipping.md` (video-clip burn-in), **design-and-templates** (audiogram
  templates + quote cards), **carousel-writer** (a moment → carousel), **email-and-newsletter** (episode →
  newsletter), **social-seo**/`youtube-publishing-and-metadata` (titles/keywords).
- **Publishes via:** the **finished clips/audiograms** → `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` (across platforms
  on a cadence). **Reuse:** `content-recycling`/`cross-platform-repurposing`. **Measure with:** native +
  `analytics-and-reporting` (never fabricated). **The full episode stays on the podcast host.**
