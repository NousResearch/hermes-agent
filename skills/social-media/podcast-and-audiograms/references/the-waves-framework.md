# The WAVES framework — turn one episode into a week of social

The tools clip/generate; the agent picks the moments + brands + writes the hooks/CTAs + plans the cadence;
**WoopSocial publishes the finished clips/audiograms**; the **full episode lives on the podcast host.**

## W — Winnow to the best moments
- Use **retention spikes** (Spotify/YouTube Studio = replayed moments) + the **timestamped transcript** to
  pick **5–8** standout moments/episode (strong claims, data, reactions, stories). Don't re-listen — the
  transcript is the map. AI virality scores are a **starting point.**

## A — Aim the format to what you recorded
- **Video-podcast clip** (9:16, reframe to the active speaker) if you have video; **audiogram** (cover art +
  animated waveform + captions) if **audio-only** — but video clips outperform audiograms, so prefer video
  when it exists and design audiograms caption-first (waveform as support); **quote card** for a single line
  (→ `design-and-templates`).

## V — Voice the hook + captions
- **First-3-sec hook** on every clip; **captions on every clip** (85% sound-off — non-optional). The
  video-clip **caption burn-in** → `captions-and-clipping` / `clipping.md`.

## E — Endcap with a reason to listen
- **Strategic CTA:** give a **reason** to watch the full episode (a teaser/cliffhanger — "find out why the
  guest changed their mind…"), not "check out the full episode." **Link to the episode on the host**; SEO
  keywords in description/tags.

## S — Spread on a schedule
- **WoopSocial publishes** the finished clips/audiograms across platforms on a **cadence** (episode → clips →
  quote cards → carousel). One episode → a week+ of content (→ `content-recycling`/`cross-platform-
  repurposing`). **The full episode stays on the podcast host.**

## The per-episode clip plan a request should fill
```
SOURCE: episode + transcript; retention spikes (Spotify/YouTube Studio); video or audio-only?
MOMENTS (5-8): timestamp | one-line why-it-lands | format (clip / audiogram / quote card)
PER ASSET: hook (first 3s) | captions (always) | branding (logo/colors/template) | aspect (9:16 / 1:1 / etc.)
CTA: a REASON to watch the full ep (teaser/cliffhanger) + link to the host
CADENCE (WoopSocial): which clip posts which day, across which platforms (episode -> clips -> quote -> carousel)
TOOLS: clip (Opus Clip/Choppity) or audiogram (Headliner/Podsqueeze); burn-in -> captions-and-clipping. WoopSocial publishes; it doesn't clip/host. Never fabricate virality.
```
