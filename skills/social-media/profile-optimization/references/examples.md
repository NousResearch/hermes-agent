# Examples — optimized profiles

Worked profile specs showing the 3-second test, the searchable fields, and a goal-matched link —
with before/after contrast. Subject: a solo founder building a SaaS social scheduler. Voice is the
blunt indie-founder voice; your output uses the loaded `voice.md` and `social-strategy.md` (goal →
CTA).

---

## Before → after (the clarity fix)

**Before (vague, leaks):**
- Name field: "Sam"
- Bio: "founder ✨ | dreamer | building cool stuff | coffee addict ☕ | living the dream 🚀"
- Link: a Linktree with 14 links.

→ Fails the 3-second test: who is this for? what does it do? why follow? A visitor leaves.

**After (clear, converts):**
- Name field: **"Sam Lee | Social Media for Founders"** (searchable keyword, not just "Sam")
- Bio (≤150): **"I help solo founders grow on social without burning out. / Building [Product] —
  schedule everywhere from one place. / Free posting playbook ↓"**
- Link: **one** destination — the free playbook (the current goal), trackable.

→ Passes the test instantly; the right visitor self-identifies and follows; one clear next step.

## Instagram spec

- **Handle:** `@samlee` (consistent across platforms).
- **Name field:** "Sam Lee | Social for Founders".
- **Bio:** the "after" above.
- **Profile pic:** clear headshot (face converts for a personal brand).
- **Link:** free playbook (goal-matched); swap to the launch when campaigning.
- **Highlights:** Start here · Proof · [Product] · FAQ.
- **Pinned (grid):** intro Reel · best-performing teardown · the offer.

## LinkedIn spec

- **Headline:** "Helping solo founders grow on social without burning out · Building [Product] ·
  ex-[notable]" (keywords + clarity, not just "Founder").
- **About:** opens with the who/what/why (front-loaded), proof, CTA to the playbook.
- **Banner brief:** value prop ("Grow on social without burning out") + [Product] logo + a subtle
  CTA; legible text → hand to `tools/integrations/ideogram.md`.
- **Featured:** the playbook + a top post.

## TikTok spec (~80 chars)

- **Bio:** "Social media for founders. Building [Product]. Playbook ↓" (niche + CTA, tight).
- **Pinned:** 3 — an intro, a best performer, the offer.

---

## Contrast — what not to do

- A name field that's just "Sam"; a bio of emojis and vibes; a 15-link junk-drawer hub; a stock-photo
  banner; no pinned posts. Lots of visits, no follows — and the owner blames the algorithm.

---

## What the good specs share

- **Searchable fields used** (name field / headline carry a niche keyword).
- A **who/what/why bio** with **proof** and **one CTA**, on-voice, within the limit.
- A **goal-matched link** (one destination or a hub leading with the priority).
- **Pinned + highlights + banner** controlling the first impression.
- **Consistent** handle/pic across platforms; **re-fit** bio per platform.
- Advisory spec (ready to paste); no fabricated proof; no analytics claims.
