# Strategic Commenting

Commenting on **other people's** posts — the under-used growth play where a good comment borrows
someone else's (highly relevant) audience. ~41% of brands now leave proactive comments on others'
posts to boost visibility, and when a comment **sparks a reply, engagement jumps ~1.6×**. On LinkedIn
it's a *primary* growth channel; it works on IG/TikTok/X too.

> Re-verify specifics quarterly; the principle (value-adding comments borrow audiences) is durable.

## Why it grows you

- **Borrowed audience.** A genuinely valuable comment on a bigger account's post is seen by *their*
  audience — exactly the people you want — and drives curious profile visits back to you.
- **Comments get their own reach.** On several platforms comments now earn their own impressions, so a
  sharp comment is a tiny piece of content in its own right.
- **It compounds your authority.** Consistently smart comments in your lane mark you as a knowledgeable
  voice in the niche (reinforces the topic authority the growth skills rely on → `linkedin-growth`,
  `instagram-growth`, `tiktok-growth`).

## The value rubric (what to actually write)

A strategic comment must **add value** — it earns attention, it doesn't beg for it. Good comments:

- **Add an insight** the post didn't cover ("This matches what we saw — the nuance is X…").
- **Respectfully disagree and explain** ("I'd push back on point 2, because…") — the highest-engagement
  type, done in good faith.
- **Build on it** with a concrete example, a number, or a next step.
- **Ask a genuinely good question** that moves the discussion forward.
- Are **substantive** — aim for real sentences (15+ words), in your brand voice.

**Never:**
- "Great post! 🙌" / "So true!" / "Thanks for sharing" — worthless, and increasingly *blocked and
  reported* as spam.
- **Veiled self-promo** ("Love this — we actually built a tool for this, check my bio") — it reads as
  hijacking and burns goodwill.
- **AI-obvious / automated** comments — detected, ignored, and penalized (see the no-automation rule in
  `dms-hard-cases-and-safety.md`).

## Where to spend the time (the right rooms)

- **In your lane** — comment on topics you're credible on, so the people who discover you are the right
  ones.
- **On ICP + niche thought leaders** — the accounts whose audience *is* your audience. A viral comment
  in front of the wrong crowd builds nothing (see "the right people, not vanity" in
  `dms-hard-cases-and-safety.md`).
- **On posts with momentum, early** — commenting thoughtfully while a post is climbing gets your comment
  (and you) more eyes.

## Honest framing

This is **genuine participation done consistently**, not a hack. It works because the comments are
actually good and actually yours. The agent **drafts** strong, on-voice comments for the human to post
in real conversations — it does **not** spray automated comments across hundreds of accounts (that's
spam, and it backfires).
