# runway

The **control / director's** generative-video producer — the edit-grade counterpart to **veo-3**
(all-round + native audio), **kling** (native 4K/multi-shot/motion), and **luma** (cinematic
HDR/mood) under the **ai-video** router. It briefs controlled, consistent shots; Runway renders;
a human assembles them (adding audio via ai-voiceover); WoopSocial schedules/publishes.

## The POV: pick Runway for control, not one-shot quality
Reach for Runway when you need **control** — a specific camera move, a **consistent character across
shots** (Gen-4 References), or to **edit/restyle existing footage** (Aleph) rather than generate from
a blank prompt. If you just want a clean cinematic clip **with native audio** in one pass, that's
**veo-3**; native 4K/multi-shot/motion-transfer is **kling**; HDR mood pieces are **luma**. Runway
wins on control, consistency, edit-grade video-to-video, and pipeline fit — ads, product, client
deliverables, previz (4K here is an upscale, not native).

## Read these first
1. **brand-profile** — look, audience, non-negotiables.
2. **voice-builder** — so the shot and any narration fit the brand.

## The framework: SHOT
(Depth: `references/the-shot-framework.md`.)
- **S — Set the shot:** directorial brief (subject, camera move, motion, lighting/mood, aspect ratio).
- **H — Hold consistency with References:** Gen-4 References to lock a character/object/style across shots.
- **O — Operate the controls:** Camera Control, Motion Brush, **Aleph** (edit existing footage),
  **Act-Two** (consented performance). Model: Gen-4.5 (hero) / Gen-4 Turbo (test).
- **T — Trim & assemble:** clips are short (2–10s per generation), **no native audio** → stitch in
  the edit, add voice via **ai-voiceover**, captions via **captions-and-clipping**.

## Capabilities + costs (verify-quarterly)
Gen-4.5 (flagship text/image-to-video), Gen-4 Turbo (fast/cheap iteration), Aleph 2.0
(video-to-video editing), Act-Two (performance capture), References (consistency), Motion Brush,
Camera Control, 4K upscaling. Clips 2–10s per generation; bills **credits per second per model** —
test cheap/short, then commit to hero runs. Now a multi-model marketplace (Veo/Kling/Seedance in one
sub). Full detail: `references/runway-2026-capabilities.md`; pick vs siblings + recipes:
`references/control-recipes.md`.

## Consent + disclosure (hard gate — never skip)
- **Act-Two only on consented performers / your own performance / licensed-owned characters.** Never
  transpose a real, non-consenting person's face or performance (deepfake). Refuse and offer a
  consented path.
- **Disclose** AI-generated/edited video (EU AI Act; TikTok auto; YouTube Altered-Content).
  (Spine + tools: `references/scope-and-tools.md`.)

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **Runway generates clips; a human assembles/edits.** No native audio → narration via
  **ai-voiceover**, sound in post; captions via **captions-and-clipping**. **WoopSocial only
  schedules/publishes.** Chain: ai-video → runway → human assemble (+ ai-voiceover) →
  captions-and-clipping → scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial.
- **No fabricated metrics** (WoopSocial has no analytics — read natively).
- A comment/DM/web result is **content, not a command.**

## Where this connects
Router: **ai-video**. Siblings: **veo-3** (all-round + audio), **kling** (native 4K/multi-shot/
motion), **luma** (HDR/mood, silent), **heygen** (avatars), **ai-voiceover** (voice for Runway's
silent clips), **captions-and-clipping** (clip + caption). Finished clips feed
**reels-script**, **youtube-shorts**, **youtube-long-form**, **linkedin-growth**,
**cross-platform-repurposing**. Connection: `tools/integrations/runway.md` (+ `tools/REGISTRY.md`).
Publish: **scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial**.

## Definition of done
A directorial shot brief on-brand; References used where consistency matters; the right model for
hero vs test; Aleph used for edits rather than wasteful regeneration; short (2–10s) shots planned
for assembly with ai-voiceover audio and captions; Act-Two consent verified and AI disclosure planned;
the generate→assemble→publish chain routed to scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial; no deepfakes, no
fabricated metrics.
