# Scope, distinctions + connections

seasonal-and-moment-marketing maps the **few moments that fit your brand**, gates them for **fit +
authenticity**, **tiers** them by weight, and **plans them far in advance** — the planned counterpart to
`trend-jacking`. The agent **plans**; big commercial moments → `campaign-and-launch-planning`; **WoopSocial
schedules the planned content ahead.**

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** builds the brand's **moment calendar**, runs the **fit + authenticity gate**, **tiers** by
  commercial weight + sensitivity, **plans the angle + lead-time**, and **feeds the `content-calendar`.**
- **Routing:** big commercial moments (BFCM/Christmas) = a **campaign** → `campaign-and-launch-planning` (+
  paid); the reactive ~50% → `trend-jacking`; the cultural-moment **craft** → `meme-and-culture`; the
  **visual** → `image-prompt`/`design-and-templates`.
- **WoopSocial** **schedules the planned seasonal content in advance** (where advance planning shines). It does
  **NOT** pick which moments fit, **judge authenticity**, build the calendar, or **run paid amplification**
  (BFCM paid is native/ads).
- **The few that fit > blanket-posting.** **Never** participate in identity/cause moments (Pride/MLK/Juneteenth/
  Earth Day) **without genuine year-round standing** (performative/rainbow-washing backlash); **non-promotional
  respect** for solemn/cultural days; **plan ahead** (8–12 wk major / 2–4 wk minor); **measure vs the moment's
  goal**; **never fabricate** a tie-in or a metric. **Verify-quarterly** (confirm exact 2026 dates/market).

## Distinct from its siblings
- **seasonal-and-moment-marketing (this)** — the **PLANNED, predictable, advance-planned** moment map.
- **trend-jacking** — the **REACTIVE** counterpart (real-time, unpredictable; the **~50% open** calendar).
  **They're two halves of the same calendar.**
- **content-calendar** — the **operational scheduling system** (slots/cadence/the grid); this **feeds** it the
  moments.
- **campaign-and-launch-planning** — the **multi-post launch arc**; the **big commercial moments route there.**
- **batch-content-plan** — the **batch production** system (this feeds it the moments).
- **meme-and-culture** — the **cultural-moment participation craft** + brand-safety judgment (how to execute a
  moment post); this decides **which** moments.
- **social-strategy** / **content-pillars** — the **overall plan**; this is the **time-anchored** layer.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (voice/identity/standing), `audience-research` (what the audience cares
  about + when).
- **Feeds:** `content-calendar` (the moments → the grid), `batch-content-plan` (batch the assets).
- **Routes to:** `campaign-and-launch-planning` (big commercial moments + paid), `trend-jacking` (the reactive
  half), `meme-and-culture` (cultural-moment craft), `image-prompt`/`design-and-templates` (the visual),
  `email-and-newsletter` (the moment across owned channels).
- **Publishes via:** the **planned content** → `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` (scheduled **ahead**).
  **Measure with:** native + `analytics-and-reporting` against the moment's goal (never fabricated). **Moment
  selection, authenticity judgment + paid stay with the human/native.**
