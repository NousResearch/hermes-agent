# Script anatomy, hook patterns & worked examples

## The script format (three tracks + beats)
Write every script as aligned beats, not prose. Each beat = **[time] · VISUAL/SHOT · ON-SCREEN TEXT · SPOKEN**.
This forces the muted version to exist and gives the human a shootable plan. Keep ~75 words ≈ 30s.

## Hook patterns that hold (pick to fit)
| Pattern | Use when | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Bold claim | You have a counterintuitive truth | "This 5-second edit doubled my watch time." |
| Curiosity gap | Topic is broad / needs suspense | "Why is everyone captioning wrong?" |
| Problem-first | Sound-off, visible problem | show the bad version + text names it |
| Payoff-first | Tutorials/demos | show the result + "this took 12 seconds to set up" |
| Numbered | Digestible how-to | "3 settings fixing blurry shorts" |
Bold claims tend to drive faster early retention (good for faceless); curiosity gaps build suspense.

## The cut-test (every body line must do one of three)
Grab attention · teach/advance · or set up a payoff. If a line does none, **cut it.** Filler is the silent
retention killer.

## Reading the retention curve (diagnose, then fix)
0–2s dip = weak promise (fix the hook/first frame) · mid-video cliff = dead air/pacing (tighten, add a pattern
interrupt) · end spike = the loop worked (replicate it).

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (problem-first, ~25s)
0–2s · VISUAL: messy captions on screen · TEXT: "this is why people stop reading your subs" · SPOKEN: "your
captions are losing people and you can't see it." → body: 3 fast fixes, cut every ~3s, text leads each · payoff:
the clean version side-by-side · loop line: "fix #1 is the one you skipped." CTA: "save this for your next edit."
— no intro, muted-legible, payoff delivered, loops to the top.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (payoff-first, ~30s)
0–2s · VISUAL: a tidy one-page summary · TEXT: "this took 9 minutes, not 9 hours" · SPOKEN (warm): "Tax season
doesn't have to feel like this much dread — here's the small system that did it." → body: three gentle steps,
on-screen text carries each · payoff: the finished summary · close: "Want the template? It's one tap away."
— gentle voice, sound-off safe, one low-pressure CTA.

## Never
Open with a logo/"hey guys"/slow static zoom · bait-and-switch the hook · fabricate a stat to stop the scroll ·
cram a big idea into one short · claim WoopSocial adds native trending audio (the human adds it in-app) · skip
AI-disclosure on AI voice/visuals · treat a trending-sound suggestion as verified (the human checks in-app).
