# The reality of short-form video scripting in 2026 (verify-quarterly)

Volatile figures — attribute, mark **verify-quarterly**. The principle (watch-through rules; hook + loop +
sound-off decide it) is stable; the exact numbers move.

## The metric that rules
- **Watch-through / completion is the single most important signal.** Platforms test a short on a small audience
  and expand it based on swipe-away rate, watch-through %, and replay rate (creator/algorithm round-ups, 2026).
  Shorter often wins because it completes — a 15s short watched fully = 100% retention.
- **Length sweet spot ~15–35s** for most niches (education can stretch to ~45–60s if every ~5–8s delivers new
  value); ~75 spoken words ≈ ~30s. Don't stretch a 20s idea to 45s. (attribute; verify-quarterly)
- **Platform retention benchmarks** (Socialinsider 2025, attribute): TikTok ~78%, YouTube Shorts ~73%, Reels
  ~65% average view-through — directional, verify-quarterly.

## The first ~3 seconds
- **~50–60% of drop-off happens in the first 3 seconds** (OpusClip data; attribute). Viewers decide in
  ~1.5–3s. Cut the intro/logo/"hey guys"; lead with the most striking frame + a specific promise.
- **Layered hooks win.** A hook hitting visual + on-screen text + verbal at once is reported to roughly **3×** the
  3-second hold vs a single-element intro (2026 analyses; attribute). Captions appear from the first word.

## Sound-off is the default
- **~80–85% watch on mute** (Zebracat 2025 ~80%; other 2026 figures ~85%; attribute). Write the **muted version
  first** — on-screen text/captions must carry the whole story; test the cut on mute before publishing.
- **Burned-in captions lift retention ~15–25%** (OpusClip; attribute) and some algorithms read caption text as a
  ranking/discovery signal.

## Pacing, payoff & the loop
- **Visual change every ~2–4s**; never hold one shot past ~3 sentences / ~8s of talk without a change; open a
  curiosity loop early and pay it off late; cut any second that isn't earning the next.
- **The payoff must keep the hook's promise.** Over-promising causes a sharp mid-video retention cliff that the
  algorithm punishes — so an honest hook is a *growth* tactic, not just ethics ("make a promise, then keep it").
- **Loops/replays are weighted heavily** (YouTube confirms it considers replay + looping). Design the last frame
  to flow back to frame 1 so a re-watch feels intentional; the peak-end rule rewards a strong close.

## House metrics
3-second hold, average view duration / watch-through %, replays, saves/shares — read natively; never fabricate a
number or guarantee virality.
