# The WATCH framework — scripts engineered for watch-through

Short-form lives or dies on **watch-through**, so the script's only job is to be un-skippable. Each letter is a
gate; most scripts fail at W (slow intro) or C (the payoff doesn't keep the promise).

## W — Win the first 3 seconds
~50–60% of drop-off is here, so this is the whole ballgame.
- **Lead with the most striking frame** (text overlay / dramatic visual / mid-action) — something interesting is
  on screen *before* the first word. No logo, no "hey guys," no slow zoom on a static image.
- **Layer the hook:** visual + on-screen text + verbal at once (~3× the hold of a single-element hook). One short,
  specific, bold/curious sentence — outcomes/numbers/contrasts, not a vague topic.
- **Shot-list 5 hook variants** over the same body; the hook is a variable to *test*, not a one-time decision.

## A — Arc with open loops
Structure = **Hook → Body/escalation → Payoff → CTA/loop.** Keep them watching:
- A bridge sentence carries curiosity from hook into body; open a loop early ("I'll come back to why this
  matters"); number the points so progress is visible ("step 2 of 3").
- **Visual change every ~2–4s**; never hold a shot past ~3 sentences / ~8s without a change (pattern interrupts:
  cut, zoom, B-roll, SFX).
- **Cut anything that isn't earning the next second** — if a line doesn't grab, teach, or advance, delete it.

## T — Two tracks (sound-off + sound-on)
~80% watch on mute, so **write the muted version first.**
- On-screen text/captions carry the story alone; VO/audio *adds* when sound is on. The three tracks (visual,
  spoken, text) reinforce, never compete.
- Captions from word one, legible (bold, high-contrast, 4–7-word chunks, safe zones). **Test the cut on mute** —
  if the hook doesn't land silently, rewrite it.

## C — Cash the promise
The payoff is *why they stayed* — the answer/reveal/punchline/transformation that **delivers what the hook sold.**
If the hook said "3 changes that fixed my reach," the third (best) change must land. Over-promising tanks AVD and
gets suppressed; a true promise kept is the cleanest path to reach.

## H — Hold the loop, land the CTA
- **Engineer the loop:** make the final frame/line flow back to frame 1 (a circular last subtitle: "…and that's
  why the first line mattered") so replays feel intentional — replays compound distribution.
- **One fitting CTA**, ≈2s, tied to value: save ("you'll need this for step X"), share, or follow for the next
  part — never a rote "like & subscribe" that drags completion.
- Then route platform specifics to `reels-script` / `tiktok-script` / `youtube-shorts`; **WoopSocial publishes**
  the finished file.
