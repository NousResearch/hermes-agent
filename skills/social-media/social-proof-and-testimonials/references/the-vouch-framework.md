# The VOUCH framework — real proof that actually converts

A testimonial is someone vouching for you. The job is to make that vouch **real, specific, objection-matched,
and credible** — never to manufacture it. Each letter is a gate; most weak proof content fails at U (no
specifics) or skips V (consent/authenticity).

## V — Vet real proof
Source genuine proof — reviews, testimonials, DMs, UGC, case studies, results — and verify it's real before it
becomes content:
- **Consent first.** Get explicit permission to feature the person + their words/face; a public tag is *not*
  blanket permission, and **paid/ads use needs separate, broader rights.**
- **Never fabricate.** No invented testimonials, no AI-generated "customers", no putting words in a real
  customer's mouth. (FTC rule + the 2026 likeness crisis: no real-or-AI lookalike without permission.)
- **Disclose** material connections (employee/paid/gifted); never condition an incentive on positive sentiment.
- **Preserve meaning** when you trim a quote.

## O — Overcome the right objection
Proof only works when it answers the **specific doubt** in the buyer's head. Map proof to objection:
price → ROI/value proof; risk → guarantee + "people like me" proof; "will it work for *me*?" → a near-identical
customer's result; trust → volume + recognizable names. Right proof, right funnel stage — objection-dissolving
proof sits near the decision point.

## U — Unearth the specifics
Specificity is believability. Pull the concrete: a real **number** (time saved, revenue, % change), a
**before/after**, a sensory detail, a real **face + first name + role** (with consent). Cut vague raves. And keep
the occasional **mixed/negative** note where honest — it raises credibility (a flawless wall reads fake).

## C — Cast the customer as the hero
Frame the proof as **their** story: where they were stuck → the turning point → the result they got. The customer
is the hero; you're the guide (this is where it hands to `storytelling-and-narrative`). It should read as the
customer winning, not the brand bragging.

## H — Hand it across formats + placements
One proof asset → many cuts, routed to the writers and placed where doubt is highest:
- **Quote/stat card** → caption-writer + design-and-templates. **Results carousel / case study** → carousel-writer.
- **Video testimonial** → reels-script. **UGC reshare** (with rights + credit) → ugc-and-influencer pipeline.
- **Placement:** decision points — product/landing context, launch posts, DMs (`social-selling-and-dm`), email
  (`email-and-newsletter`), funnels (`lead-magnets-and-funnels`).
- **WoopSocial publishes**; measure saves/shares + clicks; never fabricate a metric or a review.
