# Social selling & DMs 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (Meta DM rules + limits change often).*

## The shift: feed → inbox (but only on permission)
Feed engagement fell from ~2.94% (2024) to **<1% (2026)** while **DM open rates sit at 88–90%** — "the
opportunity moved from public feeds to private conversations." But it works **one way only**: as a
**response to intent** (someone commented, replied to a Story, followed, or sent a keyword), **never as
cold outreach.** The test: *did the user ask for this through an explicit action?* If yes → fine. If no →
spam. **Permission is the whole game.**

## Warm vs cold (the risk axis)
**Warm** (commented / followed / replied / previously messaged) = low risk; the platform reads it as
continuing a conversation they started. **Cold** (no relationship) = highest spam risk and **where most
bans originate.** Above ~50 DMs/day, cold outreach is simply the wrong approach.

## Why it converts
Speed compounds — classic lead-response research puts a **~1-minute reply at ~391% better conversion**
than a 30-minute wait, and the same decay applies in the inbox. **Micro-accounts (1K–50K) convert
15–20%** DM-to-sale (vs 7–10% larger) — smaller, more engaged audiences respond more. Personalized
outreach ~32% response vs ~18% cold email.

## The comment → DM → email funnel (the canonical 2026 play)
Caption a Reel/carousel "**Comment KEYWORD and I'll DM you the free [magnet]**" → follower comments →
a **warm DM delivers the value** → **capture the email natively in the chat** → soft next step while
warm. Public comments = social proof + a strong engagement signal + a natural bridge public→private. Five
flows: comment-to-DM, story-reply, keyword-triggered, welcome DM, re-engagement.

## Meta's rules (May 2026) — official API only
**Allowed:** automated replies to comments/story-replies/keyword DMs **via the official Meta API**; links/
media in DMs; collecting email in DM; follow-up **within the 24-hour window**; tools on the official API
(ManyChat, LinkDM, CreatorFlow, PilotDM…). **Not allowed:** DMs to people who haven't interacted (cold
via API); exceeding pacing caps (~200/hr tool-side); messaging **outside the 24h window** without a new
interaction; **unofficial/scraping/password tools**; misleading/spam content.

## The 24-hour window
The API lets you reply for **24h after the user's last interaction**; after that, **no follow-up unless
they re-engage.** Best practice: **compress promo into the first 24h, then move the relationship to
email** (→ `email-and-newsletter`).

## The ~12 behaviors that cause ~94% of bans
Top triggers: (1) **same exact text to >25 recipients/hour** (fingerprint detection — incl. emoji/link
slugs); (2) **cold/non-opted-in** sends; (3) **>1 external link** per message; (4) **URL shorteners**
(bit.ly etc. — deboosted/flagged); (5) **unofficial scraping tools**. Also: **report rate >0.6%** →
auto-review; **opt-out >4%** is a top trigger. Official-API tools = **~27× lower ban risk.** Limits
(verify-quarterly): 20–50/day new, 50–100 established, 5–15/hr, 1,000 chars; new accounts warm up over
~30 days.

## Manual warm / partnership outreach
For genuine 1:1 outreach, send **manually** in time blocks (each message approved) — "fully automated
cold DMs trigger immediate bans." **Personalize** (reference something specific → +40% response); no
generic "Hey!"; **no link in the first message**; avoid spam words ("free money", "click here", "limited
time"); **respect bios** ("no DMs / email only"); **partnership disclosures must be public** (comments/
captions), not just DM. Route high-intent to a **human**. Tools (advisory): ManyChat/LinkDM/CreatorFlow/
PilotDM — **always official Meta API**, never scrapers.
