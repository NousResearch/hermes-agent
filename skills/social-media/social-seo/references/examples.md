# Examples — social SEO in practice

Worked optimizations showing keyword research → placement across the surface → cluster thinking, plus
honest scope. Subject: a solo founder building a SaaS social scheduler. Voice context is the blunt
indie-founder voice; your output uses the loaded `voice.md`, `audience.md`.

---

## Example 1 — optimizing one TikTok for search

**Target query (from autocomplete):** "how to schedule social media posts for free" (typed the seed,
TikTok suggested it — real demand, no invented volume).
**Optimization (triple mention):**
- **Spoken (first 3s):** "Here's how to schedule social media posts for free…"
- **On-screen text:** `Schedule posts for free` (keyword headline, not decoration).
- **Caption:** "How to schedule your social media posts for free — the exact setup I use. [natural
  context]" + 3 niche hashtags.
- **Cluster note:** this is one of a set — pair with "schedule Instagram posts," "best free scheduler,"
  "schedule a week of content in 20 min" → becomes the answer for *scheduling* on the FYP and in search.

## Example 2 — Instagram (name field + caption + alt)

**Move people miss:** the **name field**. Change "Maya R." → **"Maya · Social Scheduling Tips"** so the
account surfaces when people search the category, not just the handle.
**Post:** caption leads with the natural keyword ("how to batch a week of content…"), Reel is captioned,
alt text describes the carousel in searchable terms. 5 niche hashtags max.

## Example 3 — YouTube Short

- **Title:** "How to Schedule a Week of Social Posts in 20 Minutes (Free)" — keyword-led, descriptive.
- **Description:** ~200 words of natural context + the steps + a link; not one line.
- **Captions** uploaded; **chapters** if long-form ("00:20 The free tool," "01:10 Batch the captions").
- Same phrase in the spoken intro. Works for the Short *and* Google's video panel.

## Example 4 — Pinterest pin

Keywords are everything (no hashtags). **Pin title:** "Free Social Media Scheduling Setup."
**Description:** keyword-rich, intent-matching ("plan and auto-publish a week of posts for free…").
**Board:** "Social Media Scheduling" (a topic cluster). Alt + on-image text describe it. Evergreen —
keeps surfacing for planners for months.

## Example 5 — a keyword cluster (the real strategy)

Pillar: **"saving time on social."** Cluster of evergreen posts, each on one long-tail query:
batch-scheduling, free tools, repurposing one idea across platforms, a realistic weekly cadence,
captions fast. Across TikTok/IG/YouTube/Pinterest. Result: the account becomes *the* searchable answer
for time-strapped founders — and the recommendation algorithm categorizes it cleanly too.

## Honest scope (say this)

- **Searchability, not a #1 guarantee** — you optimize the signals; the algorithm + competition decide
  the rank.
- **No invented volumes** — research is autocomplete / related / native analytics / tools, not numbers
  the agent made up.
- **No keyword stuffing** — natural language, primary keyword once or twice; spam dilutes your signal.
- **Evergreen compounds** — the payoff builds over months; it's not a one-post trick.
- **GEO is separate** — getting cited by ChatGPT/Google AI is `ai-search-optimization`'s job.
- **No WoopSocial analytics** — WoopSocial publishes the optimized content; the search read-out comes
  from native platform insights (+ Search Console/UTMs).

---

## What the examples share

- They start from a **real query** (autocomplete), not a guess.
- They place the keyword across the platform's **specific indexed surfaces** (triple mention where it
  applies).
- They think in **clusters / evergreen**, not one-off posts.
- They lead with **keywords, support with hashtags**, in **natural language**.
