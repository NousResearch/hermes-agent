# Keyword Research & Technique

How to find the terms people actually search, how to place them, and how to build evergreen topical
authority — honestly (no fabricated volumes).

> Re-verify tool/feature specifics quarterly.

## Finding social keywords (use real demand, never invent it)

Social keyword research is different from Google SEO research — in-app engines rank short, visual
content by **engagement, freshness, and the language inside the post**, and people search in
**natural, spoken language.** Sources, best first:

- **Platform autocomplete** — start typing a seed ("vegan meal prep for…") and the suggestions ARE
  real queries. The single most reliable, free source.
- **"Others searched for" / related searches** — the carousel on results pages (esp. TikTok) and
  related-search chips reveal adjacent intent.
- **The search-results page itself** — what already ranks shows the intent and the bar to beat.
- **Comments & DMs** — questions people ask = queries to answer.
- **Audience language** — from `audience-research`: the exact words your audience uses (not industry
  jargon).
- **Native search analytics** — YouTube ("traffic source: search" + the search terms), TikTok/IG
  search insights show what found you. The user pulls these.
- **Tools (optional)** — TikTok Creative Center, TubeBuddy/VidIQ (YouTube), Google Trends/Keyword
  Planner for cross-platform demand.

> **Honesty rule.** The agent **cannot pull live in-app search volumes.** Never state precise volume
> numbers as fact. Give the user the *method* (autocomplete, related, native analytics, tools) and
> work from the qualitative signal — relative interest, intent, specificity — not invented figures.

**Prioritize** long-tail, intent-rich, *specific* queries (lower competition, higher intent) over
broad head terms — and queries that match what you can genuinely answer.

## The on-content technique — lead with keywords, support with hashtags

The core move: **write for the search bar first, the hashtag second.** "Hashtags categorize; keywords
get you found."

- **State the primary keyword in plain language, early and once or twice** — in the caption/title, not
  buried. One clear search intent per post.
- **The triple mention (video):** the same primary phrase in **(1) spoken audio** (say it in the first
  few seconds — it gets transcribed), **(2) on-screen text** (a keyword headline, not decoration), and
  **(3) the caption/description.** Align every signal to one intent so the algorithm is certain what
  you're about.
- **Then add 3–5 niche hashtags** (per platform caps) as category signals → `hashtag-strategy`.
- **Descriptive over clever in search contexts** — but keep the hook for the feed (see the tension
  below).

## Don't keyword-stuff

In-app engines **reward natural language and penalize spam.** Cramming 20–30 keywords/hashtags signals
spam, **dilutes your topical signal** (the algorithm can't tell what you're about), and tanks
readability. Write something a human wants to read; make sure the primary keyword appears **clearly,
once or twice.** Decorative on-screen text that hurts readability also backfires.

## The search-vs-feed tension (resolve it, don't ignore it)

Search rewards **clarity** ("Best Budget Mic for Podcasting 2026"); the feed rewards an **intriguing
hook**. They can conflict. Resolve by: leading the **title/first line clear and keyword-true**, then
adding intrigue *after* the keyword; or letting the **on-screen hook** carry curiosity while the
**caption/title** carries the keyword. Don't sacrifice the searchable phrase for a clever-but-vague
line — or you win the scroll and lose the search.

## Build topical clusters (evergreen authority)

One post rarely owns a query. **Cluster** content around a pillar topic — many posts answering the
long-tail questions around it — so the platform (and Google) see you as **the answer/authority** for
that topic. This:

- mirrors the **niche topic authority** the growth skills rely on (search + recommendation overlap),
- compounds: each evergreen post keeps surfacing and reinforces the others,
- maps cleanly onto `content-pillars` → a pillar becomes a keyword cluster → many evergreen posts.

Favor **evergreen, intent-answering** formats (how-tos, "best X for Y", explainers, comparisons) that
stay searched — alongside, not instead of, timely feed content.

## Measure (honestly)

Track **discoverability** signals, not vanity: impressions **from search**, search terms that found
you, profile visits from non-followers, saves, and follower growth from **evergreen** posts — via
native platform search analytics (+ Google Search Console / UTMs for off-platform). **WoopSocial has no
SEO analytics** — it publishes the optimized content (caption, alt text where supported); the search
read-out comes from the platforms.
