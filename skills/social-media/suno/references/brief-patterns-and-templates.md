# Brief patterns, decision tables & worked examples

## The brief pattern (specificity beats vibes)
"[Genre 1-2]. Mood: [one anchor — e.g. 'quietly confident']. Instruments: [2-4 named — 'warm upright piano,
brushed drums, soft upright bass']. Vocals: [spec — 'female alto, breathy, conversational' | 'instrumental'].
Tempo: [~BPM or feel]." Then structure via meta-tags in the lyrics field. Exclude-styles for the leaks.

## The use-case router
| Job | Move | Rights note |
|---|---|---|
| Video background / series theme | Instrumental brief + Extend to length | paid tier; replaces CapCut library-music trap |
| Podcast intro/outro | 15-30s structured sting ([Intro] heavy) | paid tier; archive the WAV + stems |
| Ad / client work | Human-in-the-loop version (lyrics/re-recorded part) | strengthens claim; no indemnification — generic styles |
| Brand sonic identity | Persona → own Voice → Custom Model (owned tracks) | test post-update consistency first |
| Streaming release | Curate + humanize + DDEX AI flag; no PRO registration for pure-AI | honest performance expectations |
| Trend-riding TikTok/Reel | NOT Suno — native trending audio in-app | trend reach is native-only |

## The archive checklist (do this now — model deprecation is scheduled)
WAV downloads of keepers ✓ · stems exported (Auto Split) ✓ · MIDI exported for anything you may re-record ✓ ·
Custom Model source tracks backed up ✓ · winning style prompts + personas documented ✓.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (the rights catch)
"made 30 tracks on the free tier before reading the terms. all 30 are non-commercial forever — upgrading
doesn't fix them. regenerated the 4 keepers on pro (took an hour), archived wavs + stems same day because the
old models are getting sunset. also stopped prompting 'like [artist]' — there's an active lawsuit about exactly
that, and my ad budget doesn't include a legal defense fund. generic mood words work fine." — no-retroactive
trap, archive discipline, litigation-aware prompting.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (the series theme)
"We wanted our videos to *sound* like us — calm, a little warm, never salesy. One brief did it: 'soft jazz,
quietly reassuring, warm upright piano and brushed drums, instrumental, ~85 BPM.' Generated six, kept one,
extended it into 15s, 30s and 60s cuts, and now every video opens the same gentle way. One small step: write
one sentence describing how your brand should sound — then make the machine match it, not the other way
around." — brief-first, curation, one theme reused.

## Never
Generate anything commercial on the free tier · assume upgrading fixes old tracks · treat commercial rights as
copyright ownership · expect indemnification · clone a real artist's voice or fake a collab · prompt artist
names for commercial work while litigation is live · skip the DDEX AI flag at distribution · register pure-AI
works with PROs · leave keepers unarchived with deprecation scheduled · claim WoopSocial generates or attaches
audio.
