# The reality of Suno in 2026 (verify-quarterly)

## What it is (and the moment it's in)
Suno (v5.5, shipped 26 Mar 2026) generates full songs — vocals, lyrics, instrumentation — from text, with the
most natural-sounding vocals in the category (Udio wins on instrumental mix polish but became a no-download
"walled garden" after its UMG settlement). The product now includes **Suno Studio** (Premier browser DAW —
multitrack, up to 12 stems rebuilt from the latent model with zero bleed, MIDI export, Warp Markers),
**Personas** (reusable voice/style), **Voices** (clone your **own verified** singing voice), **Custom Models**
(fine-tune v5.5 on 6+ tracks you verifiably own; up to 3, private), **My Taste** (preference learning), stems
(Auto Split / Split from Mix), Extend, hum-to-song input, and an API (Python/Node; Zapier/Make) — API tier
gating conflicts across sources; verify. (attribute: eesel, jamesm.blog, blakecrosley, margabagus)

## The legal spine (the reason this skill exists — attribute; verify-quarterly)
- **June 2024:** RIAA sued Suno + Udio for UMG/Sony/WMG. **Nov 2025: Warner settled + partnered** (first
  major-label AI-music licensing deal; Suno acquired Songkick). **UMG and Sony are still suing Suno** — a
  pivotal fair-use summary-judgment hearing is set for **July 2026** (D. Mass.); after fingerprinting evidence,
  UMG/Sony moved (May 2026) to **add 61k+ recordings** to the case — Suno is opposing (attribute: MBW);
  Germany's GEMA case verdict was scheduled June 2026.
- **The Warner deal rewrote user rights:** ToS softened from "you own it" to users being **granted commercial
  rights** (Suno's wording: users are "generally not considered the owner"); free-tier commercial downloads
  removed (play/share); paid download caps described as forthcoming; **pre-deal models scheduled for
  deprecation** when licensed models ship later in 2026 — **archive WAVs/stems/MIDI now**; artist **opt-in**
  (voice/likeness, compensated, artist-controlled) announced.
- **No retroactive rights:** free-tier songs stay non-commercial **forever** — upgrading later does not unlock
  them. Generate on a paid plan from the start.
- **No indemnification:** commercial rights ≠ legal defense — if a track draws an infringement claim, the risk
  sits with the user (indemnification runs user → Suno).
- **The protectability trap:** Suno's own docs state 100%-AI music **doesn't qualify for copyright** (prompting
  isn't authorship; the Thaler v. Perlmutter human-authorship line). You can monetize, but stopping someone
  re-using your raw track is legally weak — **human elements** (original lyrics, re-recorded parts over stems,
  real arrangement in a DAW) strengthen the claim and reclassify the work as **AI-assisted** for distributors.
- **DDEX AI-disclosure:** since late 2025, Spotify/Apple enforce AI flagging at distribution; don't register
  purely-AI works with PROs (ASCAP/BMI).

## Tiers + credits (≈; verify in-app)
**Free:** 50 credits/day (~10 songs) — strictly non-commercial, forever. **Pro ≈ $8/mo annual (~$10 monthly):**
2,500 credits (~500 songs), commercial rights, Personas, Voices, stems, uploads. **Premier ≈ $24/mo annual
($30):** 10,000 credits + Studio — same legal rights as Pro; the extra buys volume + tooling. Subscription
credits don't roll over; top-ups persist but need an active sub. Known frictions (community-reported):
Persona/Voice regressions after the May 2026 update; Studio's failed stem regenerations still deducting
credits; ISRC self-upload blocking on users' own distributed tracks.

## Performance honesty
One analytics firm's campaign data (Chartlex, 2,400+ campaigns) reports fully-AI tracks underperform human
releases on Spotify save rate ~25–40% and completion ~15–25% — AI music is leverage for content and brand
sound, not a shortcut to a streaming career. House metrics: human-ears fitness for the brief + the performance
of the content it scores (native) — never fabricated.
