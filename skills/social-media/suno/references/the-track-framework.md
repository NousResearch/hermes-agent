# The TRACK framework — brand music that's on-brief and on-rights

Suno's leverage is a full song from a sentence; the expert separators are generating on the right tier BEFORE
creating anything, and knowing that commercial rights ≠ copyright. Each letter is a gate; most failures happen
at R (rights assumed) or by shipping the first shapeless blob.

## T — Target the brief
Music does a job — score a video, open a podcast, carry an ad, become the brand's sonic signature. Brief it
like a director: **genre (1–2) + one mood anchor + 2–4 named instruments + vocal spec** (gender, register,
delivery — or instrumental). Specificity collapses wasted generations; "something upbeat" pays the variance tax
all day. Pull the brand's feel from `brand-profile`; route the model-agnostic choice through
`ai-music-and-sound` first.

## R — Rights first (before generating anything)
- **Paid plan from the first note** — free-tier songs are non-commercial **forever**; upgrading never
  retroactively unlocks them.
- Know what you hold: **commercial rights, not ownership** — purely-AI output isn't copyrightable (prompting
  isn't authorship), so your ability to stop re-use of a raw track is weak; and there's **no indemnification**
  if a track draws a claim (UMG/Sony litigation is live — favor generic style language over artist references).
- Never clone a real artist's voice or imply their involvement; the Warner **opt-in** program is the legitimate
  path when it ships, not a prompting trick today.

## A — Arrange with structure
Custom mode + **meta-tags in the lyrics field** give you the architecture: [Intro], [Verse 1], [Chorus],
[Bridge], [Outro] (+ energy/delivery notes where supported). Style prompt = the brief from T; **exclude-styles**
for what keeps leaking in; verses in short blocks (~4 lines). **Extend from a timestamp** to grow a good take
instead of re-rolling; hum/upload input seeds a melody. Consistency stack for a sonic identity: **Personas** →
your own verified **Voice** → a **Custom Model** trained on tracks you own (test first — post-May-2026 voice
regressions are community-reported).

## C — Curate + humanize
Generate variations, then **curate** — the ears are the instrument: pick the best seed, extend it, and cut the
rest. For anything that matters, **add human elements**: original lyrics, a re-recorded part over the stems, real
arrangement decisions in a DAW (Studio → Ableton/Logic) — this both **strengthens the copyright claim** and
**reclassifies the work as AI-assisted** for distributors. **Archive early**: WAVs + stems + MIDI out, before
pre-deal models are deprecated. Batch stem jobs deliberately (failed regenerations reportedly still cost
credits).

## K — Keep disclosure clean
At distribution: **flag the AI origin** (DDEX-standard disclosure via the distributor — hiding it risks the
account); **don't register purely-AI works with PROs**; keep honest expectations (fully-AI tracks underperform
human releases on saves/completion — attribute). In the social stack: the finished track scores the video in
`capcut` (replacing its license-trap library music); **trending audio stays native-only** — Suno is the *owned*
brand sound, not the trend sound; **AI-disclosure** on content where platform/region requires; then
**WoopSocial publishes** the content the music sits under (it does not generate or attach audio).
