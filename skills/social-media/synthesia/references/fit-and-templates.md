# Fit tables, script patterns & worked examples

## The avatar fit test (run before rendering anything)
| Content | Avatar? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding / training / compliance | YES | consistency + localization is the whole value |
| Product explainers, FAQ, support | YES | informational; scale beats warmth |
| Localized versions of existing video | YES | one script → 140+ languages, no re-shoots |
| Faceless educational channel | YES (disclosed) | works; skew corporate-polished — test vs HeyGen feel |
| Founder/trust/parasocial content | NO | clinical reads kill trust → talking-head-and-piece-to-camera |
| Testimonials / emotional persuasion | NO | synthetic warmth underperforms; also consent/authenticity issues |
| Regulated medical/pharma topics | CAUTION | moderation over-flags; 12-24h review delays reported |

## The spoken-word script pattern
Short sentences. Contractions. One idea per sentence. Rhetorical signposts ("Here's the thing.").
SSML: <break> for pauses, phoneme/sub for names, emphasis sparingly. Read it ALOUD before locking. Then —
and only then — generate. (A typo fixed after render = a full ~8-12-min re-render off your minute cap.)

## The localization chain (one master → 20 languages)
Lock master script → render master → 1-click translation/AI dubbing (voice-clone across languages) →
native-speaker QA per language (idioms, formality register) → localized on-screen text + subtitles →
per-market disclosure check → tracked variants of ONE master.

## The plan-math worksheet (before subscribing)
Videos/mo × avg minutes × languages = annual minutes → compare to tier caps (≈120/yr Starter, ≈360/yr Creator;
Enterprise unlimited). Add: custom avatar ≈$1,000/yr each; overages; no rollover; non-refundable annual.
Gate check: SCORM, 1-click translation, Video Agents = Enterprise. Verify all in-app.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (the hybrid split)
"tried replacing myself with my avatar for everything. engagement on the founder posts dropped — people follow
me, not my render. new split: i film the opinions and stories (phone, one take), the avatar does the how-to
library — 40 support videos, 3 languages, disclosed as AI in every description. right tool, right layer." —
the fit test applied, disclosure kept.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (locked-copy discipline)
"Our first avatar video took five renders — every typo we caught after generating cost another ten minutes off
the monthly cap. Now the rule is gentle but firm: the script gets read aloud twice and signed off before we
touch Generate. One render each since. One small step: print the script, read it to a colleague, then render."
— the re-render trap, solved by process.

## Never
Use an avatar where trust content belonged · create an avatar of anyone without verified consent · fake a
celebrity/competitor presenter · skip the AI-disclosure label · paste essay prose into the script box ·
generate before the copy is locked · promise same-hour delivery on a live deadline · assume SCORM/translation
is on your tier · claim WoopSocial generates avatars.
