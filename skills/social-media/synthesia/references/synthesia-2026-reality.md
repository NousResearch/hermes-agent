## What it is (and where it honestly wins/loses)
Synthesia is the **enterprise avatar-video category leader** (used by 90%+ of the Fortune 100 per its own
positioning; attribute): script → a consented AI presenter speaking it in **140–160+ languages** with 1,000+
voices. **Where it wins:** multilingual training/L&D, onboarding, compliance, explainers, localization at
scale — one script, dozens of languages, no re-shoots, a presenter who never has a bad day. **Where it loses
(reviews converge):** avatars read polished-but-**clinical** — they lag real presenters on emotional nuance,
which makes them wrong for trust-led founder content, testimonials, and emotionally-driven marketing (route
those to `talking-head-and-piece-to-camera`); HeyGen-style tools read more native for casual TikTok content.
"Not for you if" (attribute max-productive): solo creators under ~3–4 videos/month, regularly-regulated
medical/pharma topics, cinematic brand storytelling.

## The consent architecture (the industry benchmark — from Synthesia's own docs)
- **Stock avatars** (240+) are built from **real, paid, consenting actors.**
- **Personal Avatars** require **identity verification via a live consent recording** — the person consenting
  must be the person in the source media; source video must be a **single continuous take (1–5 min), never
  spliced or manipulated**; photo avatars: well-lit, waist-up, neutral background, teeth visible (lip-sync).
- **No celebrities/politicians without authorization; deepfakes, misinformation, and impersonation
  prohibited.** Moderation = AI + human. Sharing governance: outfits/spaces shareable, recipients can use but
  not edit; the voice is shared separately.
- Certifications: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 42001, ISO 27701; Content Authenticity Initiative membership
  (Enterprise); customer data not used for base-model training without written approval.
- The honest cost of that governance: **moderation over-flags legitimate content** — healthcare/pharma teams
  report 12–24h manual-review delays (attribute).

failures near deadlines — build buffer.
