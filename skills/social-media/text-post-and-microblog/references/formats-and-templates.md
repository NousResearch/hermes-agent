# Post formats, openers & worked examples

## Text-post formats (pick to fit the idea)
| Format | Shape | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Story | hook → personal/client story → lesson → question | relatability, personal brand |
| PAS | name the problem → agitate the cost → the solution | educational/authority |
| Contrarian | the common belief → why it's wrong → what's true | comments/debate (see contrarian-and-opinion) |
| Listicle | "5 things I learned about X" → tight points | scannable, shareable |
| Before/after | where you/client started → where you ended | proof, transformation |
| One-liner | a single sharp claim/observation | quick takes (works under ~500 chars) |

## Opening lines that earn the tap (front-loaded, true)
A specific result ("We cut churn 18% by deleting one onboarding step.") · a real contrarian take · a concrete
observation · a surprising true stat. NOT: "I'm excited to announce…", "Happy to share…", "Hey everyone", or a
vague question.

## Length cheat-sheet (match content + platform; verify-quarterly)
LinkedIn: ~1,300-1,900 for depth, <500 for takes (cap 3,000) · X: brief, one idea · Threads: ~500 · Bluesky:
~300 · Facebook text: short. Structure beats count — don't pad to a number.

## Formatting restraint check (the 2026 anti-slop test)
≤1 emphasis · no emoji/checkmark per line · no rigid Problem/Solution/Takeaway skeleton · key terms + hashtags
left plain (searchable, screen-reader safe) · white space generous · reads like a human wrote it, not a template.

## Worked example A — "Blunt indie founder" (contrarian, ~short)
"We killed our weekly status meeting 3 months ago.

Nobody's missed it.

Turns out the meeting wasn't keeping us aligned — it was hiding that we weren't. The async doc that replaced it
does the alignment in 10 minutes, on everyone's own time.

What's the meeting you keep out of habit?"
— front-loaded claim, white space, one idea, genuine question. No bait, no decoration.

## Worked example B — "Warm bookkeeping studio" (story, ~medium)
"A client cried on a call last week — happy tears.

She'd spent three years dreading tax season. This was the first year she opened the email and just… felt fine.

Nothing magic. We'd set up one simple monthly habit so nothing piles up. That's it.

If tax season still feels like dread for you, what's the part that scares you most? I read every reply."
— warm, specific, one idea, real invitation. Honest, human, low-pressure.

## Never
Open with an announcement/greeting · over-format into AI-slop · pad to a character count · fabricate a stat or
story · use 'comment YES / like & share' bait · claim WoopSocial natively threads · bury the best line at the
bottom.
