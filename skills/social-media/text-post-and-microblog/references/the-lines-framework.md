# The LINES framework — text posts that earn the read

A text post is built line by line, and the first line is ~80% of the job. Each letter is a gate; most weak posts
fail at L (soft opener) or N (a wall of text, or the opposite — over-decorated AI-slop formatting).

## L — Lead with the line that earns the tap
The first ~140-210 chars (the mobile "see more" fold) are a **mini-post**: write the first line as if it's the
only line anyone reads, because for most it is.
- **Front-load the insight** — a bold claim, a specific result, a surprising stat (a true one), a contrarian take,
  or a sharp observation. Never open with "I'm excited to announce…", a greeting, or a flat question.
- Design for the **mobile** cutoff; preview how the feed renders it.

## I — Isolate one idea
A post is **one** clear idea/story/take — not an essay. Ruthless focus; if you're making three points, it's three
posts. **Match length to the content type and platform** (LinkedIn ~1,300-1,900 for depth, <500 for a take; X
brief; Threads ~500; Bluesky ~300) — not a generic number. Structure (hook → turn → payoff → CTA), not character
count, does the work.

## N — Negative space
- **White space = oxygen** — short 1-2 sentence paragraphs, line breaks between thoughts; kill the wall of text
  (it lifts reading time ~20%).
- **But don't over-format.** The Unicode-bold-everywhere, emoji/checkmark-per-line, rigid-template look reads as
  AI slop in 2026 and gets scrolled. One emphasis at most; keep key terms plain (searchable + screen-reader safe).
  Restraint beats decoration.

## E — Earn the read
The body **delivers on the first line's promise** — a real insight, story, or value, one point per paragraph, in
a human brand voice (not corporate, not template). No formatting trick saves a post with no real idea, and the
agent never fabricates the idea, a stat, or a story. **Native over external links** (link in comments where the
platform punishes outbound).

## S — Spark a reply
End on a strong last line + a **genuine, specific engagement driver** — a real question ("what am I missing?"), an
invited counter-take, or an open loop. Never "comment YES / like & share" (engagement-bait, suppressed). Optimize
for **comments/saves/dwell**, not likes. Then route platform specifics to `linkedin-post-writer` / `threads-post`
/ X / `caption-writer` / `thread-writer`; **WoopSocial publishes** to supported platforms (the human posts to
advisory ones).
