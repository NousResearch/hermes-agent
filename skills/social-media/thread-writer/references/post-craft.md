# Post Craft — writing the posts

A thread is read post by post. Each one is its own unit on the screen, so each has to be written
as one — not as a sentence sliced from a paragraph.

## The opener formula

Post 1 does two jobs in one or two lines: **hook + promise.**

- **Hook** — a real mechanism from `hook-writer` (curiosity, stakes, specificity, contrarian,
  story). Stop the scroll.
- **Promise** — tell the reader what they'll get by staying ("here's exactly how," "7 lessons from
  it," "the full story").

It must also **stand alone as a strong post**, because the opener is what people reshare — and the
reshare is how the thread reaches new people. If post 1 only makes sense as setup for post 2, it's
too weak.

Opener shapes (rewrite in voice):
- *Specificity + promise:* "We deleted 80% of our features and revenue went up. Here's what we
  learned 🧵"
- *Stakes + promise:* "This one mistake cost us our biggest customer. The 5 lessons so you avoid
  it:"
- *Story open:* "Two years ago we almost shut down. Here's the 30 days that saved us 👇"

## Each post is a standalone unit

- **Front-load the point.** The first line of every post is what shows in the timeline — lead with
  it, no warm-up.
- **One beat per post.** One step, one lesson, one scene.
- **Readable within the post.** Use line breaks for scannability; don't write a wall.

## Length discipline

- X: **280 characters per post** (longer with Premium, but short travels and reads better). Don't
  split a sentence awkwardly across posts; end posts on clean breaks.
- Keep each post tight — a thread is a series of punches, not paragraphs.

## Transitions and momentum

- Number posts ("3/" or "3/9") where it helps orientation and signals more is coming.
- Use light forward pulls between posts ("but here's the part that surprised us") — without
  withholding value for cheap.
- Cut every filler post and every "as I mentioned" — momentum dies on throat-clearing.

## The close + CTA

The final post:

1. **Lands the payoff** — the lesson, the recap, the result. Don't trail off.
2. **Makes one CTA**, in voice, matched to the goal:
   - *Growth (X-native):* "If this was useful, RT the first post so others see it 👇" and/or "I
     write about {topic} — follow for more."
   - *Traffic:* link to the full piece (often in a reply, since links can dampen reach).
   - *Community:* "What would you add?"

**One ask only.** The reshare-the-opener move is the highest-leverage growth CTA on X — it sends
new readers back to the top of the thread.

## A quick post-by-post check

For each post ask: does it **front-load**, deliver **one beat**, stay **in voice**, fit the
**limit**, and **pull forward**? If a post fails any, fix it. If it adds nothing, delete it.
