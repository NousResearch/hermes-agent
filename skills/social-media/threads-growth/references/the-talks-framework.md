# The TALKS framework — grow a Threads account the way the algorithm rewards

Threads rewards **conversation, velocity, and consistency** — and the single biggest lever is **replying
more than you post.** TALKS structures the account strategy. The agent **strategizes + drafts the original
posts + can draft suggested replies**; **WoopSocial publishes the original posts**; the **reply flywheel,
Communities, DMs, dashboards, and analytics are human/native.**

## T — Talk more than you post
- **Mosseri's #1 lever:** **reply much more than you post.** Your **own replies count**; **leap into
  relevant conversations fast.** *This reply flywheel is the engine — and it's run by a human/
  `community-management` (no reply surface in WoopSocial).*

## A — Anchor one niche/angle
- **One sharp value promise**; **niche consistency** (1–2 areas) → the **interest-graph** algorithm
  classifies you and the audience trusts you.

## L — Land the first hour
- **Engagement velocity is the #1 factor** (50 likes/30 min > 100/24h). **Post when the audience is awake**
  and **stay to reply** in the first hour. Add **one topic tag** for discovery.

## K — Keep it original + positive
- **Original > recycled** (recycled demoted); **NO engagement-bait** (downranked); **positive/constructive**
  (negativity throttled). **Format mix:** photos (+60%), video w/ captions, links (+17%, under media);
  **one topic tag.** (Post craft → `threads-post`.)

## S — Sustain + funnel to Instagram
- **Consistency 3–5/week** (not >3/day); **compounds over 60–90 days.** **Threads = discovery → Instagram =
  conversion** (repurpose top posts in 24–72h). **Track** reach, **replies ÷ reach**, follower growth,
  profile visits.

## The growth plan a request should fill
```
NICHE: one sharp value promise + 1-2 topic areas (interest-graph classification)
REPLY ENGINE: reply more than you post; leap into relevant conversations fast (human/community-management)
VELOCITY: post when awake + stay the first hour; one topic tag; aim for fast early replies
FORMAT: photos/video > text-only; links under media; original + positive; one tag (-> threads-post for the post)
SUSTAIN/FUNNEL: 3-5/week, 60-90 day horizon; Threads (discovery) -> Instagram (conversion); repurpose in 24-72h
MEASURE: reach, replies/reach, follower growth, profile visits (native analytics; never fabricated)
```
