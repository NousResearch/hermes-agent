# Threads growth 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (Threads + Mosseri ship changes often).*

## The POV: conversation-first — grow by replying more than you post
Threads is **~500M MAU (mid-2026 estimates) / ~141.5M daily mobile users (more than X's ~125M — though X
still leads on web + total MAU), with a ~6.25% median engagement
rate (≈73.6% higher than X's 3.6%)** — and only ~1% of brands post consistently (an open window). It's
**neither X nor Instagram**: a **conversation-first**, **positive/constructive** culture where **sarcasm,
dunks, and negativity get throttled.** Mosseri's core advice: **"reply much more than you post."** Replies
are as valuable as posts, **your own replies to comments count toward the post's total**, and broadcasting
without conversing makes reach plateau.

## The algorithm (account-level)
- **Multi-model AI** (not one algorithm). **For You = AI-ranked** (an **interest graph**, not a follow
  graph); **Following = reverse-chronological.** 2026: **"Dear Algo"** lets users steer For You in plain
  language. Loop: gather inventory → read signals → predict (like / reply / follow / profile-visit / keep
  reading). **Smaller accounts can reach big audiences** here.
- **Engagement velocity is the #1 ranking factor** — **50 likes in 30 min beats 100 over 24h**; **the first
  hour is critical** (post and walk away = miss the reply window = tanks distribution).
- **Replies are weighted heaviest**; **saves + shares + meaningful conversation > raw likes** (50 likes/10
  saves beats 200 likes/2 saves). **Not all replies are good** — engagement-bait replies are discounted.
- **Instagram graph cross-pollinates** (following/viewing an IG profile boosts their Threads content) — but
  **an IG following ≠ automatic Threads success** (different algorithms; earn it here).
- **Originality + credibility prioritized** (recycled content + growth hacks demoted). **Engagement-bait
  actively downranked** (asking for likes/follows/shares is penalized). The **Account Status dashboard** tells
  you if your posts are eligible for non-follower recommendation.

## What wins (format mix — post craft lives in `threads-post`)
- **Photos get ~60% more engagement** than text-only; **video with captions** performs best; **text-only
  ranks last.** **Links now ~+17%** vs text-only (Mosseri reversed the penalty in 2025) — still under media.
- **ONE topic tag per post** (NOT hashtags; Threads limits to one to discourage spam — no multi-tag
  strategy). Topic-tagged posts get more views + discovery. Natural-language keywords in the **first two
  lines** help the AI read intent.

## Cadence, niche, timing + the compounding curve
- **Consistency over volume: 3–5 posts/week** optimal; **>3/day risks fatigue** and splits engagement.
- **Niche consistency** (1–2 areas) → the algorithm classifies you + the audience trusts you; "one sharp
  value promise per audience."
- **Timing:** strong first-hour windows (e.g. Wed 7am, Thu/Fri mornings — verify); post when the audience is
  awake. **Growth compounds** — most accounts take **60–90 days** of consistent posting before momentum
  (≈200–500 followers in 30 days for active accounts — verify). **Communities** (post original ideas there
  first → repurpose) + **DMs** (concierge/VIP) help.

## The Threads → Instagram funnel + metrics
- **Threads = discovery funnel** (topical reach) → **Instagram = conversion funnel** (deeper engagement,
  commerce, richer formats). Route attention deliberately; **repurpose top Threads posts within 24–72h.**
  (Benchmarks, verify: Threads→IG profile-visit conv ~6–12%; keep reply rate above ~2%/post.)
- **Track weekly:** reach per post, **replies per post (the heaviest signal — track replies ÷ reach)**,
  follower-growth rate, profile visits. High reach + flat follower growth → the hook/value needs work.
