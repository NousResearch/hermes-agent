# The REPLY framework — a Threads post built for conversation

Threads rewards what most platforms only claim to: genuine conversation. **Replies outweigh likes**, and
Mosseri's rule is **"reply more than you post."** REPLY writes the post AND is honest that the post is the
smaller half. **WoopSocial publishes the post**; the **replying that wins reach is a human job**.

## R — Reason to reply
- Write a **conversation starter**: a question, a hot take, a genuine opinion — give people something to
  respond to. **Not** a broadcast or press release. (Avoid engagement bait — it's penalized + dishonest.)

## E — Engagement velocity first
- The **first 30–90 min** decide distribution. **Post at a peak window** (schedule it there) and **be ready
  to reply fast** — early replies compound into For You reach.

## P — Personality + niche
- **Authentic, human, original** (credibility/originality are rewarded; polished promo is suppressed). Keep
  a **consistent niche** and add **one primary topic tag** so the topic-neighbourhood AI categorizes you.

## L — Light media + link if it fits
- **Photos out-engage text-only (~+60%)**; video <30s with captions works. **Links are now rewarded** —
  put them **inline** (no first-comment tax). Stay within **500 characters.** Media supports the
  conversation; it doesn't replace the reason to reply.

## Y — You reply more than you post
- The post is the **smaller half**; the **replies are where reach is won.** **WoopSocial publishes/schedules
  the post**; a **human (or `community-management`) does the replying** — there's no reply surface in the API.
  Draft reply angles so the human can move fast.

## The Threads post a request should fill
```
ANGLE: the conversation starter (question / hot take / opinion); niche fit + goal (replies/profile visits)
POST: <=500 chars, human voice, one reason to reply; one topic tag; optional photo/video; inline link if useful
VELOCITY: schedule to a peak window; reply plan for the first 30-90 min
PUBLISH (WoopSocial): single Threads text/media post (no chain/poll/voice/GIF via API); validate before publish
REPLY WORK (human / community-management): the half that drives reach; metrics from native analytics (never fabricated)
```
