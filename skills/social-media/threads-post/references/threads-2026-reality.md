# Threads 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly (Mosseri ships ranking changes often).*

## The platform
Text-first Meta platform (Instagram-linked login, same handle/followers), launched July 2023. By 2026:
**~400M+ MAU** and **~141.5M daily mobile users — passed X's ~125M** (Jan 2026). Benchmark **engagement
rate ~6.25%** (vs X ~3.6%); **Buffer 2026: ~73.6% higher engagement than X.** Gen Z/Millennial-heavy. **Ads
went global Jan 2026** → expect **organic reach to tighten** as it monetizes (an open-but-closing window).

## The POV: conversation-first — reply more than you post
Mosseri, directly: **"if you're really trying to grow your presence, you should reply much more than you
post."** Threads is an AI recommendation system that rewards **genuine conversation**, not broadcasting.
Write **conversation starters** (questions, hot takes, opinions) — not press releases. **Spend ~10–15
min/day replying** in your niche; that's how new audiences discover you.

## Ranking signals (Mosseri-confirmed, 2025–26)
- **Replies outweigh likes**; **conversation depth** (multi-reply threads) ranks higher than surface likes.
- **Engagement velocity** — the **first 30–90 minutes** decide whether a post gets broader distribution.
- **For You feed** surfaces **new/smaller accounts** to non-followers — the main growth lane; still open.
- **Links are now rewarded** — Mosseri **reversed the earlier link penalty**; URLs get *greater* value, so
  put the link **inline** (no first-comment tax like X/LinkedIn).
- **Engagement-bait crackdown** — the algorithm distinguishes genuine replies from bait; "not all replies
  are good." **No "comment YES below."**
- **Credibility + originality** prioritized — authentic, original content over recycled growth-hacks.
- **Account Status** dashboard tells you if your posts are eligible for recommendation (shadowban transparency).
- **"Dear Algo"** (Feb 2026) — users tune their feed for ~3 days; makes authentic engagement matter more.

## Topic tags + niche neighbourhoods
Threads uses **topic tags** (pill-style labels that appear as you type), **not hashtags** — there's no
trending-hashtag culture. **Most posts get ONE primary topic tag.** The AI groups accounts into
**topic-neighbourhoods** by recurring themes, so **niche consistency is critical** — posting unrelated
topics confuses the algorithm.

## Formats
**Up to 500 characters** of text; supports **photos, video (up to 5 min), polls (≤4 options), GIFs
(GIPHY), voice notes.** **Photos get ~60% more engagement than text-only** (~25% of posts have images);
video <30s with captions works. But **substance = conversation** — media supports, it doesn't replace the
reason to reply.

## Avoid / cross-platform
**Avoid:** polished promo, generic motivation, press-release tone, **engagement bait**, aesthetics-over-
conversation, and **cross-posting Instagram captions verbatim.** Deep **Instagram integration** helps
(share to IG Stories, seed from IG audience) — but treat Threads as its own platform. **Post ~1×/day,
consistently.**
