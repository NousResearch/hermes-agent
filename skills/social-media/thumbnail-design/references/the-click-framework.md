# The CLICK framework — design a thumbnail that earns the click honestly

A thumbnail's whole job is the **click that leads to a watch** — so it has to be a **promise the video
keeps**, designed as a **unit with the title**, and **legible on a phone**. CLICK gets there. The agent
**designs + briefs + plans the test**; a **human or image tool makes the raster**; the **YouTube thumbnail
is uploaded + A/B-tested natively** (no WoopSocial field).

## C — Clarify the promise
- Write the **one-sentence promise** ("this video shows [audience] how/why [outcome] by [mechanism]").
  Design the **thumbnail + title as one unit** — **show vs tell, never repeat.** **Promise, not poster** —
  the watch-time A/B test punishes clickbait.

## L — Lead with one focal point
- **One or two hero elements**, one focal point; **minimal/blurred background.** Parseable in **under a
  second.** A single **directional element** (arrow/gaze/circle) to point at the key detail.

## I — Invoke genuine emotion
- An **expressive, genuine face** with **gaze** pointing at the key element — **if it adds context.** Skip
  the face when the brand is concept-focused or the visual subject is stronger. **Match the expression to
  the content** (no fake shock).

## C — Contrast + concise text
- **High contrast:** complementary pairs, **avoid YouTube red/white**, **60-30-10**, pass the **B&W test**,
  works in light + dark mode. **0–4 bold sans-serif words** with an outline, ~20–30% of frame, **not a
  duplicate of the title.** **Mobile-legible at ~168×94**; **safe zones** (avoid bottom-right badge +
  bottom 15%). **Specs: 1280×720, 16:9, JPG/PNG.**

## K — Keep testing
- **3 meaningfully-different concepts** (different promises/emotions), changing **ONE variable** so results
  attribute. **Native Test & Compare** picks by **watch-time share** (not Shorts; needs traffic; ~14 days).
  A **human uploads + runs** it in Studio. **Keep a testing log**; hold a **brand-consistent template.**

## The thumbnail brief a request should fill
```
PROMISE: one sentence; thumbnail+title as a unit (show vs tell, no repeat); promise not poster
FOCAL POINT: one hero element + minimal background; optional directional element
FACE/EMOTION: genuine expression + gaze (or skip if no context)
CONTRAST/TEXT: complementary high contrast (avoid YT red/white, 60-30-10, B&W test); 0-4 bold words + outline; safe zones; 1280x720
TEST: 3 meaningfully-different concepts, one variable; native Test & Compare (watch-time share); human uploads/runs; raster by image tool/designer; CTR native (never fabricated)
```
