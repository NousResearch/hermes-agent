# Completion, Search & Volume — the three TikTok pillars

The three growth levers that are *distinctively* TikTok (more so than on Instagram). Pull these and
the FYP works in your favor.

> Re-verify benchmarks quarterly (algorithm in flux through ~mid-2026); the principles are durable.

## Pillar 1 — Completion + rewatch (the dominant signal)

TikTok rewards one thing above all: **content people watch to the end, then watch again.**

- **Aim ~70%+ completion** to have a real shot at going wide. That bar rose from ~50% in 2024, which
  means **hooks and pacing have to be stronger than they used to be.**
- **The first ~1 second** decides Phase 1. No intro, no logo, no slow build — open on the claim, the
  stakes, or a striking visual. (→ `hook-writer`, `tiktok-script`.)
- **Design for rewatch/loop** — the single most under-used lever. A seamless loop (the end flows into
  the start), a fast line worth catching, a hidden detail, or density that needs two watches. A
  rewatch counts the whole video again — it's the highest-leverage second you'll spend.
- **Match length to completion, not to a number.** A 30-second video at 80% beats a 3-minute one at
  40%. Shorter is usually safer for completion; go long only when the payoff sustains it.

If completion is low, nothing else matters — fix this first.

## Pillar 2 — Search / SEO (the evergreen pillar, bigger than on IG)

TikTok is a **search engine**, especially for younger users, and search is a *larger* growth channel
here than on Instagram. Most TikTok searches happen during exploration (people browsing for ideas),
so search-optimized content gets discovered for **months**, independent of the FYP lottery.

- **The triple mention:** put your **primary keyword in the spoken audio, the on-screen text, and the
  caption** (front). TikTok transcribes audio and reads on-screen text — saying it + showing it +
  writing it is the core ranking move. (→ `tiktok-script`, `hashtag-strategy`/`social-seo`.)
- **Write captions like search queries** — the phrases people actually type ("how to schedule posts
  for free"), not clever copy.
- **Build keyword clusters** — multiple videos around a topic make TikTok see you as the answer for
  that query. TikTok content also ranks on **Google**, a bonus discovery lane.
- Hashtags are supporting keyword signals (treat as keywords, niche over `#fyp`) — not the main lever.

Search turns each video into a long-lived asset, not a 48-hour flash. This is how you grow *between*
viral hits.

## Pillar 3 — Volume of at-bats + read the patterns

Because every video is an independent, cheap **test**, growth is a numbers-and-learning game:

- **Consistent at-bats.** A sustainable cadence (commonly ~**3–5×/week**, more if you can keep
  quality) gives the algorithm more tests to find your winners and learns your style. Consistency
  beats sporadic viral attempts; once-a-week-or-less reduces priority. **Match cadence to capacity**
  (→ `content-calendar`) — quality-gated, because low-effort posts get buried.
- **Judge by patterns, not single videos.** One flop is noise. Look across many videos: which
  *hooks*, *topics*, or *formats* repeatedly land? That pattern is your signal — do more of it.
  (→ `viral-reverse-engineering` to extract the mechanism.)
- **The back-catalog keeps working** — videos can resurface weeks later, and search keeps old videos
  discoverable. Every good post is a durable asset, so volume compounds.

The mindset: **produce → test → read the pattern → double down → prune.** Not "make one perfect video
and hope."
