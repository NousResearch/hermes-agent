# Examples — growth diagnoses

Worked TikTok diagnoses showing the test-and-expand loop, the broken-phase call, and the routing —
plus honest expectations. Subject: a solo founder building a SaaS social scheduler. Voice context is
the blunt indie-founder voice; your output uses the loaded `voice.md`, `social-strategy.md`,
`audience.md`.

---

## Diagnosis 1 — "200 views then it stops"

**Signals (native analytics):** videos stall at ~200 views; average watch time low; slow intros;
completion well under 70%.
**Broken stage:** **Phase-1 death.** The video isn't holding the test audience, so it never expands.
**Prescription:**
- Rebuild the **1-second hook** (open on the claim/stakes; no "hey guys"). → `hook-writer`,
  `tiktok-script`.
- Raise **completion** — faster pacing, cut dead air, shorter cut, add a **loop/rewatch trigger**.
- This is a completion problem, not a followers or volume problem.
→ Re-check average watch time / completion % in analytics.

## Diagnosis 2 — "Good views, barely any followers"

**Signals:** some videos hit 20–50k; follower count crawls; profile is a vague bio; every video is a
one-off with no through-line.
**Broken stage:** **Follow-conversion.** Cold viewers watch and leave — no reason to follow.
**Prescription:**
- Fix the **profile** (3-second test, searchable name, who/what/why, pinned). → `profile-optimization`.
- Give people a reason to follow: a **Series** ("Building [Product] — Part N") so viewers come back
  for the next episode, and a **consistent niche** so a follow means "more of this."
→ Track **follows-per-video** and profile visits.

## Diagnosis 3 — "I rely on the FYP lottery and it's inconsistent"

**Signals:** reach swings wildly; nothing discoverable between hits; no keywords in captions/audio.
**Broken stage:** **Search** (missing the evergreen lane) + **volume/consistency.**
**Prescription:**
- Build the **search lane**: triple-mention keywords (spoken + on-screen + caption), captions as
  search queries, keyword clusters. → `hashtag-strategy`/`social-seo`, `tiktok-script`.
- Sustainable **at-bats** (~3–5×/week, quality-gated) → `content-calendar`; **read patterns** across
  videos to find repeatable winners → `viral-reverse-engineering`.
→ Watch the **traffic-source** split (FYP vs Search vs Profile) climb on Search.

## Honest expectations (say this)

- **The interest graph is meritocratic but ruthless** — it rewards what the video *does to viewers*,
  not your follower count or effort. Good news for small accounts; humbling for everyone.
- **Followers don't buy reach.** Reach is per-video (completion, rewatch, search). Followers are the
  business asset (LIVE/Shop/owned audience).
- **One flop is noise.** Judge by patterns across many at-bats; the back-catalog can resurface weeks
  later.
- **No hacks, no bought followers, no guarantees** — virality has luck; you stack the odds.
- **Flux:** the US algorithm is retraining through ~mid-2026 — expect swings; hold to fundamentals.
- **No WoopSocial analytics** — read native TikTok analytics (needs a business/creator account).

## What the diagnoses share

- They name **one broken stage/phase** of the loop, not a tip list.
- They **route to the specific skill** that fixes it.
- They center **completion/rewatch**, the **search** lane, and **consistent at-bats**.
- They treat **followers as a business outcome**, set **honest expectations**, and use **vanity-free
  metrics**.
