# Features & Diagnosis

The TikTok-native growth features, and the diagnostic that routes a stuck account to the skill that
fixes it. tiktok-growth diagnoses and orchestrates; it doesn't re-do the content.

## Native growth features (use the right one for your stage)

- **Stitch** — clip the start of another video and add your take. Embeds you in an existing
  conversation; contextual relevance retains viewers and lifts watch time. Great for reactions/hot
  takes in your niche.
- **Duet** — pair your video beside a trending/high-visibility one; piggybacks its momentum and
  exposes you to that creator's audience across FYPs.
- **Series / Parts (Part 1 / 2 / 3)** — TikTok recommends episode 2 to viewers of episode 1, driving
  **session depth and follows**. One of the strongest follow-conversion tactics — give people a
  reason to come back.
- **Pinned comment** — within the first hour, pin a comment that seeds conversation or points to
  "Part 2" / a resource. Drives comment engagement and watch time.
- **Video replies to comments** — answer a good comment with a new video; feeds the comment culture
  and gives you free content ideas that already have demand.
- **Playlists** — group videos by topic for **bingeing and search** (back-catalog discovery).
- **LIVE** — algorithmic promotion for active creators + monetization (gifts, subscriptions, live
  shopping). **Requires ~1,000 followers.** A *community/business* tool for warm fans — **not** an
  early-growth reach lever.

Match the feature to the stage: Stitch/Duet/Series help **reach + follows** early; LIVE/playlists
deepen an **existing** audience.

## Niche clarity — train the interest graph

TikTok clusters content and viewers by interest ("sides of TikTok" — TechTok, BookTok, etc.). A
**tight, consistent niche** trains the algorithm on who to show you to; a scattered account (gaming +
cooking + marketing) confuses the interest graph and stalls. Define the lane, reinforce it with
keywords (caption/on-screen/audio), and use niche community hashtags to signal which "side" you're
on.

## Followers are a business asset, not a reach mechanism

Because follower count isn't a ranking factor, the goal of growth isn't "followers for reach." It's:

- **Reach** comes from content performance (completion, rewatch, search) — per video.
- **Followers** are your **owned audience** — they unlock **LIVE** (≥1k), **Shop/affiliate**,
  repeat-binge of your back-catalog, and a warm base that improves early signals. They're a business
  outcome, not the reach input.

So measure reach/views/completion for distribution, and followers/profile-visits/leads for the
business — don't chase follower count as if it drives reach.

## The diagnosis — symptom → broken stage → fix

| Symptom | Broken stage | Primary fix (skill) |
|---|---|---|
| ~200 views then stops | **Phase-1 death** — weak 1-sec hook / low completion | `tiktok-script`, `hook-writer` (hook + completion + loop) |
| Decent early views, plateaus fast | **Phase-2** — doesn't hold a colder/broader audience | broaden the hook, add a rewatch trigger, less inside-baseball |
| Views but **few follows** | **Follow-conversion** — no reason to follow / weak profile | `profile-optimization` + **Series** + consistent niche |
| Not found in search | **Search** — weak keywords | `hashtag-strategy`/`social-seo` + `tiktok-script` keyword layer |
| One hit, then nothing | **Volume/consistency** — no repeatable system | `content-calendar` + read patterns (`viral-reverse-engineering`) |
| Scattered, can't get traction | **Niche** — interest graph can't categorize you | tighten the niche/lane |
| Followers don't engage / no community | **Retention/community** — no relationship | comments, video replies, Series, LIVE (if ≥1k) |
| Views dropped suddenly in 2026 | **Flux/quality** — algorithm retraining + low-effort detection | fundamentals, original native quality |

Most "TikTok won't grow" problems are **one** of these — find which, fix that.

## What doesn't work (skip)

- **Buying followers / engagement / pods** — fake signals hurt performance and risk the account; and
  followers don't even drive reach.
- **`#fyp` / generic mega-tags** — no FYP boost; niche/keyword tags only (see `hashtag-strategy`).
- **Polished, ad-style videos** — underperform vs native; "make TikToks, not ads."
- **Low-effort volume** — detected and buried; at-bats must be quality-gated.
- **Quitting after one flop** — single videos are noise; read patterns across many.
