# Hooks & Retention on TikTok

Watch time, completion, and rewatches are the FYP's top signals — so the hook and the retention
design are where a TikTok is won or lost. This is the craft layer.

## The first second

You have ~1 second to stop the scroll, and ~3 to convince them to stay. Fire on three channels at
once:

- **Verbal hook** — the first words out of your mouth (or the voiceover's). No preamble.
- **On-screen text hook** — a bold text line that states the stakes/curiosity (also caught by
  sound-off viewers).
- **Visual hook** — motion, a striking first frame, a pattern interrupt (do something unexpected in
  frame).

Pull the hook mechanism from `hook-writer`. Banned openings: "Hey guys," a logo/intro card, a slow
pan, "In this video I'm going to…". Start *in* the action or the claim.

## Hook angles that work on TikTok

- **The bold claim / contrarian** — "Your most-requested feature is the one to delete."
- **The open loop** — "I lost $40k learning this. Here's the 20-second version."
- **The result / number** — "We went from 0 to 10k users with one boring button."
- **The callout** — "If you run a SaaS, stop doing this."
- **The POV / relatable** — "POV: the feature everyone begged for, that nobody used."
- **The "wait for it"** — promise a payoff that requires watching to the end (loop fuel).

Whatever the angle, it must be **true** — no clickbait the video doesn't pay off. A betrayed hook
tanks watch time on the *next* video too (TikTok learns).

## Retention design — keep them to the end

- **No intro tax.** The value starts at second 0. Cut anything before the hook.
- **Fast pace.** Tight cuts, no dead air, no rambling. Trim every pause.
- **Open loops.** Pose the question early, answer it late ("the third one surprised me").
- **Re-hook mid-video.** A second curiosity bump around the middle for longer videos.
- **Front-load value, but hold one payoff** for the end to pull completion.
- **Captions/on-screen text** keep sound-off viewers in.

## Design for the rewatch / loop

Rewatches are one of the most powerful signals, and the most underused lever:

- **Seamless loop** — end the video so the last frame/line flows back into the first, so it replays
  without a seam. The viewer watches twice before realizing.
- **The rewatch trigger** — a detail, a fast line, or a "did you catch…" that rewards (and invites)
  a second watch.
- **Density** — pack enough that one watch isn't enough to absorb it (great for listicles/tips).

## Retention killers (cut these)

- A slow or branded intro.
- Burying the point ("so basically, what happened was, um…").
- Dead air / long pauses / filler.
- A hook the video doesn't deliver.
- Reading a script stiffly — TikTok rewards energy and a real person.

## The measurable check

A good TikTok script can answer: what's the **1-second hook** (all three channels)? where's the
**open loop**? what's the **loop/rewatch trigger**? is there **any** dead air? If the hook is weak
or the loop is missing, fix those before anything else.
