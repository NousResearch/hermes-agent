# The Remix — executing a trend-jack

A trend has cleared all three gates. Now the job is to ride it **fast** while adding your **own
angle** — because a logo-slapped copy of a viral video earns nothing, but the trend's structure +
your perspective can.

## Add an angle, don't just copy

The difference between a trend-jack that works and one that flops:

- **Copy** — recreate the viral video with your product in it. Generic, forgettable, slightly
  embarrassing.
- **Remix** — take the trend's **structure/sound/format** and fill it with **your niche's specific
  truth**. The familiarity of the trend + the freshness of your angle is what earns the share.

Ask: *what does this trend look like through our world?* A trending "expectation vs reality" format
becomes *your customers'* expectation vs reality. A trending sound scores *your* niche's running
joke. The trend is the vehicle; your perspective is the payload.

## Keep the brand voice

A trend-jack still sounds like you. Don't abandon `voice.md` to chase trend-speak — forced slang and
out-of-character energy read as a brand trying too hard. The best version feels like *your brand
noticed the trend and had something to say*, not like you hired a teenager for one post. Translate
the trend into your voice, don't translate your voice into the trend.

## Add value or wit, not just presence

Earn the spot in the feed: be **funny**, **useful**, **relatable**, or **surprising** with your
angle. Mere participation ("we're doing the trend too!") isn't enough. The trend gets you the
attention; the angle decides whether it converts to a follow/share or a scroll.

## Speed without sloppiness

Trends decay, so move fast — but "fast" means **decisive**, not careless. The gates are non-negotiable
even under time pressure (a fast safety mistake is the expensive kind). Keep production light and
native (especially on TikTok — handheld beats polished); ship while the window's open. Hand execution
to the right content skill so it's still well-made:

- short video → `tiktok-script` / `reels-script`
- caption/text → `caption-writer`
- carousel → `carousel-writer`
- then schedule/publish → `scheduling-and-queue`.

## The fast lane (for brands/teams)

Trend-jacking dies in approval chains — by the time legal signs off, the trend is dead. So set up a
**fast lane in advance**:

- Pre-agree **guardrails** (the SAFETY hard-no list, brand voice, what's off-limits) so trends that
  fit can ship within hours without a committee.
- Name **who can approve** a trend-jack quickly.
- Anything that touches the caution/no zones still escalates — the fast lane is for clearly-safe
  trends only.

## Don't over-chase — reactive complements evergreen

Trend-jacking is the **reactive** slot, not the whole strategy. A brand that *only* chases trends has
no identity, exhausts its team, and rides other people's moments forever. Keep it as a portion of the
mix (the reactive slots in `content-calendar`), on top of evergreen pillar content that builds who
you actually are. Ride trends to get discovered; post your own content to be remembered.

## Measure honestly

WoopSocial has no analytics surface — so judge a trend-jack by whether it **fit, was safe, and
landed** (native platform insights the user pulls, or qualitative read), not by metrics this skill
can't provide. One trend-jack flopping isn't failure; forcing the next one would be.
