# Trend Types & Finding Them

What counts as a "trend," how each type is ridden, and where to find them — plus an honest note on
what this skill can and can't do about *discovery*.

## Honest first: this skill vets and executes; it doesn't have a live trend feed

Trends are real-time. What's trending right now changes hourly and can't be recalled from a model's
training data — so **don't trust any AI (including this one) to tell you what's currently trending.**
This skill's value is the **judgment (the three gates)** and the **execution (the remix)**. For
*discovery*, the user brings a specific trend, or scans the live sources below. Treat any "here's
what's trending" claim without a live source as unreliable.

## Where to find trends (live sources)

- **Your own FYP / feed** — the fastest read on what's rising in *your* niche. Watch what your
  audience and peer creators are doing.
- **Platform trend surfaces** — TikTok's Creative Center / trend tabs, Reels trend tools, YouTube
  trending, X trending, Pinterest trends. (Re-verify which exist; platforms change them.)
- **Trending sounds** — the platform's audio library, sorted by what's rising (catch on the way up).
- **Google Trends** — for search-driven and news-adjacent topics.
- **Reddit / niche communities** — early signal for memes and topics before they hit the mainstream.
- **Trend newsletters / creator roundups** — curated weekly trend digests for your vertical.
- **Real-time tools** — social listening / trend-spotting tools the user may have.

The edge is being **early in your niche**, which usually means watching your own corner closely, not
chasing platform-wide mega-trends everyone's already on.

## Types of trends (each ridden differently)

- **Trending sounds/audio** (TikTok, Reels) — use the rising sound under your own content; often the
  single biggest reach lever on short video. Sync your hook to it. (Mind commercial sound licensing
  for brand accounts.)
- **Trending formats / memes / templates** — a repeatable structure ("expectation vs reality,"
  "tell me X without telling me X," a specific edit). Take the *structure*, fill it with your niche.
  The most brand-friendly type.
- **Trending topics / hashtags** — a subject everyone's posting about. Add a genuine take; don't just
  tag in.
- **News / cultural moments (newsjacking)** — reacting to real events. Highest reach, highest risk —
  the SAFETY gate matters most here. Light cultural moments (a big game, an awards show, a product
  launch in your space) are usually fine; serious/sensitive news usually isn't.
- **Platform features/trends** — a new feature or native behavior the algorithm is pushing; early
  adoption can earn reach.
- **Seasonal / predictable** — recurring moments (industry events, seasonal hooks). The one type you
  *can* plan ahead for — build these into the `content-calendar` instead of reacting.

## Match the type to the gates

Sounds and formats usually clear SAFETY easily and live or die on FIT + TIMING. News/cultural
moments are the opposite — they can be timely and on-brand but **fail SAFETY** the moment the event
is serious. Knowing which type you're holding tells you which gate to scrutinize hardest.
