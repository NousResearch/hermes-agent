# Scope, distinctions + connections

ugc-and-influencer is a **strategy/relationship skill** — it sources and vets the creators, writes the brief,
inks the rights, enforces the disclosure, and runs the customer-UGC pipeline. The agent **advises + drafts +
plans**; **WoopSocial publishes the brand's own posts** (rights-cleared UGC reshares, campaign posts); the
**outreach, contract, payment, ads and the creator's post are native/human.**

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** defines the creator spec + vetting checklist (fit, engagement rate, audience-authenticity
  red flags); drafts the **outreach**; writes the **one-page brief** (goal + mandatories + creative
  freedom); drafts the **rights/usage term sheet** — a checklist to negotiate from, **not legal advice** (a
  human/lawyer reviews + signs); writes the **UGC ask** (comment/DM/email/insert copy) and the brand's
  reshare posts; plans measurement (tracked links/codes per creator).
- **WoopSocial publishes the BRAND'S OWN posts to the BRAND'S OWN accounts**: rights-cleared UGC reshares
  (credited in the caption) and the brand's campaign posts. It does **NOT**: **DM or negotiate with
  creators** (no DM surface — outreach is human, optionally shaped by `social-selling-and-dm`), **pay
  creators / hold escrow / manage contracts**, **run whitelisting, Spark Ads, or partnership ads** (those
  live in the native ad managers with creator authorization), **apply the native paid-partnership label**
  (the creator toggles branded-content tools natively on their post), **post to a creator's account**, or
  **verify followers / audit audiences** (external tools + human judgment).
- **Disclosure is a hard rule.** Every **paid** post and every **gifted-product** post carries clear,
  conspicuous, upfront disclosure (#ad / native paid-partnership label). Material connection = payment,
  free product, commission/affiliate, employment, or family ties. Asked to skip, bury, or euphemize the
  disclosure ("#sp buried in 30 hashtags", "just don't mention it") → **decline and correct** — it's an
  FTC/ASA violation and the brand is liable too, not just the creator.
- **Rights:** a **tag is not a license.** Reposting UGC needs consent; **paid use (ads, whitelisting,
  packaging, email) needs broader, explicit, written rights** including likeness. Paying for a post buys
  the post, not reuse — usage scope/duration/channels/exclusivity are agreed **before** content is made.
- **Never** buy followers or engagement, work with creators who do (vet first), or fabricate reach/results.
  **Metrics come from tracked links, codes + native analytics only.** **Verify-quarterly.**

## Distinct from its siblings
- **ugc-and-influencer (this)** — **brand→creator**: the brand pays (or gifts) for content/reach;
  licensed + disclosed; plus the **customer-UGC pipeline** (ask → consent → credit → reshare).
- **collabs-and-cross-promotion** — **creator-to-creator MUTUAL** partnership: unpaid, both contribute +
  both promote. The moment money or free product changes hands, it's a sponsorship → it routes **here**.
- **creator-monetization** — the **same deal seen from the creator's side**: their rates, media kit, and
  income portfolio. This skill is the brand buying; that one is the creator selling.
- **social-proof-and-testimonials** — turns the **resulting** UGC/reviews into objection-matched proof
  content. This skill sources/manages the creators and secures the rights; that one frames the output.
- **interactive-content** — the challenge/Add-Yours/poll mechanics; the **UGC rights + reposting** for a
  challenge route here.
- **campaign-and-launch-planning** — the **campaign wrapper**; it orchestrates creator/press moments and
  calls this skill for the creator piece.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** `brand-profile` (niche/values/claims), `goals-and-kpis` (what the campaign must move);
  `audience-research` (who your buyers already follow + trust).
- **Feeds:** `social-proof-and-testimonials` (UGC → proof content), `storytelling-and-narrative` (customer
  stories as true material).
- **Pairs with:** `caption-writer`/`hook-writer` (the brand's reshare posts), `community-management`
  (spots organic UGC + mentions worth licensing), `interactive-content` (challenges that generate UGC),
  `social-selling-and-dm` (shapes the human-run outreach), `linkedin-company-pages` (B2B creator programs —
  BrandWorks/Creator Marketplace), `email-and-newsletter` (UGC in email — with rights covering it).
- **Publishes via:** `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial` (the brand's own posts only) +
  `platform-specs-and-validation` (validate each post); `content-calendar` slots the UGC/reshare cadence.
- **Measures with:** `analytics-and-reporting` + per-creator tracked links/codes;
  `experimentation-and-ab-testing` (creative variants, creator tiers). **Contracts, payment, whitelisting/
  ads, follower audits + the creator's own post stay native/human.**
