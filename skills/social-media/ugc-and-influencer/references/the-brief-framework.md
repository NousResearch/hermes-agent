# The BRIEF framework — run a brand→creator campaign that keeps the trust it's renting

A creator campaign only converts when the **creator fit is real, the brief protects their voice, the rights
are inked before content exists, and every post is disclosed.** BRIEF structures that. The agent sources,
briefs, and drafts; **WoopSocial publishes the brand's own posts** (rights-cleared UGC reshares, campaign
posts); **outreach, contracts, payment, ads and the creator's post are native/human.**

## B — Base the pick on fit + real audience
- Write the **creator spec** from `brand-profile` + `goals-and-kpis`: niche + values fit ("would their
  audience plausibly buy this?"), platform, format strengths, past brand work quality.
- **Vet before you pay:** engagement rate vs tier benchmark, comment quality (20 real comments),
  follower-growth curve, audience geo/language match — the **fake-follower red flags** in the 2026-reality
  file. Never buy engagement; don't hire creators who did.
- **Pick the tier by goal:** conversion/trust → nano/micro (best engagement-per-dollar); time-boxed
  awareness/launch splash → mid/macro. A **UGC creator** (content for your channels, no posting) when you
  need assets, not reach.

## R — Run a brief that guides, not scripts
- **One page.** The goal + the ONE key message; the audience insight; **mandatories** (claims you CAN make,
  claims/words you can't, required disclosure, deadlines, deliverables + formats); hooks/angles that have
  worked; **explicit creative freedom** ("your voice, your format — that's why we hired you").
- **No word-for-word scripts** — a scripted creator post is an ad with worse production values, and
  audiences spot it. Approve concepts, not syllables; one revision round max in the deal.

## I — Ink rights + whitelisting up front
- **Before content is made**, agree in writing: **usage scope** (organic reshare / paid ads / web / email /
  packaging), **channels**, **duration** (e.g. 90 days / 12 months / perpetual — price scales), **edit
  rights**, **exclusivity** (category lockout + window), and **whitelisting** (IG partnership ads / TikTok
  Spark Ads code — creator authorizes natively).
- **Paying for the post buys the post, not reuse.** A tag is not a license. Paid use needs explicit
  **likeness** rights. The agent drafts the term sheet; a **human/lawyer reviews + signs** (not legal advice).
- **Gifted deals too** — even "just keep the product" seeding should state whether posting is expected and
  what the brand may reshare.

## E — Enforce disclosure, every post
- **Every paid AND gifted post**: clear, conspicuous, **upfront** disclosure — #ad at the start / native
  paid-partnership label (creator toggles it) / YouTube's paid-promotion flag + verbal mention. Not "#sp",
  not buried in hashtags, not behind "more".
- **The brand is liable too** — monitor creator posts for compliance; the brief makes disclosure a
  mandatory. Asked to skip or bury it → **decline and correct.** No exceptions for gifted, "small", or
  story-only posts.

## F — Feed the UGC pipeline + measure
- **Customer UGC engine:** post-purchase ask (email/insert), branded hashtag, challenges
  (`interactive-content`), reposts spotted by `community-management`. Flow: **ask → consent + rights →
  credit → reshare** via the brand's accounts (WoopSocial publishes; credit in the caption).
- Hand the best UGC to `social-proof-and-testimonials` (proof content) and
  `storytelling-and-narrative` (customer stories).
- **Measure per creator:** tracked link/UTM + unique code; engagement quality (saves/shares/comments);
  whitelisted-ad numbers from the native ad manager. Read from native analytics — **never fabricated**;
  EMV is soft, never reported as revenue. Double down on creators who convert; end deals that don't.

## The campaign brief a request should fill
```
PICK: creator spec (fit, platform, tier by goal); vetted (engagement, comments, growth curve, audience match)
BRIEF: one page - goal, ONE key message, mandatories (claims/don'ts + disclosure + deliverables), creative freedom
RIGHTS: usage scope/channels/duration/edits/exclusivity/whitelisting agreed in writing BEFORE creation; human signs
DISCLOSE: #ad / paid-partnership label, upfront, on EVERY paid AND gifted post; brand monitors; no exceptions
FEED+MEASURE: customer-UGC ask -> consent -> credit -> reshare (WoopSocial, brand's own posts);
              per-creator tracked links/codes; native analytics; never fabricated
```
