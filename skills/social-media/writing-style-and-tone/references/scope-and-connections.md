# Scope, distinctions & connections

## Honest scope (never violate)
- **The agent** drafts in the defined voice, runs the four edit passes, renders tone from the map per moment,
  and **flags where a real specific is needed** — placeholder slots ("[your example]") stay visibly unfilled
  until the **human** fills them with lived material (anecdotes, opinions, numbers only they have) and makes
  the final call.
- **The authenticity line (absolute):** the agent never invents the human's experiences, failures,
  credentials, or feelings; never manufactures vulnerability; never stages typos or fake imperfection as a
  deception strategy. "Sound human" is achieved by real substance, not counterfeit texture. Small true
  details beat grand invented ones.
- **Detector honesty:** no detector-score theater (detectors misflag real writers — non-native-English
  essays at a 61.3% average false-positive rate in the Stanford finding); the judge is the reader. **AI-disclosure** where
  platform or policy requires — the goal is writing that earns trust, not writing that hides provenance.
- **Tone ethics:** crisis and bad-news moments get plain, direct language — never cleverness, never spin;
  complaint replies stay accountable. Stats in drafts route through content-research-and-sourcing before
  publishing. Never fabricate metrics or reader-response claims.

## Distinct from its siblings (route correctly)
- **voice-builder** = *defines* the brand voice (the identity document); **this** = the applied writing craft
  that inhabits it per piece and per moment (they chain: voice-builder writes the constitution; this governs).
- **hook-writer / hooks-and-retention-deep-dive** = the first line's craft; **this** owns everything after it.
- **text-post-and-microblog / caption skills / email-and-newsletter** = format-specific conventions this
  craft applies inside.
- **copywriting/conversion skills** = persuasion architecture; **this** = the prose quality layer under it.
- **content-research-and-sourcing** = verifies the claims; **this** styles the verified substance.
- **contrarian-and-opinion** = the POV strategy; **this** renders POV in prose.
- **writing-with-ai workflows** (any skill using AI drafts) = this skill is their mandatory edit layer.

## Where this connects
- **Reads first:** voice-builder (the voice) + brand-profile (identity) + the piece's format skill.
- **Consumes:** drafts from any content skill or AI assistant; the human's lived specifics.
- **Feeds:** every written surface — text posts, captions, carousels, scripts (spoken-word register routes to
  scripting-and-storyboarding), newsletters, community replies (community-management).
- **Publishes via:** the finished piece → scheduling-and-queue → **WoopSocial.**
- **Measure with:** read-through/dwell, saves/shares, reply quality, and the absence of "did AI write this?"
  comments — native, via **analytics-and-reporting**; never fabricated.
