# The STYLE framework — style as choices, tone as control

Style isn't decoration; it's a sequence of choices a reader feels. This framework runs as edit passes on any
draft (human or AI-assisted). Most "sounds like ChatGPT" pain is a missing S (nothing only-you) and a missing
L (no rhythm); most brand-damage pain is a missing T (wrong tone, right voice).

## S — Sound like someone
The anti-slop core: the disease isn't em dashes — it's sentences anyone could have written. Every piece
carries at least one thing **only this writer could say**: a real number from their work, a named moment, a
stated opinion, a lived detail. Concrete beats abstract ("31 of the last 40 receipts arrived after 10pm"
beats "clients often send receipts late"). Subjectivity is a feature — human writing takes positions and
wanders productively where AI stays neutral and rigidly on-topic. The voice itself (vocabulary, stance, humor)
comes from `voice-builder`; this gate makes each piece *inhabit* it.

## T — Tune tone to the moment
**Voice is identity (constant); tone is register (flexes).** Build the tone map once: celebration = full
personality · education = warm, clear, unhurried · sales = confident, restrained · complaint response = human,
accountable, un-clever · crisis/bad news = plain, direct, zero playfulness. The voice's fingerprints
(vocabulary, rhythm) persist underneath every register — a playful brand announcing layoffs goes plain, not
corporate-anonymous. Platform register shifts the same way: length/formality adapt, identity doesn't (the
cover-the-handle test: regulars should recognize the writer anywhere).

## Y — Yank the dead weight
The subtraction pass: **fluff sentences** (professional-sounding, information-free) deleted whole ·
throat-clearing openers cut ("In this post I'll…") · hedges trimmed to the ones that carry real uncertainty ·
adjectives doing a verb's job replaced ("moved fast" → "shipped in nine days") · one idea per sentence ·
front-load the point. If a line can vanish without loss, it already had.

## L — Let the rhythm breathe
**Burstiness — sentence-length variation — is the human signature** the monotone draft lacks. Alternate
deliberately: a long sentence that builds and builds, then a short one. That lands. Punctuation is pacing, not
ornament; paragraphs on social run short; plain words beat impressive ones. **The universal test: read it
aloud** — where you stumble or run out of breath, the reader already left. (First-line hook craft routes to
`hook-writer`; this gate owns everything after it.)

## E — Edit against the tells, intelligently
The final pass runs the tell **cluster** — hallmark vocabulary, "It's not X, it's Y," forced rule-of-three,
rigid structure, misplaced social proof, relentless positivity — with judgment, not bans: no single marker
proves anything, detector-score theater optimizes for the wrong judge, and the Stanford finding (a 61.3%
average false-flag rate on non-native-English essays) shows what cargo-cult hunting costs. The real question is the substance test from S: *does
this say something only we could say?* If yes, punctuation panic is noise. Disclose AI use where platform or
policy requires; then the human signs off and **WoopSocial publishes.**
