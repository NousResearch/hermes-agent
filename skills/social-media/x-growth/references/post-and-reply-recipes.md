# Post types, replies + two worked examples

## Native post types (in rough 2026 order of favor)
| Type | When | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form single post (Premium) | a real insight/argument | now often out-distributes threads |
| Native video (<60s) | demo, hot take, BTS | heavily pushed; keep watch-time high |
| Contestable take / question | spark replies | end on something worth arguing |
| Image / data-viz | proof, contrast | ~2× text engagement |
| Article (long-form) | depth | now boosted (native, not external) |
| Thread | genuine narrative arc | use `thread-writer`; not the default |
| Poll | contested question | high engagement, weaker follow conversion |

**Link rule:** keep external links **out of the main post** → put the link in the **first reply**.

## The reply strategy (the afterburner)
- ~**60%** replies on **accounts larger than you** in your niche · ~30% peers · ~10% smaller.
- Reply **early** to fresh posts from big accounts (be in the first wave); add a genuinely useful or
  contrarian point — not "great post". Standout replies get surfaced to that post's engaged audience.
- **Quote-post** to add your angle (one sentence of original insight minimum). Never copy-paste.

## The first-hour routine
Post in your audience's online window → reply to your own first replies immediately → spend ~20–30 min
replying into 2–3 bigger conversations in your niche → check back at 60 min. Velocity in this window
decides out-of-network reach.

## Expertise-moment play
When news breaks in your niche, post a **fast, informed, opinionated take** with a clear POV and a
reply hook. These get picked up in For You, spike follows, and attract collaborations. (Pair
`trend-jacking` for the moment, `ai-search-optimization` for evergreen discoverability.)

## Worked example 1 — contestable long-form (blunt indie-founder voice)
```
POST (long-form, no link): "Most 'post daily' advice is wrong for X. I posted 3x/day for a month and
grew nothing. Then I cut to 1 post + 20 replies a day on bigger accounts in my niche — and tripled
reach. Replies are the algorithm. Posting is just the bait. Here's the exact split I use: ..."
REPLY 1 (link): "Full breakdown + the 60/30/10 split → [link]"
TIMING: 9am audience window; engage every reply for the first hour.
```

## Worked example 2 — expertise moment (warm studio voice)
```
TRIGGER: a tax-rule change trends.
POST (native, contestable): "The new threshold change everyone's panicking about? For most freelancers
it barely matters -- here's the one number that actually does, and the rule we give every client."
HOOK: ends inviting "ask me your situation 👇" (drives author-reply chains = ~150× a like)
FOLLOW: answer replies personally; quote the best question with the full answer. Link to the guide in a reply.
```

Both: read brand-profile + voice-builder first; native + contestable; link in reply; first-hour
engagement; replies into bigger niche accounts; verified facts. Threads → `thread-writer`; schedule
posts via `scheduling-and-queue → WoopSocial`; live replies are human.
