# The SPARK framework — X growth runs on conversation

X rewards content that **sparks conversation**, not broadcasting. The open-sourced weights are blunt
(a reply ~27× a like; an author-reply chain ~150×), and the first hour decides out-of-network reach.
SPARK is the 2026 engine.

## S — Spark conversation
- Post **native, contestable, reply-worthy** content: a genuine take, a contrarian-but-true claim, a
  question, a data point people will argue with. Bland broadcasts die.
- Favor **long-form single posts** and **native video/image** over bare text; **link in the first
  reply**, never the main post. 1–2 hashtags max.

## P — Premium & profile
- **Premium is a baseline** for reach in 2026 (significant multiplier; free ≈ near-zero median reach)
  — but it **amplifies quality, doesn't replace it.** It also unlocks 25k long-form, higher-ranked
  replies, edit, analytics. Decide it as a **channel-priority** call.
- Set up the profile to convert reach → follows (clear bio/positioning; ties to `profile-optimization`);
  build **TweepCred** with a healthy follow ratio and engagement with quality accounts.

## A — Amplify via replies (the afterburner)
- A standout **reply** on a bigger niche account surfaces in **For You** to people who engaged with it
  (often 5–20× your baseline). Weight replies to **larger accounts in your niche** (~60/30/10).
- The algorithm learns who you are by **who you engage with** — reply where your target cluster lives.
- **Quote-post** with original commentary (never copy-paste — spam filter).

## R — Race the first hour
- **Engagement velocity in the first 30–60 min** drives out-of-network reach. **Post when your
  audience is online**; reply to your own early replies immediately to extend the chain.
- **2–3 quality posts/day**, spaced 30–60 min — quality over volume.

## K — Keep it credible & compounding
- **Accuracy has distribution consequences** (Community Note penalty) — don't post shaky claims.
- **No engagement-bait, pods, purchased/coordinated amplification, or copy-paste virality** (detected
  + penalized). **Bookmarks** = lasting-value signal — make save-worthy posts.
- Ride **"expertise moment"** posts (fast informed take on breaking niche news → follower spikes;
  ties to `trend-jacking`). Monetization is real but concentrated — earn it as a by-product, don't chase it.

## The X-growth brief a request should fill
```
FIT: is X a priority channel for this brand (B2B/tech/finance/real-time)? Premium budgeted?
POST: native + contestable; format (long-form / video / image / Article); link in reply
TIMING: audience-online window; 2–3/day spaced; engage own replies in the first hour
REPLIES: 60/30/10 into bigger niche accounts; quote with original commentary
CREDIBLE: verified facts; no bait/pods; bookmark-worthy
```
