# X (Twitter) 2026 — verified

*Volatile. Re-verify quarterly. Figures vary across sources; the open-source weights are the anchor.*

## The engine: conversation, not broadcasting
X open-sourced its ranking (re-released by xAI Jan 2026; pledged 4-week updates). The weights are
blunt — **a reply is worth ~27× a like, and an author-reply chain ~150× a like.** Widely-cited
relative weights: Retweet ×20 · Reply ×13.5 · Profile click ×12 · Link click ×11 · Bookmark ×10 ·
Like ×1. **Likes barely count; conversation dominates.** Bookmarks signal lasting value.

## First-hour velocity + reach mechanics
- **Engagement velocity in the first 30–60 min** is the strongest signal for **out-of-network / For
  You** reach (where discovery happens). Post when your audience is online; engage your own replies fast.
- **TweepCred** (0–100, PageRank-style): below ~**65**, only ~3 of your posts are considered for
  distribution; above it, all are eligible. **Premium adds +4 to +16.** **SimClusters** = topic
  clusters; reach comes from engagement by users in the target's cluster.
- Dwell time + 70+ behavioral signals (hover, opening replies, returning to a post).

## Premium is now a baseline (state it honestly)
Premium gives a **significant reach multiplier** (sources range ~2.4× to ~4–8×, some say ~10×;
verify-quarterly) and **free accounts see near-zero median reach.** It also unlocks **25,000-char
long-form**, **higher-ranked replies**, **edit**, **analytics**, revenue share, Grok. **Caveat:** it
**amplifies** quality, it doesn't replace it — Premium + mediocre content still loses.

## Format hierarchy (2026 shifts)
- **Long-form single posts** (Premium, up to 25k chars) are now often favored **over multi-tweet
  threads** for distribution. Threads still build authority when there's a real arc.
- **Native video** is heavily pushed (~42% of media posts); **images ~2× text engagement**;
  **Articles are now boosted** (a reversal — X wants to be "where you discover everything").
- **External links are suppressed** — ~30–50% less reach in the main post (near-zero non-Premium).
  **Put the link in the first reply.** Polls = high engagement, weaker follower conversion.
- **Hashtags:** 1–2 (≈ +21% lift); **3+ penalized** (≈ −17%); zero is fine.

## Replies = the afterburner
A standout reply on a bigger account's post surfaces in **For You** to people who engaged with the
original (often **5–20× your baseline**). Use a split weighted to **larger niche accounts** (~60/30/10)
to enter high-traffic conversations and the right cluster. **Quote posts:** always add original
commentary; **copy-paste/duplicate triggers a spam filter** and kills reach.

## Cadence + anti-patterns
**2–3 quality posts/day** (3–5 under 5k followers incl. replies), **spaced 30–60 min**; volume with low
per-post engagement is penalized. **Penalized:** engagement-bait ("RT if…"), pods/purchased/coordinated
amplification, copy-paste virality, low-value "good morning". **Accuracy has a distribution
consequence** — an active **Community Note** correction reduces reach.

## Monetization + fit (honest)
Creator revenue-share is real but **X publishes no payout totals**; the pool keys on **verified/Premium-user
engagement, not followers** (eligibility now from ~500 followers), and earnings are **extremely
concentrated** — most monetized creators make a few dollars to a few hundred per quarter
(verify-quarterly). Don't promise income.
**Topic amplification zones:** AI/tech, politics/current events, finance/crypto, sports — "expertise
moment" posts (a fast, informed take on breaking niche news) are the highest-ROI action. **Honest fit:**
X has the **lowest engagement rate** of the majors; it's strong for **B2B, tech, finance, thought
leadership, real-time/news, customer service** — not a default for consumer brands targeting under-25s.
