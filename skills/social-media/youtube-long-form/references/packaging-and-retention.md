# Packaging, retention + two worked examples

## Packaging (the CTR driver) — do this before scripting
- **Title:** specific, outcome-oriented, curiosity with a clear payoff. Not a vague teaser.
  ("The freelance tax setup that saved me €4,000" > "My tax tips".)
- **Thumbnail:** one idea, high contrast, readable text at mobile size, accurate to the content.
  Remove clutter before adding anything. Generate concepts with ideogram / nano-banana, then
  **A/B test** in Studio.
- Rule: if you can't make the packaging compelling, the idea isn't ready. Reverse the usual order.

## The cold open (first 30 seconds)
Patterns that deliver the promise instead of throat-clearing:
- **Promise + proof:** "By the end you'll have a tax setup that runs itself — here's the whole thing."
- **Stakes:** "I almost owed €4,000 I didn't have. This is the system that fixed it."
- **Question + tease:** "Why do freelancers always get surprised by tax? One missing step —"
No logo animation, no "welcome back to the channel." Start delivering immediately.

## Retention structure (the AVD driver)
- Re-hook every 60–90s: open loops ("the third one is the one nobody does"), pattern interrupts,
  B-roll, location/tone changes.
- One topic; cut anything that doesn't serve it. Chapters help Search and let viewers commit.
- Find the retention cliffs in Studio and rewrite those exact moments.

## Diagnose with three numbers (read natively in Studio)
| Symptom | Number | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Few impressions→clicks | **CTR** low | Packaging: title/thumbnail; A/B test |
| Clicks but instant drop | **30s retention** low | Hook/promise mismatch in the open |
| Holds then sags | **AVD %** low | Pacing/length; cut padding, add re-hooks |
| Views but no growth | **Session** low | Weak end screen / no chain to next video |

## Worked example A — blunt indie-founder voice
```
PACKAGE  Title: "The 30% rule that saved me €4,000 at tax time"
         Thumbnail: a split jar (spend / tax), bold "30%", no clutter.
COLD OPEN (0–20s): "Almost owed four grand in tax I didn't have. My fault — I tracked income,
         not the set-aside. Here's the whole system, takes five minutes to set up."
BODY     5 segments, one per step, re-hook before each. ~9 minutes. No padding.
CLOSE    One CTA. NEXT: end screen → "My full freelance finance setup" (playlist start).
```

## Worked example B — warm studio voice
```
PACKAGE  Title: "The calm freelance tax setup (no spreadsheets)"
         Thumbnail: tidy desk, soft palette, "Tax, sorted" — readable at mobile size.
COLD OPEN (0–25s): "If tax season makes your stomach drop — by the end of this, it won't. We use
         one account and one rule, and the panic just… goes."
BODY     gentle walkthrough, em-dashes, one small step at a time, chapters. ~11 minutes.
CLOSE    One CTA. NEXT: series link → "Part 2: what to set aside for retirement."
```

Both: read brand-profile + voice-builder first; packaged before scripted; cold-open delivers the
promise; explicit NEXT for session contribution; description + chapters for Search; human films/
edits/designs the thumbnail. To cut Shorts from this long-form, see captions-and-clipping.
