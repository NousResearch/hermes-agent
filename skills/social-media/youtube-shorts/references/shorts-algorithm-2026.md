# YouTube Shorts algorithm 2026 — the verified signal stack

*Volatile facts. Re-verify quarterly against YouTube Creator guidance and Studio analytics.*

## The headline: Shorts are decoupled from long-form
YouTube **fully separated the Shorts and long-form recommendation systems in late 2025.** They
are **two growth engines on one channel** with different rules. Posting Shorts no longer
algorithmically boosts your long-form, and a Short that flops won't drag long-form down. Treating
them as one strategy is what quietly stalls channels. (Long-form is the sibling skill
**youtube-long-form**.)

## The deciding metric: swipe-through, not CTR
Long-form lives on impressions + thumbnail CTR. **Shorts have no thumbnail and no click.** The
algorithm watches **Viewed vs Swiped Away** above all: did the viewer stay, or swipe? A Short
**earns distribution by not being skipped.** Retention is the whole game from frame one.

## Explore-and-exploit + the brutal first hour
Each Short is tested on a **small interest-based seed audience** (not your subscriber count — small
creators can go viral). If they stay and don't swipe, distribution expands; if they swipe, it
stops. **The test window is ~30–60 minutes:** clear the threshold in the first hour or the Short
is largely dead on arrival. Hook and posting time matter more than ever. (Shorts can resurface
weeks later if they find a new audience.)

## Retention targets (read these in Studio)
- **Viewed vs Swiped Away:** aim **~75%+** viewed. Below 50% at 10–15s → the hook needs work.
- **Average percentage viewed:** **~70%+** is the internal "good" bar. **Over 100% = replays**,
  one of the strongest positive signals.
- **Swipe-away rate:** healthy **10–30%**; **>40% is a red flag.** High swipe-away but strong
  retention-on-stayers → a packaging/first-frame problem, not a content problem.

## Length (up to 3 minutes, but bimodal)
Shorts can run to **3 minutes** (since Oct 2024), but data is bimodal: **<30s** tends to higher
completion; **~50–60s** tends to higher total views. Design the idea first, then the **shortest
length that still feels complete.** A tight 20s watched twice beats a meandering 90s abandoned.

## Novelty is now mandatory
An **Anti-Repetitive Content AI** suppresses recycled formats/hooks and content too similar to
what's already trending or what you've posted. There's an **information-gain expectation**: wide
distribution needs a genuinely unique angle. **Volume without novelty is a negative** — which is
exactly why mass-produced templated AI Shorts run into trouble.

## Two under-used facts + decay
- **Shorts rank in search independently** (dedicated Shorts filter, early 2026) and appear in
  Google search. Use **keyword-rich titles/descriptions** — most creators ignore this (social-seo).
- **"The Flattening" (late 2025):** Shorts older than ~28–30 days get deprioritized. You can't
  coast on a back catalogue; consistent new production matters. Post ~3–7/week, not spam.

## Counting + monetization (post March 31, 2025)
A view now counts when a Short starts to play/replay (no minimum), inflating raw view counts
~30%. Use **engaged views** as the stable quality metric — it drives YPP eligibility and Shorts
revenue sharing. Captions matter: most watch **without sound**, captions lift completion, and
YouTube indexes caption text for search.
